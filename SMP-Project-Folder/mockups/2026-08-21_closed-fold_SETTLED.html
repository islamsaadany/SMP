The closed fold — four treatments

The open state is settled and identical in every row: accent words, no fill. Only the closed fold changes. Each row shows it twice — sitting on the Group page with Mobile behind the fold, and with the fold open beside the selected unit.

A · today — a bordered capsule, uppercase, 11px

Two boxed controls beside a row of plain tabs. The uppercase letterspaced type is a different species from everything around it, which is most of why it reads as bolted on.

B · the row's own type, no box at all

The fold becomes a tab that happens to open a list. Same size, same weight, same height, same hover as Group beside it — and the caret is the only thing saying it does something different. Quietest, and the most consistent.

C · the row's own type, caret in a quiet chip

B, but the caret is unmistakably a control rather than a punctuation mark. Costs one small circle each; buys an affordance B has to rely on convention for.

D · a soft ghost — quiet fill, no border, sentence case  CHOSEN

Keeps a container so the fold still reads as a group of things rather than a page, but drops the border and the shouting type. A middle answer if B feels too invisible.
