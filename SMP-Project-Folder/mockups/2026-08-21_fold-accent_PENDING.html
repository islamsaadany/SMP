The open fold — five treatments

The bar is drawn exactly as it ships: quiet labels, the selected page underlined in the accent, the closed fold a translucent white pill. Only the open fold differs between rows. Each row shows the same moment — Mobile is the page you are on, and you have just opened Units ▾ to go somewhere else.

A · today — a solid accent pill

The loudest thing on the bar is the menu you opened, not the page you are on. It also repeats the accent the underline is already spending two inches away.

B · the closed shape, kept — the accent goes to the caret alone

Open reads as "pressed" through the brighter wash and border it already has on hover. Nothing on the bar competes with the underline. Cheapest change; the weakest open/closed signal.

C · an accent edge instead of an accent fill

Same move the rail took: the ground stays quiet, the accent is a line. Unmistakably open, and it costs an outline rather than a slab.

D · accent words, no fill

The quietest. On a navigation control it reads as a link rather than a button — and the closed state has a border the open one loses, which is backwards.

E · the rail's answer, borrowed exactly — quiet ground, accent edge on the left

Literally the rule we just settled for the rail: a raised ground off the bar's own family, with the accent as a 3px edge. Consistent with the rail without matching the nav.
