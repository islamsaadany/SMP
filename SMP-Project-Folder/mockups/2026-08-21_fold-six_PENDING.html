The navigation fold — six treatments

Each shown twice: closed on the Group page, and open beside the selected unit. Everything else on the bar is exactly as it ships — the tabs, the selected underline, the separators. Only the two folds change.

1 as deployed today

11px uppercase letterspaced in a bordered capsule, beside 13.5px sentence-case tabs. A different typographic species, which is why it reads as bolted on — and the open state is a solid accent slab, louder than the page you are actually on.

2 soft ghost — what is on the branch now

Keeps a container, so the fold still reads as a group of things rather than a page, and drops the border and the uppercase. Open is accent words with no fill.

3 bare words

No container at all. The fold is a tab that happens to open a list — same size, weight, height and hover as Group beside it, with the caret the only thing saying it does something different. The quietest of the six.

4 one shared container

Both folds in a single box divided by a hairline, like a segmented control. Halves the number of boxes on the bar and says outright that these two are the same kind of thing. Open is the segment lit rather than the word coloured.

5 the word carries its count

No caret; the numeral does the work. Units 10 tells you what is behind the fold before you open it, which no other option here does. Open turns the word and its count to the accent.

6 a mark instead of a caret

Bare words, but a small stack glyph replaces the caret — two rules suggesting a list. Reads as “a list opens here” rather than relying on a triangle nobody looks at.
