One thing in there I want to flag before building it

Dropping “Finance” from the chip drops the half that decides access. You are right that it is redundant beside Head of Finance — but it is redundant only because the job title happens to match. Strategy custodian tells you nothing about whose plan they may change, and two people can hold that same role over different units and look identical in this table. The stylesheet has carried a note about this since the register was built: never a bare role name.

Row 4 above is the case — Nourhan holds Business unit owner and Strategy custodian, and the table no longer says over what. So, two ways, and I need one word from you:

(a) As drawn. Role name only, attachment on hover and in the +1. Cleanest, and the Roles & access page is where scope really lives anyway.
(b) Attachment kept, truncated. Strategy custodian · Mobile, clipped to the column with the full text on hover. Two more words per chip, and the table still answers “whose?” without a hover.

I lean (b) — the register's whole job is answering who is this person here, and the attachment is half that answer. But it is your call and (a) is what you asked for.

Two words and I build it

  1. Pillar title: B1, B2 or B3? I'd take B3 — it borrows the rail's own grey-plus-gold-edge rather than inventing a fourth banded thing, and the drawing puts the rail beside it so you can see the two agree.
  2. Role chips: (a) bare, or (b) with a truncated attachment? I lean (b), for Nourhan's row. Either is a one-line change.
  3. One I noticed rather than was asked: the live tenant's only person is literally named “Strategy Management Office” — an office, not a person. It is just data and you can rename it on this page.
