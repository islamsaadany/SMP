The open question: does capability delivery count toward the group score?

Today the group figure is the weighted average of ten unit headlines, and a BU head can check their own number against it. Folding capabilities in means giving them a weight, which reopens the weighting model, and makes the group figure something no unit can reconcile. My suggestion is a second reading shown beside it — Capabilities 54%, its own number — rather than inside it. Same principle as focus measures: a lens, not a second weighting.

One file per capability, with its own sheets: brief, deliverables, milestones.
Capabilities carry projects only. The measures they had were inherited from when a capability was modelled as a pillar. A capability has no targets of its own — its health is whether its projects land — so one table leaves the screen and progress has a single definition: milestones completed.
The template needs its own scope. The importer is scoped per unit, and a capability belongs to no unit. The file carries a capability rather than a unit, and its sheets are Brief, Deliverables and Milestones instead of Pillars, Measures and Tactics — but the same parser, the same validation and the same id rules underneath.
