Progress is the milestones, not a typed number. 4 of 5 completed is 80% — so the figure at the top of the project can never disagree with the table beneath it. A deliverable carries its own kind: a measure with a target, a percentage of delivery, or simply delivered or not.
Something the platform should catch. This project ends 30 Apr 2026, but its last milestone finishes 31 May 2026 — a month after the project is due. In your sample that is just a slip on a slide; here it is a validation the platform can make, the same way an import refuses a theme that does not exist.
Timeline by date or by quarter, per project. The first project runs on exact dates and the other two on quarters. Both derive the same things — what should be delivered by now, and whether a milestone is late — so one calculation serves both and the owner picks whichever their work actually has.
