Three things to confirm on the capability side before I build it.
  • Two subtabs, not three. Foundation and Projects. A capability has no SWOT — that belongs to a business unit. Say if you want an empty one for symmetry; I would not.
  • Is a Foundation subtab worth it for a capability at all? It holds a definition and, for six of the eight, a short objectives table. The alternative is no subtabs: Strategy simply is the projects, with the definition as a line at the top. Fewer clicks, but then the two halves of the platform stop matching again — which is the reason we are doing this.
  • The capability's third column is Weight, not 3-year. A capability's objectives carry the optional weighting; they have never had a three-year horizon. Same layout, different column.
