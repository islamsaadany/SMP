A head over two units

The cost, stated plainly. The navigation now looks different for different people: the SMO has folds, nobody else does. That is a real support cost — "click Business units first" is advice that makes no sense to nine out of ten users. The platform already accepts this, since a unit head has never seen the same row as the SMO, but folds make the difference structural rather than just shorter.

The fold alone did not fix it. With the labels written out, the row still wrapped at every width I tested \u2014 the two pills cost more than the eight capabilities they hide. Shortening them to Units and Functions is what buys the single line, and even then eight long capability names wrap on a 1366px laptop. Worth knowing before this is built: the fold needs short labels to work at all.
When the fold appears. Suggested rule: only when a person reaches more than one capability and more than one unit — which today means the SMO and the CEO, and nobody else. Worth confirming, because the rule decides how many people ever see it.
