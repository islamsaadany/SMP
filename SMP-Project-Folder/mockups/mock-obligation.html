Mockup — My reporting as a person's obligation. Nothing is built.
The change, in one line. Today My reporting is a tab underneath a business unit. A supporting function is not a business unit, so its owner has no tab to stand on — and someone who owns both a unit and a capability has to visit two places. Making the page belong to the person rather than the unit solves both, and moves it to the top of the navigation where an obligation belongs.
