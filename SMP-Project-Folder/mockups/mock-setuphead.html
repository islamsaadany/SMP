Two things I decided in the drawing, which you can overrule. The clear mark opens a menu of two rather than putting two icons in the row — clearing is rare and destructive, and a menu makes it deliberate. And per-row clearing keeps a small mark in the Status cell beside Retire, rather than moving to the header, because it acts on one row and the header acts on all of them.
One thing worth checking. The chips are read-only facts — who you are, how many rows, whether you are editing. If Editing is now said by the tick being lit, that chip can go, and the header gets shorter still.
