One Edit button, not three. Foundation, SWOT and Plan are all the plan, and today each carries its own edit bar. Under one tab they should share one — you enter edit mode for the strategy, not for a page of it. That also stops the odd case where someone edits the SWOT, moves to Foundation, and finds themselves reading again.
Two decisions this raises.
  • Which subtab opens first. Drawn as Foundation, in the order you gave. But Foundation and SWOT are read rarely and Plan is what people return to, so opening on Plan would serve the common case. The cost is that the tab you land on is not the first tab, which is mildly odd the first time and invisible after that. I would take Plan; the order stays Foundation, SWOT, Plan because it reads as a narrative — who we are, where we stand, what we will do.
  • Key objectives appear twice. They are authored on Foundation and they are also the top of the Plan. Drawn here on Foundation only, with Plan starting at the items. The alternative is to move them to Plan and leave Foundation as pure identity — cleaner in one way, but then the thing a unit is judged on is not on the page that says what the unit is.
What Strategy costs as a container. Foundation and SWOT go from one click to two. They are read rarely, so this is probably the right trade — but whoever maintains them will feel it every time, and they are also the pages a new unit head reads first.
