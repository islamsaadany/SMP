Screen DThe three states of that third row

The tab appearing is easy. What happens after submission is the part that needs deciding, and I have drawn what I think is right rather than what you described — the difference is in the middle column and it matters.

Cycle open, not submitted
Performance   Report 14/27
The tab is there and gold. The bar on Performance says what is owed and offers the way in. This is the state a unit head is in for about two weeks a quarter.
Submitted
Performance   Submitted
It does not disappear — it goes quiet. You said it should disappear after submission. I would keep it, unstyled and read-only, saying Submitted 12 Jul. Someone who has just sent figures to the SMO will want to check what they sent, and if the tab is gone there is nowhere to look.
Cycle closed
Performance
Now it is gone. The figures are a record, there is nothing to enter and nothing pending. If the SMO reopens the cycle it comes back, gold again.
The one disagreement, stated plainly. Disappearing on submission and disappearing on close are two different rules that look like one. On submission the work is done but the cycle is still live and the SMO may reopen it — the person still has a stake in what they sent. On close there is genuinely nothing there. Hiding it at submission saves one row of chrome and costs someone the answer to what did I actually submit. Overrule me if you want it gone at submission; it is one line either way.
Three things this restructure costs.
  • A third row of navigation. Two tabs, then subtabs, then in Strategy nothing further — but on Performance the Report tab is a third level. That is one more row of chrome than the platform has anywhere today, and on a phone it is a lot of header before any content.
  • Foundation and SWOT get further away. They are one click today and two after this. They are read rarely, so that is probably right, but it is a real loss for whoever edits them.
  • The capability side stops matching. A capability would have Performance / Projects / Reporting while a unit has Performance / Strategy. Either capabilities take the same shape — Performance with Report folded in, and Projects renamed Strategy — or the two halves diverge again, which is what A13 exists to prevent. My recommendation is to do both together or neither.
Data honesty. Mobile's items, owners, themes, measures, targets and actuals are real — the platform's own data. The cycle dates, the entry tallies and the due date are invented for the mockup.
