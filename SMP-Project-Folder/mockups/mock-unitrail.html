Where the rail is worth more on a unit than on a capability. Mobile has four items carrying 23 measures and 22 tactics between them. Portfolio & Value Chain Expansion alone has 8 measures and 7 tactics. In the accordion the fourth item sits below roughly two screens of tables — the rail puts every item one click away regardless. The pattern was designed for three projects and pays for itself at four items with fifteen rows each.
Two things the unit needs that a capability did not.
  • The rail has to group by kind. A unit's items are Capabilities and Directions and the distinction matters — it is drawn here as two small headings inside the rail. A capability's projects have no such split.
  • An item with no measures reading needs a dash, not a zero. Item 01 has that today in the real data. The rail says so in words underneath rather than showing 0%, which would read as failure.
What applying it to units costs. Foundation and Analysis have no list, so a unit gets the rail on three of its five tabs and not on the other two — the page shape changes as you move across the tabs, which the capability does not suffer since all three of its tabs have the list. Worth it, I think, but you will notice it.
If the rail goes on both
One pattern, learned once.
  • A unit and a capability work identically — the thing you are reading is on the right, the list of siblings is on the left.
  • The pillar accordion disappears, so there is one fewer component to maintain.
  • Costs: it touches the unit pages, which are the most-used and most-tested screens in the platform. Bigger change, more to re-test.
If it stays capability-only
Smaller change now.
  • The unit pages are untouched, so nothing already working is put at risk.
  • Costs: two halves of the platform choose things differently, which is exactly what A13 exists to prevent. And the accordion's real problem — the fourth item two screens down — stays unsolved on the pages where it is worst.
Data honesty. Everything on this page is real — Mobile's four items, their names, owners, themes, measures, targets, actuals, notes, tactics and quarter flags are the platform's own data. The three card figures are the unit's stored performance numbers. Nothing here is invented.
