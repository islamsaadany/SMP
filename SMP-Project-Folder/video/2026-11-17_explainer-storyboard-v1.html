SMP explainer — storyboard and script, v1

A three-minute motion video for clients: the plan, the reporting, and the result

What a client sees, from their own seat: the shape of the company, a plan and how it reads, a reporting cycle, the performance it produces, the group's dashboards, and the review deck. The office's own pages (setup, the register, access, gap-filling, the chat, history, import) are deliberately left out. Each scene below carries the real screen it is drawn from, the narration, what appears on screen, and how it moves.

Length about 3:05 Format 16:9 · 1920×1080 · MP4 Scenes 10 Narration about 470 words, at a calm pace Built with Remotion, rendered in this session

Five decisions before anything is built

  1. Whose company is on screen. Every screen below shows Raya Trade: the name, the Raya logo on the deck, Mobile's real plan and real figures. A video shown to other clients should not. Options: A — rebuild the screens as clean motion-graphic recreations of the platform's own layouts, with an invented group and invented figuresRecommended; B — animate the real screenshots as they are, with Raya's consent; C — real screenshots with the tenant's name and mark swapped before capture (the plan content would still be Mobile's).
  2. Voice. A recorded voice (yours or a colleague's), an AI voice (Descript is connected to this session and can read the script), or no voice with on-screen captions only.
  3. Language. English only, Arabic only, or English with Arabic subtitles.
  4. Length. The full cut below (about 3 minutes), or also a 90-second cut that drops the function's plan and the deck.
  5. Where it will be shown. Shared as a file and shown in meetings (16:9 is enough), or also posted on LinkedIn (then a square cut is worth adding).

How it gets made

1 · Sign-off on this page

  • The five decisions above, and any change to a scene's words or order.
  • Comments on this page reach me directly.

2 · Build in this session

  • Remotion (React), one composition per scene, in the platform's own type and colours.
  • Option A rebuilds each screen as motion graphics with invented data; option B animates the screenshots with pan, zoom and callouts.
  • A first full cut, silent with captions, for you to review as a video.

3 · Voice and finish

  • Your recorded voice is dropped in and the scenes are retimed to it, or Descript reads the script with an AI voice.
  • Final render: 1920×1080 MP4, plus a square cut if wanted.
  • The project source is committed to the repository so the video can be re-rendered when the product changes.

Left out on purpose, because it is the office's work rather than what a client's people see: the sign-in door, the welcome screen, Setup (the register, roles and access, scoring bands, the cycle), gap-filling, the office chat and notifications, History, import and export, and the master presentation. Any of these can be a second, shorter video for the office.
