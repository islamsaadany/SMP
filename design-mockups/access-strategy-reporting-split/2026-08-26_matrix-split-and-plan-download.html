Strategy locked, reporting granted

“the strategy should be locked from the non SMO but the reporting should be editable by who we grant the access so they can submit … we need this split in the roles and access table” — and a plan download in slides for the custodian and the BU owner.

Everything on this page is the real platform driven in a browser — the proposal is injected into the live table and pane, so both sides wear the product’s own pixels. Two changes are proposed; each has its screenshots and its costs, then three open questions at the end.

1 · The Roles & access table, today

One column answers for everything in a person’s own unit — strategy pages and reporting. The custodian’s gold pen under “Own business unit” looks like it opens the plan, when since §94 a hidden rule keeps strategy authoring to the office and the grant really only opens reporting. The table cannot say that, and you cannot grant one without the other.

2 · Proposed — each Own column splits in two

How it behaves
  • Strategy covers Foundation, Analysis & SWOT and Plan — and on a function, a capability’s definition and projects. Reporting covers entering figures, saving drafts and submitting.
  • Defaults keep today’s behaviour exactly. Strategy edit starts with the office only; owner and custodian start at Strategy view + Reporting edit; a function head the same on their function; a Contributor stays view (and the granted-edit, own-lines-only rule is untouched).
  • The SMO can open Strategy edit deliberately — your call from our questions. The pen is offered on the Strategy half for every role, so §94’s lock becomes the shipped default rather than an unbreakable floor. Granting it is a visible act on this table, logged like any other access change.
  • Nobody’s rights move on upgrade. A tenant’s stored grant on the old single column is read as the Reporting half — that is what it actually governed — and the Strategy half starts from the defaults above.
  • The server refuses what the screen refuses. Both halves are enforced in the shared rules and the save authoriser, not only on the page — a person without the strategy grant cannot write a plan through the API either.
  • Reordering stays as you set it in §101 — the custodian’s and owner’s up-down arrows are a separate rule and do not move.

Cost: the table goes from 8 columns to 10. It still fits the full-width window (§94.13); the two-line header above is the real render at 1700px.

3 · Download the plan as slides

A download button beside the pen on the Strategy pane, for the office, the BU owner and the custodian. It produces a real PowerPoint file (.pptx) — editable slides they can mark up outside and bring back to you — built by the platform’s own zip writer, so it works offline like everything else.

On the slides: the unit’s Foundation, the SWOT, the key objectives, and each pillar’s measures and tactics (a function’s projects on that side). No reported figures — this is the plan for refinement, not the review deck.

For a custodian or BU owner — who have no pen — the same button sits in the same corner, next to the arrange arrows when those are shown. The download asks only for strategy view, which every role in the unit already has.
