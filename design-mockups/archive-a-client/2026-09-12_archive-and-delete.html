Why a band and not a filter. A client that simply vanished could never come back — the only route would be through a card that is no longer drawn. The band is also where Delete becomes reachable, so being archived is a real step and not a hidden state.

The card has no door. Their link is already closed; a card that still opened would promise something that does not work. Bring back takes the corner that Settings holds on a live card, because on an archived client it is the one thing you came to the card to do.

Archiving takes ElAbd Foods off the clients page and closes their link — anybody who goes to it is turned away exactly as if this client had never existed. Nothing is lost, and you can bring them back.

Why here. It is the same argument the reporting cycle's pen settled: a door is only a guard if everything dangerous is behind it. Settings is already that door, and the block sits under a rule with a heading over it, so nobody arrives at it by tabbing down the form.

The worked example never draws this block, and neither does anybody who is not the platform admin — not greyed out, not drawn at all.

Deleting

Deleting removes everything this client has — their plan, their figures, their people and their conversations. It cannot be undone and there is nothing to restore from.

Delete is not on a live client anywhere. Archiving first is what makes the second press deliberate — and an archived client's door is already closed, so nobody is working in it while it is deleted.

What this costs, and what it decides

  • Most of it already exists. The platform already knows how to treat a client as archived — the cards hide it and the door turns people away — and the delete step is already written and proved. Neither has ever had a button. This is the button.
  • Delete cannot be undone and there is no backup. That is the real cost and it does not go away: two doors, a typed name and a count of what goes are what stand in for one.
  • The word “Archive” is used twice in the product now — archived plans inside a client, and an archived client out here. They never meet on one screen, and the second is your word.
  • The demo client can be neither archived nor deleted. The block is not drawn for it.
  • Both are the platform admin's alone. Adding a client can be given to a consultant; removing one cannot.
  • One row of height on the clients page when anything is archived, and nothing at all when nothing is.
