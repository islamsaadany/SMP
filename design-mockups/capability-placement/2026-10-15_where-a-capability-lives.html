7The five statements, confirmed

Checked with you on 15 October: all five correct.

  • 1A pillar lives on a unit or on the company. Nowhere else.
  • 2A pillar is external (a direction) or internal (a capability). Both plan in measures and tactics; either may plan in projects instead.
  • 3A supporting function's plan is a separate thing. It may measure its part while a capability is being worked on, but it never contains one.
  • 4Who owns a capability — a person, a function head, a unit head — says nothing about where it lives.
  • 5Joined or split is a setting on the box, not a different kind of client.
