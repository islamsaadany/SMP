One thing needs your word

A client that has capabilities is currently refused a second pass through set-up. The flow blocks a re-shape once a client holds authored work, and it counts a capability as authored work — which was right while nothing out here could make one. The moment this step can make them, adding one locks the consultant out of their own set-up.

The fix is to count a capability that holds something — a project or an objective — rather than a capability at all. An empty one the wizard just made stops being a reason to refuse; one with real work in it still is.

Say the word and I will build both, with the check first.
