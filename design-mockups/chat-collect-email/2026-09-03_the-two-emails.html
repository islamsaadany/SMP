One thing this changes Today this email is built in your browser as you press Send. Collecting for ten minutes means the platform builds it later instead — same builder, same branding, no longer depending on the tab that wrote it still being open.
The subject carries the count “3 conversations waiting” when there are several, “A question is waiting: Hend Farouk” when there is one — so the inbox list alone tells you the size of it.
Where the button points The platform's address is taken from the platform's own traffic, so the link is a real absolute address rather than something a mail client tries to open as a file.
Screenshots Named in words rather than attached — an email carrying pictures is an email that gets held up on the way in.
