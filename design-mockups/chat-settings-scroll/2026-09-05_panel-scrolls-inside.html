The settings panel scrolls inside itself

The Settings dropdown on the Platform Inbox grows taller than the window and has no scroll of its own, so what scrolls instead is the page behind it. Two ways to close that, drawn in the running platform.

Reported by Islam, 5 September 2026 Measured at 1400×760, Platform Inbox Awaiting sign-off before any source is touched

What was reported

There is no scrolling inside the settings panel while there is a useless scrolling in the main page. Islam, on the shipped build

Both halves are one fact

The panel computes overflow-y: visible and max-height: none, so there is nothing inside it to scroll — measured, 0px. It is 725px tall once both diagnostics have been run, hanging off the bottom of an ordinary laptop window; and because it is positioned inside the page, that overrun becomes the page’s own scroll. The Inbox page needs no scroll at any of these heights: the page’s scroll is the panel’s overrun, to the pixel.

What it looks like today

Two ways to close it

Both cap the panel at 64vh and give it its own scroll. That number is not a guess: it is the cap the platform already puts on its other tall dropdown, so this adds no second answer to how tall may a dropdown be. Both bring the page’s scroll back to 0px and the panel to 486px. They differ only once you are scrolling inside it — which is why both are drawn part-way down.

A

Cap it, and nothing else

One rule. The whole panel scrolls, heading included.

What it costs Nothing on the page says there is more below the fold — and a scrollbar cannot say it either, because on a Mac it is an overlay that fades away. The panel also loses its own name as soon as you move.
B

Cap it, and say it continues

The same cap, plus the heading staying put and a soft foot.

Recommended The fade and the pinned heading are the platform’s own, lifted from the Setup rail, which was capped for exactly this reason. It costs 22px of blank under the last row at the end of the scroll — paid on purpose, because trying not to pay it is what once left the last few rows unreachable.

B in the dark palette

The fade is drawn from the panel’s own surface token rather than a colour of its own, so it follows the palette without a second value to keep in step. Measured in both.

What it is in the sources

Four lines of CSS, in chat.css, beside the rule that already sets the panel’s width. No change to any builder, no new element, and nothing stored moves.

/* The panel takes the window and scrolls inside it. */
.hmenu-panel.chset { max-height:64vh; overflow-y:auto;
                     overscroll-behavior:contain; padding:0;
                     scrollbar-width:thin;
                     scrollbar-color:var(--line) transparent; }
.chset .chset-h    { position:sticky; top:0; z-index:2; }
.hmenu-panel.chset::after { /* the fade, and it gets out of its own way */
  content:""; display:block; position:sticky; bottom:0; height:22px;
  background:linear-gradient(to top, var(--surface), rgba(0,0,0,0)); }

What was checked, and what it does not change

  • The last row stays reachable, and so does the note behind its ⓘ mark — measured with the panel scrolled to its foot, because that is the fault this shape has caused once before elsewhere.
  • Every note still opens inside the panel sideways, which is what the existing check asserts; capping the height leaves that measurement untouched.
  • Nothing else on the page moves. The panel is 392px wide before and after, every row keeps its position, and the Inbox behind it is unchanged.
  • Scrolling past the panel’s end no longer drags the page, which is the second half of what was reported.
  • No setting, no stored value and no server rule is touched.
