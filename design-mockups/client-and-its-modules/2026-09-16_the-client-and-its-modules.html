What is drawn, and what is not

  • The client’s mark, name and colours — so you know which client you are in when there is nothing else on the screen.
  • Save & close, which returns to the console.
  • No house mark, no Group dropdown, no Units | Functions, no row of units, no tabs, no module switcher.
  • Terminology is the same page Strategy opens — one page, two doors, never two copies that sync.
  • Outgoing email is the address, the name and the footer. The inbox and sending a message stay with the module: they are tools, not set-up.
  • History is the whole record, with tabs — The client · Strategy · Insights. Each module’s own settings shows only its own slice.

Nothing here changes

  • Settings on a card opens screen 2. Save & close comes back here.
  • The module rows are already doors — pressing one opens that module for that client. This is where we choose which module to see.
  • A client’s own people never reach this page. Their chooser is the module switcher in the module bar, already built, already drawn only where there is more than one module.

What this drawing is asking you to sign off

  1. The client’s settings wear the client’s bar and nothing else — mark, name, Save & close.
  2. The rail as grouped above, including the three new entries: Terminology, Outgoing email, History. Outgoing email is a proposed name.
  3. Back is Save & close, and it lands on the console.
  4. Nobody’s access moves on the day this ships — it is a re-housing, never a re-granting, and it will be asserted rather than promised.
