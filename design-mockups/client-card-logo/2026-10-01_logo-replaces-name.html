What changes
  • A client with a logo shows the logo only. The name line goes, because the logo already says it.
  • The logo grows to fill the space the small mark and the name used together (about 49px tall, up to 180px wide), left-aligned, so every card in a row keeps the same height.
  • A client with no logo is unchanged: initials square, then the name.
  • The name stays on the card's hover and as the picture's text for screen readers, and the console search still finds the client by name.

The logos here are drawn stand-ins; the real cards use each client's uploaded PNG on the same white plate.
