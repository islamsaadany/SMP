A circular mark on a client card

“on addiing a circular logo the view is not good. plus I’m not sure of the placement of the client name in case we have the logo or at least should we remove it or change it’s size and loction to make the card neat”

Two questions, and the first one has a single cause. Every card below is the console’s own card with its CSS lifted line for line; each option adds only the declarations printed under it. The marks are stand-ins drawn for the two shapes that matter — a roundel and a wordmark. Every figure was measured in the real console at 1440 × 900.

Today — and it is one declaration

The mark sits in a column flex, so an <img> with width:auto stretches to the card — capped by max-width:120px — and object-fit:contain then centres the picture inside that stretched box. A wide wordmark fills it and looks right. A square or circular one is drawn 30 px wide in the middle of a 134 px box, so it floats about 50 px in from the edge while the name, the industry and the tags all start at the left. The white plate under it is invisible on a white card, so nothing on screen explains why.

The mark — three shapes

Whichever is chosen keeps one property §368 already paid for: the mark band is the same height whether there is a logo or initials, or a client with a logo makes the whole grid row taller for a reason nobody can read.

A · Stop it stretching

Smallest

One line. The roundel sits at the left the way the initials already do, and nothing else moves — but it stays 30 px, which is small for a mark somebody chose.

.ccard img.cmark{ align-self:flex-start }

B · One mark band, 40 px, at the left

Recommended

Every mark occupies the same 40 px band and starts at the same 15 px: a roundel fills it, a wordmark runs up to 126 px across it, and initials become a 40 px plate that reads as a mark rather than a chip. The light plate stays — a PNG cannot be recoloured, so a dark card needs a ground under a dark logo (§52) — and the box is border-box, so the plate sits inside the 40 rather than adding to it.

.ccard .cmark{ width:40px; height:40px; border-radius:9px; font-size:13px; line-height:40px }
.ccard img.cmark{ align-self:flex-start; width:auto; height:40px; max-width:126px;
  padding:3px 6px; box-sizing:border-box; border-radius:7px }

C · Mark and name on one line

Killed by drawing it

Tighter by 18 px, and it does not survive real names. A wordmark is 92 px wide, so the name starts 118 px in on that card and 66 px in on the next — nothing lines up down a row. A long name wraps into a narrow column beside the mark (four lines here), and because the mark centres against a name block of changing height, the mark itself slides up and down from card to card.

The name, when there is a logo

Drawn on top of B. The card without a logo is in every row on purpose: whatever the name does beside a mark, it still has to read beside a card that has none.

Keep it exactly as it is

Recommended

A logo is not a name at 40 px, some marks are wordless symbols, and the console’s search box matches the name — so a card showing only a mark cannot be found by typing. Nothing changes here but the mark above it.

What I would build

B, with the name kept. Two declarations in platform.html and the same two in the generated copy the new stack serves — screen only: nothing stored moves, nothing is migrated, and no rule about who may see what changes. Every card grows the same 4 px, so no row goes ragged. Say the word and I will build it; name a different option and I will build that one instead.
