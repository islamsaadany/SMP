What you see: two screens for one errand. Getting started is on the console's blue chrome; every other entry (marked ↗) leaves the console and opens the client platform on its navy chrome, with the same rail drawn a second time there.
What moves: nothing. The flow stays where it is; the console page gains a rail of links.
Cost: the rail exists twice — once as links in the console, once as pages in the client — and the two are kept in step by hand. Going back and forth means changing chrome each time, and the console's Getting started has to know the client's pages by address.
When B is the right answer: if you want consultants to shape a client without ever entering the client platform. Today that boundary is not one anybody needs — the office is us.

The same in A and B

The welcome page at the client's address goes. Strategy keeps its own welcome, offered once a session and reachable from the house mark. A client's own people sign in and land in their module.
The knowledge base stays on Strategy's rail, where the questions and the interaction with the client are managed. Email stays on Strategy's Send an email page.
Getting started is the set-up flow's own seven steps, with the mark and the brand colours in the first; done is a press of Done with set-up; then it sits at the bottom as Client set-up, the shape steps read-only once a plan exists, the mark and colours still editable.
The module's own Setup rail keeps one row under its head, Client settings ›, so a consultant already inside reaches the register without the console.
