The three buttons

  • Save writes the change and takes you back to the clients list — the common case, and what you expected it to do.
  • Save and open writes it and opens the client's own platform.
  • Back to clients leaves without writing, and asks first if anything is unsaved.
  • The team on the right still saves the moment you press it — adding somebody is an act, not a draft — so Save covers the boxes on the left and says so by sitting beside them.

What this is asking you to sign off

  • Set-up happens out here, before anybody from the client signs in. Press Add a client and you walk the whole thing on Forefront's own page. Their people then open a platform that is already shaped.
  • The client's details are step 1, not a screen of their own. Name, industry, notes and the mark were being asked twice — once to create, once on the card. Asked once, here, and the client is created the moment the name is answered so the address exists and you can leave and come back.
  • There is no default. A client is born as whatever you answered — and blank if you answer nothing. No inherited units, no inherited functions, nothing to rename.
  • The set-up flow inside the client goes away. One flow in one place. Adding a unit later is still the ordinary Business units page.
  • It ends on two doors — open the platform, or back to the clients list — and every line of the summary is a way back to the step that wrote it.
  • Dull means drawn and not built yet. Capabilities as their own entry, and the objectives-and-actions plan type: both inert, both saying Later, so nothing offers a choice that would open an empty page. That stays your separate discussion.
