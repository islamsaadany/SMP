Clients: Active, Ended, Demo, Mine, and a list view

One new row above the clients. Active · Ended · Demo chooses which clients you see, and opens on Active, so past and archived clients are off the normal view. Mine narrows it to the clients you have a seat on. The two icons on the right switch between cards and a list, and the page remembers your choice on that device.

What changes

  • The page opens on Active clients. The worked example moves to Demo, and archived clients move to Ended.
  • Mine shows only the clients you have a seat on. It works with each of the three choices.
  • Cards or list, your choice, remembered on that device.

What it costs

  • Archived clients are one press away instead of at the foot of the page.
  • A past client shows "Past client" where an archived one shows its archive date, and has no Bring back: it never had a platform to return to.
  • The counts beside each choice follow Mine, so they always match what pressing it shows.
