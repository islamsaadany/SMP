What changes, in plain words
  1. The list goes. Each capability becomes a card on the left, like a direction: its name, how many projects it holds, and a red count of what is still missing.
  2. Pressing a card shows that capability on the right: its name, Owner and Custodian, then its projects (or its pillars, if it is planned in pillars).
  3. Capabilities are coded C001, C002… and directions change to D001, D002…. There is no Open button: the capability's plan is on the right.
  4. Nothing is stored differently. It is the same capability and the same plan, drawn where the directions are drawn.
