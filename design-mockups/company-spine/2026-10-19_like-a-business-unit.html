One thing this collides with

The Master presentation runs each direction's own deck, then the company's last. That is why the company's review was made short in the first place. If the company's deck now holds every direction too, that flow shows all of it twice.

The company's deck is the whole story. The Master flow presents the company only — owners' decks stay for the owners.

Two lengths. El Abd's own review is full; the Master flow keeps the short one, so nothing repeats.

Leave the flow as it is and accept the repeat.

And one smaller choice

A Raya unit opens with three Foundation slides (Foundation, What we are aiming at, Key Objectives) and five SWOT slides (a divider, then one per quadrant).

El Abd gets one Foundation (aspiration and objectives together, running to two) and one Analysis slide with four quadrants on it.
