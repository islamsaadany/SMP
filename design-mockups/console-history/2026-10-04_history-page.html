Before there is anything on it

History

Nothing recorded yet. Every act in this console is recorded from the day this page goes in. Anything done before that is not here.

The eleven acts, as you approved them

  • Made a client
  • Archived a client, or brought one back
  • Deleted a client
  • Renamed one, or changed its mark
  • Set its shape — units, functions, divisions
  • Turned a module on or off
  • Put somebody on a client's team, or took them off
  • Gave or moved the super user seat
  • Added or removed a consultant
  • Handed out a password
  • Published or withdrew a report
One act is not on your list Changes to the Who sees what table — which consultant may open which client. It is a permission, so it belongs on a record of permissions. Say if you want it on, and it goes on; say nothing and it stays off.

The small decisions inside the page

Newest first, grouped by day. The day band is the one the My work table already uses, so the When column only has to carry the time.

Opening a client is not recorded — your call. Without it the page is nine real acts; with it, the same nine would sit under forty “so-and-so opened the client”.

The last 200, and the page says so. A record is scanned, not paged through; if 200 ever stops being enough, that is the moment to add more, not now.

The search filters the rows in place and matches the person, the client and the words of the act. Typing never reloads the page.

Forefront only. This is the console, which no client's own people can reach at all. It is not the client's own History page inside the platform, which is a different record and stays as it is.

What it takes to build

Every one of those acts already goes through one door in the code, so recording them is one line of writing at that door rather than a change to fourteen screens.

The table to hold them already exists — it was built with the platform and has never had a single row written into it. Nothing new is stored, nothing is migrated.
