Islam: “something strange the window is not active on opening I need to click anyway to start accepting clicks on the cards.”

The window is active. The page is not finished — and from outside, those two look identical. The repair below is built and measured; what is left is one round trip of the same silence, and what you see during it is yours to choose.

What was happening, measured

At 600ms a round trip, which is roughly production’s own, the console asked three questions in a row for one page load — and the last two were the same question: the heaviest one in the page, which opens a database connection per client to read its units, its plan and its cycle.
