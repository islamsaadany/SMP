Or — a tab of its own

The band under the cards

You see the last five without going anywhere, so something odd catches your eye on the page you already open. See all opens the rest.

What it costs: the Clients page gets about 180px longer.

A fifth tab

Nothing changes on any page you use today. The record is somewhere you go on purpose.

What it costs: nobody looks at it, because nothing ever points at it.

What gets a line

Only the things that change something. Eleven acts:

  • Made a client
  • Archived a client, or brought one back
  • Deleted a client
  • Renamed one, or changed its mark
  • Set its shape — units, functions, divisions
  • Turned a module on or off
  • Put somebody on a client's team, or took them off
  • Gave or moved the super user seat
  • Added or removed a consultant
  • Handed out a password
  • Published or withdrew a report

Two things it is not

It is not a copy of a client's own History page. That one records what happens inside a client — a target moved, a figure entered. This records what we do from outside, which today nothing sees.

It is not something a client can see. It is Forefront's own console and stays there.

What it takes to build

Everything the console does goes through one door, and the table for this already exists in the database and has never had a row written into it. So it is one line of writing at that door, plus the band you see above — not a change to fourteen screens.
