Where the console's Light / Dark switch sits

The console followed the device while Strategy's bar and the modules' shared bar each carry a switch; the three write one key in the browser, so unifying them is giving the console the same switch. Where it goes is the choice. Each option is the console's own column and band at real size; the control at the right shows all of them in the other theme.

  • One choice, one key. Whatever is pressed here is what Strategy and every module read a click later, and the device decides only until someone chooses.
  • The same mark. The sun says you are in light, the moon that you are in dark, the title says what the press will do — as Strategy's bar does today.
  • Icons-only column. The column can fold to a 64px rail (§601); A, B and D stay as a mark in the rail, C is untouched by it.
