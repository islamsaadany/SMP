The theme switch, round 2: on the band, three states, and a sign on hover

From round 1 you picked C, the ring at the right end of the console's band. This round adds what you asked for: the switch has three states — Light, Dark, Auto — on the console, on Strategy and on every module's bar alike, one key in the browser as now; and a sign on hover that says which state you are in and what the press will do. Pick the auto mark below; the control at the right shows everything in the other theme.

  • One cycle everywhere. Light → Dark → Auto → Light. Auto follows the device, now and as it changes during the day, and is where a browser that has never chosen starts.
  • The mark shows the state you are IN. In Auto the mark is the auto mark, not the sun or moon the device happens to give — the hover sign says which the device is giving.
  • One mark, one size. 26px ring on all three bars (Strategy's is 20px today), the same three glyphs, the same words.
  • A note from the record (§27): Auto was in the product for one version and was taken out as "a third state whose whole job is to be invisible". It comes back on your word, with the hover sign as the thing that makes it visible.

The auto mark: three candidates

each at the sizes it will be worn at, and large

Recommended: 1, the half circle — the sign most people already read as "automatic / follows the system" (it is what phones use), and it is the only one that is clearly neither the sun nor the moon at 14px. 2 reads well large and muddles at 14px. 3 is literal but says "screen", not "follows the device", and is a third shape where the other two are round.

03

On the console: C, with the pointer on it

the ring at the band's right end, the hover sign under it

The ring is on every tab's band (§647, one band everywhere), always at the right end, 26px, with the state's mark. The sign appears on hover and on keyboard focus, in the console's own label style, and reads: Theme: Auto · dark, from your device — click for Light. A screen reader gets the same words.

04

On Strategy and on every module's bar: the same three states, the same sign

Strategy's ring grows to 26px to match; the modules' bar keeps its place by Sign out

Strategy's switch (theme.js) and the modules' switch (lib/topbar.ts) each turn from a two-way press into the three-way cycle, reading and writing the same key; Auto is stored as the absence of a choice, which is what the gate and the console already understand. The hover sign is the same words in each bar's own label style.
