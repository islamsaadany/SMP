  • Four moments, two voices. 24 and 12 hours in the amber the platform uses when nothing has gone wrong yet; 6 and 3 hours in the alarm colour, and the last one drops Later, because at three hours there is no later.
  • Each person's reminders end when that person has downloaded. Not when somebody else has. Four is the most anybody who ignores it ever sees, and nought after the first download.
  • Dismiss silences that one moment, not the next. Pressing Later at 24 hours does not buy silence at 12.
  • Nobody outside the SMO team ever sees it. A unit head has nothing to download and cannot open the card.
The limit, said here rather than discovered on the night. Nothing wakes this platform up — there is no clock running on the server. A reminder appears the next time that person opens the platform. At three hours before, if it is 7am and nobody has a tab open, nobody is reminded. An email would have the same floor. If that matters, the honest answer is to take the files at 24 hours and treat the later ones as a safety net.
