A 2px ring in --focus, offset 2px: 4.99:1 light, 9.80:1 dark — and it is the same indicator Strategy has had since v3.11, so nothing new is invented and nothing has to be kept in step.

What changes, and what does not.
  • Deletions only — 11 rules in Tracker, 9 in Notes, 4 in Insights. Nothing is added except the one global rule the modules are missing.
  • Hover is untouched. Every one of those rules also sets a hover background; that half stays exactly as it is, which is what makes hover and focus finally look different from each other.
  • The two controls that already keep an outline — the done-tick and the status pill — keep theirs, so the three modules stop disagreeing with themselves.
  • The cost: a gold ring now appears on a navy bar and on pale rows. That is what a focus indicator is for, and it is what every Strategy page already looks like.

2. The boundary of a control

A field’s border is the only thing that says it is a field. Today every one of them is drawn in --line, which is the table-hairline colour — so on a page that is mostly fields, the page reads as flat text. Below is a unit’s Plan pane with the pen open. The cell padding, the hairlines, the zebra and the field shape are all the product’s own; the only thing that moves is which token a control takes its edge from.

Why a second token rather than darkening --line.
  • --line draws table hairlines, card edges and dividers. Darkening it would restyle every table in the product to fix the fields — a change nobody asked for (rule 1b).
  • The standard asks 3:1 of a control’s boundary, not of a separator. The two are different jobs, so they get two tokens — the same shape as --good and --good-tx (§38.4: a colour that works as a fill fails as type).
  • The cost: a field’s edge is visibly darker. On a plan page that is the point; on a quiet settings form it is a change of character, and that is what this drawing is for.
  • Where it has to be declared: four palette blocks in _shared.css, and again in each of the three modules, because each carries its own near-copy of the palette. That is finding 4 of the audit arriving as a bill.
Neither of these is guarded by a check. scripts/contrast-sweep.py reads color against background and nothing else — it has never measured a border or a focus ring, and it runs against the frozen Strategy file only. Whichever of these is taken, the sweep wants a second pass that measures a boundary, pointed at the three module pages as well — or the next colour added there is unmeasured again.
