Accessory revenue: why it keeps missing, and what to do before Q3

Situation

Mobile has delivered 96M of the 150M accessory revenue due by June (64%). It missed in Q1 too. The attach-rate tactic planned for Q2 has not started.

What we know

  • Platform 96M of 150M due by June; Q1 also below plan
  • You Stores stopped bundling accessories at the till in March
  • File June figure is 3.8M (chosen over the platform's 4.2M)
  • Assumed Price list unchanged since January

Options

A. Restart till bundling in all stores from AugustRecommended

Cheapest, fastest; recovers the March drop. Needs the store managers' sign-off.

B. Start the attach-rate tactic now, one quarter late

Addresses the cause in the plan; results not before Q4.

C. Lower the year target to 250M

Honest about the gap; needs the office's agreement and changes the plan.

Why A

The drop starts the month bundling stopped, and the other stores' figures did not move. It is the one cause that can be reversed within the quarter.

Next steps

  1. Retail custodian confirms bundling stopped in March (by 15 Jul)
  2. Store managers' sign-off on restarting (by 31 Jul)
  3. Review accessory revenue at the August check-in
