Competitive Discipline — the scoring guide (draft for review)

What 0, 1 and 2 mean for each discipline on each of the twenty factors. It would live in Copilot settings › Competitive Strategy, owned by Forefront and editable there; the Copilot scores against it and shows the matching line beside every score, with its reason and evidence. Drafted from Treacy and Wiersema's three disciplines — every line is for you to change.

The three levels0does not point to it · 1partly · 2strongly
Each discipline on its ownA factor can give 2 to two disciplines, or 0 to all three. The three scores on a row do not compete.
The choice is the totalsEach side totals out of 20 as a per-cent. A lead over 25 points is Clear, 10 to 25 Leaning, under 10 Unclear. The market decides; the internal side shows the gap to close.
How the two sides read. A market factor asks what the market rewards — how strongly this factor, in this market, favours each way of winning. An internal factor asks how the organisation is built today — how strongly it is set up for each way of winning. The Copilot reads both from the plan (the SWOT, the Foundation, the analyses), never from nothing, and names its evidence.
