How it behaves

  • The icons appear on any card that has text: Done, From the plan, or one left half-way. The card already open in the chat shows none, because it is being worked on there.
  • They work at every step, including during the consistency check, so a part can be fixed without typing a request at the bottom.
  • After a pen save the part stays Done with your words. If the consistency check had already run, it runs again, because the text it checked has changed.
  • Once the Foundation is saved as a deliverable the icons go, as everything in a saved flow is read-only today.
  • Found while drawing this: a part you leave half-answered says Empty · Not started although its answers are kept. It would say In progress · 1 of 5 answered instead.

Three things to confirm

  1. The two icons and the pen box as drawn.
  2. Once a card has the icons, the card itself stops being a button, so there is one way to each action. The other way keeps the whole card pressable as a second way into the chat.
  3. An empty card gets no icons and stays pressable to start it in the chat. The other way gives it the pen too, so a part can be written from scratch without the questions.

Nothing here is built. The fix that lets a part open at every step is on the branch, not merged. The earlier drawing (an editable box in the middle of the chat) is withdrawn.
