The other four areas, before and after

The same layout the Foundation and the SWOT now have: the conversation on the left, in the order it happened; what it builds in the pane beside it, with Save and Apply to plan at the pane's foot; the to-do at the right. Raya Shop's Online plan in every area. Switch area and Before / After.

Area View
1 · The chat talks, the pane holds the workTables, scores, options and measures leave the conversation for the pane. The chat keeps the questions, your answers and what you asked for, in order. Today the work is drawn inside the chat and scrolls away with it.
2 · The guided pass folds where it happenedEach step's questions fold to one row once agreed (Scoring · 20 factors · Agreed ▸), then it's plain chat under Later, as in the Foundation.
3 · Save and Apply at the pane's footSave keeps a version under Deliverables; Apply to plan asks first and archives what stood. Today Directions and Execution have one "Save to plan" that writes the plan at once. Advisory has Save only: a brief is advice, not plan content.
4 · Changes are proposalsAsk for a change and it comes back in the chat as a proposal; the pane changes when you press Save to the pane, or not at all with Keep as it was.
