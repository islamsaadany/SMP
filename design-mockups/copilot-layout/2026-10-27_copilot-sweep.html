The Copilot, swept: what is broken, what does not fit the plan, what breaks the flow

A full pass over every Copilot area on main at §653 — the code of each area's save and apply, the deliverable page, the archives, and a screen of each area at its key steps (the Foundation at the draft, the check and the save; the SWOT; Competitive Discipline, Directions, Execution and Advisory before and after their save; a free chat with its draft). Findings are grouped by kind, each with where it was seen and the fix, then a proposed order.

The path every area is meant to follow (§641, §645): chat → pane → Save a version under Deliverables → Apply to plan asks, archives what stood, writes → the plan · Advisory stops at Deliverables.
What held up. The Foundation and the SWOT follow the whole path. Every held button says why. The rail stays open with room. Start from the existing plan shows only where the plan holds the thing. Directions and Execution ask before they write and archive first. Nothing failed in the browser run beyond the eleven known zoom readings.

Proposed order three batches, each merged on its own

1 · Safety firstsmall, no design question

  1. A1 — no plain-text edit on a structured version
  2. A2 — an archive of the part applied, Directions and Execution (the period included)
  3. A3 — "on the plan" survives a reload
  4. A5, A6 — the two cosmetic lines
  5. B4 — the rail's chip in plain words

2 · The path, wholeDeliverables becomes the library it says it is

  1. B1 + A4 — Competitive Discipline: Save, then Apply that asks (and respects a switched-off section)
  2. B2 — every saved version's page: drawn as its type, On the plan, Apply, Edit as vN+1
  3. B3 — a free-chat draft becomes its type, or says it stays
  4. B5 — plan Directions scored or asked

3 · The four areas splitthe largest, the most felt

  1. C1 — Competitive Discipline (with the scoring guide once reviewed), then Directions, Execution, Advisory
  2. C2 — the SWOT: chat in time, say what to change
  3. C3, C4 — fall out of the split

Everything above is on main at §653 as of 27 Oct 2026. The screens named are the browser run's own, taken at each area's key steps.
