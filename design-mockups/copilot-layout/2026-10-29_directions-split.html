Save is held until a Direction is ticked ("Tick at least one Direction first"), then after a save until something changes ("Nothing has changed since it was saved"). Apply to plan is held until a version is saved, then asks first and archives what the plan holds (§645, §654). The foot's line runs v1 in progress → v1 saved · not on the plan yet → v1 saved · v1 on the plan. Where there is no room beside the chat, "View the tables" and "To-do 3/4" open them over it (§642, §670).

The chat, in the order it happened

1StartFrom the plan's Directions or fresh — asked in the chat as today, or taken from the start screen's card (§653). Plan rows carry their scores; an unscored one cannot go ahead until scored (§654 B5).
2The DirectionsSuggested onto the pane. "Suggest more" and "+ Add my own" stay as choices in the chat; the second box goes, and the chat's own box takes the ask. Every ask and its answer is kept on the stored row, so a reload reads the same.
3Go aheadPressed in the pane under the table; said in the chat as the person's line, and the Directions fold in place as "The Directions · 7 offered · 3 ticked · Agreed", opening again on a press (§644).
4The CapabilitiesThe same shape: suggested onto the pane under the Directions, kind and owner set there, asks through the chat's box.
5Save, then ApplyBoth at the pane's foot, as §645 has them. The chat says where the version is; the foot says whether it is on the plan.
Two things to confirm. (1) The Capabilities-only chat (started from the Capabilities area) takes the same split with one table. (2) "Go ahead with these N" stays a button under the Directions table in the pane, where the ticks are, rather than in the chat; the chat records the press as the person's line.
