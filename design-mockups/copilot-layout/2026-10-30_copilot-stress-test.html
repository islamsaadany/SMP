The Copilot, stress-tested as a person would use it

Every area walked end to end on the build of 30 October 2026, with the questions a person asks: can I go back, can I skip this, where do I go next, what if the plan changes under me, what if I reload. Read off the code's own guards and the run's screens, not from memory. Findings first, then what held.

1 · No way backA step taken cannot be untaken; the only way out is a new chat.

S1HighDirections: "Go ahead" cannot be undoneDirections chat, after Go ahead with the N ticked

Once Go ahead is pressed the Directions table settles: scores and ticks stop being pressable and the Capabilities step begins. A person who then sees a wrong tick, or wants one more Direction, has no way back. The stored row allows it (chose can be unset) but nothing on the page does.

Fix A Change the Directions link on the folded row and on the pane's card, which unsets the choice, keeps the Capabilities already suggested, and reopens the table. Small.

S2HighCompetitive Discipline: the discipline cannot be changed once chosenCompetitive Discipline chat, after "Yes, Best Total Cost"

The recommendation's choices are drawn only while no discipline is chosen. After choosing, the values come; a person who meant Best Total Solution can only start a new chat. The scores stay pressable until the table exists, so a changed score after the choice changes nothing.

Fix Choose a different discipline under the recommendation once chosen, which clears the values and the table and reopens the choice; and the scores settle at the choice, not at the table, so a change there always has an effect. Small.

S3MediumSWOT: a drafted SWOT cannot be undrafted after Use itSWOT chat, after a change is used

"Say what to change" proposes and the person uses or keeps it; good. But once used there is no "put back what it was": the previous draft is gone from the chat and the row. Draft it again starts from the material, not from the draft before the change.

Fix Keep the draft before each Use it on the row (one step of undo) and offer Put back the SWOT as it was on the note. Small.

S4LowFoundation: the road cannot be changed after the yearsFoundation chat, guided or template

A person who took Guided questions and finds the questions slow cannot switch to the template without a new chat; the parts answered so far would come along if they could.

Fix Switch to the template on the chat's head after the first part, keeping the parts agreed. Medium; the lowest priority of the four.

2 · Cannot skipThe flow insists on something the person does not need.

S5HighExecution: every Direction and Capability must be drafted before SaveExecution chat, Save held

Save is held until every item has at least one measure and one tactic. A unit with six Directions of which two are not being executed this period must still draft the two, or cannot save at all. There is no "not this period".

Fix Leave out this period on a card's head, kept on the row; Save then needs at least one item drafted and says which are left out; Apply writes nothing for them. Small.

S6MediumExecution: the items are fixed when the chat opensExecution chat, after a Direction is added to the plan

The chat reads the plan's Directions and Capabilities once, when it is made. A Direction applied to the plan afterwards, or a Capability added by hand, never appears; the person does not know why and opens a second chat.

Fix Read the plan's items again when the chat opens and add the new ones as undrafted cards, with a line in the chat saying so; never remove one that is drafted. Small.

S7MediumExecution: what the plan already holds is not shownExecution chat, an item already carrying measures on the plan

A Direction with measures and tactics on the plan opens as "Not drafted yet". Drafting suggests new ones; Apply then updates by name and adds the rest, so the plan ends with both sets, and the person never saw the old ones in the chat.

Fix Open each card with what the plan holds, marked On the plan as the Directions' rows are, so a draft adds to it and Apply replaces what was shown. Medium.

S8LowDirections from the plan: scoring four old rows to tick oneDirections chat, Start from the existing ones

Plan rows arrive with the scores the plan holds; an unscored one is held until scored (§654). Right in itself, but a person who wants to keep three unscored Directions as they are, untouched, must score all three to tick them, or leave them unticked and trust "nothing is removed". The second is true but not said where the tick is.

Fix The held tick's line says it: Score it first — or leave it unticked; it stays on the plan as it is. Wording only.

3 · Movement between areas, dead ends, wordingWhere to next, and what a line should have said.

S9HighNo onward path after ApplyEvery area, after Apply to plan

After Apply the foot reads v1 on the plan and the chat stops. The method has an order — SWOT, then Competitive Discipline, then Directions, then Execution — but nothing says Next: Directions, and the person goes looking through the tabs. The same after Save in Advisory.

Fix One line after Apply with the next step as a button: Next: Directions — suggest them from this SWOT, opening that area's start screen. Small, and the biggest gain for the least code.

S10HighExecution says "choose Directions first" to someone who didExecution start, Directions saved but not applied

A person who saved Directions v1 and did not press Apply opens Execution and reads The plan has no Directions or Capabilities yet. Choose them first, in the Directions section. They did choose them; the plan does not hold them because they were not applied. The line sends them back to redo what is done.

Fix When a saved Directions version exists and the plan holds none: Directions — Mobile v1 is saved but not on the plan yet. Apply it there first, with a button that opens that version's page. Small.

S11MediumNo "Start from the existing plan" for Competitive Discipline and ExecutionBoth start screens

Foundation, Analysis and Directions offer the plan card when the plan holds something (§653). Competitive Discipline does not, although the plan may hold a discipline and values; Execution does not, although it always reads the plan's items — and then hides what they hold (S7).

Fix The same card on both: Competitive Discipline opens with the plan's discipline and values as the table, scored anew on request; Execution's card is the only card, reading Start from the plan's Directions · 6 items, 2 with measures. Medium, together with S7.

S12MediumA second chat in the same area starts cold, beside an unfinished oneEvery area's start screen

With a Directions chat half done, + New chat offers the same cards as if nothing existed. A person who meant to continue opens a second chat, and now has two "Directions — Mobile" in the rail, one 3 of 4 done, one 0 of 4. Nothing warns.

Fix On the start screen, above the cards, when a chat in this area is unfinished: Directions — Mobile is 3 of 4 done — continue it, or start another. Small.

S13LowApply to plan writes to the place the page is on, said only in the askEvery Apply

The ask names the place (Where: Mobile), and that is right. But a chat made on Mobile and opened later while the page is on Retail Stores still says Mobile in the chat's name and would still write to Mobile — correct, and not obvious; the ask is the only guard.

Fix The pane's head carries the place as the chat's name does, and the ask's Where line is bold when it differs from the page's place. Wording only.

S14LowDraft all stops silently on one failureExecution chat

Draft all runs the items one after another; if the model fails on the third, the red line says so and the chain stops, with Draft all 3 left offered again. Right, but the person does not see which item failed unless they read the cards.

Fix The red line names the item and the card in hand stays on it. Wording only.

4 · What heldTried and found sound; listed so the sweep is whole.

  • Reload at any step — every area's state is the row's; the chat, the pane and the foot read the same after a reload, including the folded rows, the asks and answers, and "on the plan".
  • Held buttons — every disabled control in the six areas says why on hover (§649); the sweep found none without a reason.
  • The box — held with a relevant grey line in every area and every step; Send only once something is typed (§652, §683).
  • Narrow windows — the chat yields, then the rail, the to-do and the work pane leave one at a time; the sheets open and close (§670).
  • Save, then Apply — no area writes the plan on Save; every Apply asks, archives the part as its own kind, and can be put back from Archived plans; Advisory stops at Deliverables.
  • Versions — every saved version's page is drawn as its type with On the plan and Apply; Edit as vN+1 opens its chat (§654).
  • SWOT skipping — an area with no method ticked is left out; Change methods works mid-way and keeps what was gathered; the draft needs only the analyses of the areas ticked.
  • Foundation skipping — parts switched off in Setup never appear; a short answer nudges once and can be overridden; "Leave it as it is" keeps a card.
  • Advisory — Proceed from the first question; two rounds at most; the brief revised through the box; the round box counts the server's count.
  • Model failure — every ask fails in words with nothing lost; the timeout says the answer will be there when the chat is opened again.
