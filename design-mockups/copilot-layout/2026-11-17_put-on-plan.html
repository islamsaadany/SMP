  • The draft beside the chat is drawn as the plan draws that part: Competitive Discipline as the discipline and a card per value (How, measures with target and today, evidence folded); a SWOT as its four boxes; the Foundation as its parts. The Copilot shapes the chat's work into the part when it drafts — a free chat's draft is no longer loose text.
  • Anything the plan needs and the draft lacks is said on it — tick “a value without measures” above: the value is marked, the chip says “1 gap”, and you can say it in the chat or put it on the plan as it is.
  • Save keeps it as a version under Deliverables, laid out the same. Put on Mobile's plan (in the draft or on the deliverable) first shows what it replaces — today's against this — then Apply writes it; the plan's old version is archived, as every Apply does.
  • Only a chat's work in one of the five parts can go on the plan. A question answered, or a brief, stays in the Copilot.
