Copilot room: the rail's hide control, and more space to work

Drawn at half size from the real chrome at 1440×900: top bar 41px, unit row 46px, tab row 47px, section row 54px. That makes 188px of chrome before the Copilot starts. The dashed gold outline marks what each option changes.

Part 1: the rail's hide control

Today the control has a row of its own above Chats (a button and the word "Copilot"). Hidden, it leaves a 44px column holding two buttons. On a narrow window that column turns into a full-width row.

Part 2: more room for the Copilot

Each of these can sit alongside any rail option from Part 1. The green tag gives the height each one gives back at 1440×900.

My recommendation

Rail: A. The control moves into the Chats header, and the hidden state becomes a 24px strip that never turns into a row. It is the smallest change, and the way back stays exactly where you left it.

Room: 1 + 2 together. Use one press to fold the three bars into a single thin line while you work, and tighten the gaps around the Copilot. That gives back about 170px of height and about 30px of width. I also considered folding the bars automatically as you scroll and did not draw it, because the platform removed exactly that behaviour once after it made the page jump (§28).

Two answers are needed: which rail option, and which room options. Nothing is built until you choose.
