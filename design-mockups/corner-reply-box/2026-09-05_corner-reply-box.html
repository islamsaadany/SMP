The reply box that talks to itself

On the office’s corner panel, the box under the Waiting list writes to your own conversation instead of to the person you are looking at. Drawn here in the running platform, before and after.

Reported by Islam, 5 September 2026 Reproduced at 1400×900, signed in as the office Awaiting sign-off — no source touched

What was reported

After sending the reply message as an SMO the message didn’t appear in the box, and when it appeared it appeared above the message of the employee, which is wrong. Islam
And the message never reached the user. Islam, a minute later

Three symptoms, one cause

Driving the corner as the office, standing on the Waiting list, typing a reply and pressing Send: the platform asks the server for “say” — a message from you, to you — with no recipient at all. It never asks for “reply”. That single fact produces everything that was reported.

Measured on the shipped build, one person waiting.
What you saw What is happening
The message never reached her It went into your own thread. No recipient was ever named.
It did not appear in the box You are looking at the list of people; the message went to a different conversation.
It appeared above her message Your own thread had just been updated, so it sorted to the top of the Waiting list.

Nothing is lost. The text you typed is sitting in your own conversation, readable under My messages.

The change

The box is not moved or relabelled — it is simply not drawn while the Waiting list is showing. There is nobody on that screen to reply to.

What does not change

“Reply to Hend…” — and the server is asked for reply, with her named. Nothing on this screen moves.

In the dark palette Both states measured identically; the panel holds its 531px either way.

And the notification test says what it cannot see

Notification is working after fixing it from system settings. Should this be an instruction for the people who are not having notifications set from settings? Islam

Yes — and the honest part is why the test could never have caught it. A browser cannot read your computer’s own notification settings, so the diagnostic will always report every step green while the Mac is quietly blocking the box. That is exactly what happened: every step working, nothing on screen.

So one sentence is added under a successful result only — the one moment somebody is confused. When a step fails, that step already names the address, and this would be noise.
