CARE The one thing that changes on your live tenant

Where your two fields disagree today, the cycle's end moves to the review month.

Your figures are measured against Reporting as of right now, so that month is carried into Covers to — which means not one score moves. That is the point of doing it in that direction.

What does change is which work is asked for. Anything dated between the old end and the new one moves in or out of this round's reporting. On a cycle where the two already agree — which is the case on the worked example — nothing changes at all.
