The proposal, at restOne quiet button before the loud one

The strip is unchanged apart from a gold-outline Edit sitting before the red Close the cycle — the ordinary act ahead of the destructive one. Drawn for the office only, and only while the cycle is open, which is the gate the month picker beside it already uses.

Option AThe panel holds the name and the dates

Recommended

Four fields, in the same order the cycle was opened with. The reporting month stays where it is on the strip, still editable in place, with its “6 of 12 months” line beside it.

What it buys

  • One control per fact. The month has one editor and it is the one that carries its explanation; nothing new can disagree with it.
  • Smallest change. The strip you look at every day is untouched except for one button.
  • The panel is the shape you already know from opening a cycle.

What it costs

  • “Edit” does not mean everything about this cycle — the reporting month is edited on the line above the panel, not inside it.

Option BThe panel holds everything, including the month

The alternative

Five fields. While the panel is open the strip shows the month as plain text, so there is never a second live control for the same value; shut the panel and the strip's picker comes back exactly as it is today.

What it buys

  • One door. Press Edit and every fact about the cycle is in front of you.
  • The month keeps the sentence explaining what it decides, inside the panel.

What it costs

  • The month picker changes shape depending on whether the panel is open. That is a behaviour to remember, and it is the kind of thing that drifts later.
  • Correcting only the month becomes two presses instead of one.

Either wayWhat the change does and does not touch

QuestionAnswer
Who can press it The strategy office, and nobody else. It is drawn from the same permission that already draws Close the cycle, and the server already refuses a change to the cycle from anybody else — no new rule, and nothing new to configure.
When it is drawn While the cycle is open only. A closed cycle's figures are filed under the name it had when it closed, so renaming it afterwards would make the strip and the history disagree.
What it changes The cycle's name, the period it covers, and the date reports are due. No figure, no score and no report state is touched — nothing is cleared, nobody is asked again.
An empty name Refused, in the words the Open panel already uses: the name is what the figures are filed under once the cycle closes.
Cancel Puts the fields back and writes nothing.
The database Nothing new is stored and nothing is migrated — these four values are already there.

One thing worth knowing before you say yes

The cycle's end date is not decoration: where no reporting month has been picked, the platform falls back to the cycle's own end to work out how much of the year has passed. So editing to on a cycle with no month set will move what every running-total figure is measured against. Where a month is set — as on the demo above — the dates are labels and change no score.
