Editing an open cycle

One Edit on the cycle strip. Everything that can change the cycle is behind it — its name, its three dates, the reporting month, and Close the cycle, which no longer sits on the strip where it can be pressed in passing.

“keep the close cycle inside the edit. as it's a critical button to click, the pen should hold everything editable so it's kept secured. closer to B option”

At restThe strip carries no button that changes anything

Shot from the running platform on the demo tenant, as the SMO. Compared with today: the red Close the cycle is gone from the strip, the reporting month reads as a value rather than a picker, and one quiet Edit stands where they were. Nothing on this line can now be pressed by accident.

What is lost, and it is worth saying: correcting the reporting month is two presses now instead of one — Edit, then the picker. That is the price of one door.

EditEverything the cycle is, in one panel

The panel shape you already know from opening a cycle: five fields, the sentence about what the month decides, then Save and Cancel. Close the cycle sits at the far end of that row, apart from them — inside the pen, and still the last thing your eye reaches.

The guardClose cannot run over an unsaved change

Closing files this cycle's figures under its name. So while a field has been changed and not saved, Close is dimmed and says why rather than silently doing one thing or the other — it never quietly saves your rename, and never quietly throws it away. Save or Cancel, then close.

Either wayWhat this changes and what it does not

Who sees Edit The strategy office, and nobody else — the permission that draws Close the cycle today. The server already refuses a change to the cycle from anyone else, so there is no new rule and nothing new to configure.
When it is drawn While the cycle is open. A closed cycle's figures are filed under the name it closed with, so renaming it afterwards would make this page and the history disagree — and with the cycle closed the strip carries Open a new cycle… exactly as it does today, untouched.
What Save changes The name, the period, the due date, the reporting month. No figure, no score and no report state is touched — nothing cleared, nobody asked again.
What Close does Exactly what it does today, including its confirmation naming how many items are unreported. Only where it is pressed has changed.
An empty name Refused, in the words the Open panel already uses.
The database Nothing new stored, nothing migrated — these five values are already there.

Two things I decided, so you can overrule them

  • A worded “Edit”, not the pen glyph. The platform's pen is built for the corner of a card and only appears when the mouse is over it — on a tablet it never appears at all. On a strip with no other control, a word is the safer mark. Say so and I will use the glyph.
  • Close is dimmed with its reason, not hidden. A control that vanishes reads as broken; one that says why it is waiting teaches the rule once.

One consequence worth knowing before you say yes

Where no reporting month has been picked, the platform falls back to the cycle's end date to work out how much of the year has passed. So on such a tenant, editing to moves what every running-total figure is measured against. Where a month is set — as on the demo above — the dates are labels and change no score.
