Reopening a closed cycle

Today a closed cycle cannot be reopened by anyone — the office included — while the reporting page tells people the SMO can. This is the button that makes that sentence true, redrawn on the strip as §261 leaves it.

What is true todayMeasured on this build, not assumed

Closing loses nothing

  • It files the cycle's objective scores and marks the cycle closed. No figure, note, status, submission mark or picture slide is touched.
  • The clearing happens when the next cycle opens — and that path archives everything first, with a Restore.

And nobody can correct anything afterwards

  • Both reporting gates open with state !== "open" before any role test, so a closed cycle takes no figures from anyone, the SMO included.
  • There is exactly one place the platform writes state: "open", and it is the mint of a brand-new cycle. Nothing reopens one.
  • Meanwhile the reporting page says "The SMO reopens a cycle if something has to be corrected." A promise nothing carries out.

The starting pointWhat a closed cycle offers now

Since §261 the strip carries no control that changes anything while a cycle runs — Close the cycle moved behind the pen. Closed, it carries one button: Open a new cycle…, which archives and clears every figure in the tenant.

Option AReopen sits where Close sits

Recommended

The closed cycle gets the same Edit pen. Inside it, the cycle's five facts read as a record — nothing here can be changed while it stays closed — and Reopen takes the far-end slot that holds Close the cycle while the cycle is open. One rule, both directions: the pen's far end holds the cycle's one dangerous state change.

What it buys

  • The strip keeps its promise. §261 cleared it of dangerous buttons; this does not put one back.
  • One place to go. Edit means change this cycle whether it is running or closed — and while it is closed, the only thing you can change is that.
  • The read-only facts answer the question people actually open a closed cycle to ask: what were its dates, and what was it reported as of.

What it costs

  • Reopening is two presses instead of one.
  • A pen that opens onto values you cannot edit needs its sentence to explain why — it is in the panel.

Option BReopen on the strip

The alternative

A red Reopen beside Open a new cycle…, which is where the earlier drawing of this put it — before §261 cleared the strip.

What it buys

  • One press, and the button names the cycle it reopens.
  • Nothing to discover: it is on the line you are already reading.

What it costs

  • It reverses §261 for the closed cycle. The most consequential control on the page goes back onto a line people open to read.
  • Two loud buttons side by side, one of which clears every figure in the tenant.

Either wayWhat it asks, and what it does not change

One thing this does not fix, and should not pretend to

Reopening lets figures be corrected. It does not undo an Open a new cycle… — that path clears the tenant and hands you an archive with a Restore, and Restore is what puts it back. Two different ways home for two different acts.
