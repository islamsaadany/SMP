Banner 2, when you have unsaved edits — one decision to make

Option A · Keep your edits (recommended)

Sara Nabil updated this page while you were workingYour changes are safe. Reload brings in her update and keeps yours on top.

Friendliest — nobody loses work. Slightly more to build (it re-applies your edits after reloading).

Option B · Warn to copy first

Sara Nabil updated this page while you were workingReloading replaces your unsaved changes with her latest — copy anything you need first.

Simplest to build, but puts the burden on the person to not lose their typing.

Why amber, and how it differs from the red you already have

Amber — caution. Nothing has gone wrong yet; act to stay safe. The two banners above.
Red — failure. A save was refused or couldn't reach the server. Already in the platform today.
