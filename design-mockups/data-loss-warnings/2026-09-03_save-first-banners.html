SMP · Save-safety banners · §258.2 · 3 September 2026

Reload saves first, and the banner says so

Every picture below is the real built platform, shot from the branch, not a drawing. Both buttons now commit the box you are in, save, and reload only when the server has answered “saved”.

  • The instruction you asked for — “Finish what you are typing, then press Reload” — on both banners.
  • The page is named in the second banner, and the banner leaves when you move to another tab.
  • A failed save never reloads — the red bar explains in plain words (no “HTTP 500”), the amber one stays, the button comes back.

Light

A newer version is ready

After a deploy. One button. The sentence tells them to finish the box they are in and says the button saves first.

“Finish what you are typing, then press Reload — it saves your work first and then opens the new version.”

Somebody else updated this page

Names the person AND the page. Goes away by itself when you move to another tab.

“Finish what you are typing, then press Reload & keep mine — it saves your work first, then brings in their update with yours on top.”

While it saves

Either button reads Saving… until the server answers. The reload happens only on the word “saved”.

If the save fails

Nothing reloads. The red bar says it in plain words — what to do, and that the tab keeps trying by itself. The technical detail sits on the hover of “Not saved.” for whoever has to chase it. The amber bar stays and the button is live again.
