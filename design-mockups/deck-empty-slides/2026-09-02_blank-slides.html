Why a slide can be blank at all

A slide whose table has no rows is still drawn: the heading, the navy column strip, then a whole empty page. A capability function's deck already refuses to draw its objectives slide when there are none. The unit deck — which a function planning in pillars goes through since §224 — never learned the same rule. One question, two answers.

The headline slide, with the objectives cell dropped

Merchandising › Where Merchandising stands

You asked for the cell to go, and for the rule to hold for any function with no key objectives — Marketing included. Marketing is already right: its deck guards that cell today, which is the half of the product this fix brings the other half up to.

The two-column shape needs no new CSS. It is the shape this slide already wore before §243 added the third number, so the surviving figures simply grow into the room.

One thing this exposed, and it is not free

The footnote still opens “Objectives measure what was committed to” — explaining a number that is no longer on the slide. It has to lose that clause when the cell goes, or the slide argues with itself. I would drop just that sentence and leave the other two exactly as they read.

The one I need a word on

Retail Stores › RS04 Merchandising

This is the worse of the two blanks: a score of 93% / 60% / 61% printed across the top, and nothing at all behind it. Those numbers are Merchandising's, arriving through the pointer by: "merchandising" that spec 10 puts on Retail's fourth pillar — your own worked example, where Merchandising's three pillars produce Retail's R04.

“Drop the merchandizing connection with the retail stores”

That can mean three different things, and two of them change the product rather than the deck. I will not guess between them:

  • Only on the deck — Retail's review stops showing the carried pillar's two empty tables. The pillar keeps its score, its title slide and its place in Retail's plan. Nothing about the product changes.
  • Cut the pointer — RS04 stops being fed by Merchandising and becomes an ordinary Retail pillar with measures and tactics of its own. Retail's score moves.
  • Remove the pillar — Retail drops to three pillars and Merchandising stops appearing in Retail's plan at all. This retires spec 10's worked example, so the carried-pillar feature would no longer be visible anywhere in the demo.
