SMP Design Standard

The short list every page is built from. Today the platform uses 36 text sizes, 31 corner roundings, 40 shadows and 130 colours typed in directly. This page sets it down to six sizes, four spacing steps, three corners, two shadows, one card, one icon set and one typeface.

Source Sans 3 everywhere, headings included. A heading stands out by size and weight, never by a second typeface. Codes such as MB01 use the same face with even-width numbers. No size between two steps, so no 12.5 beside 12 and no 11.5 beside 11.

How pages move to the standard

  1. The standard is written into the stylesheet as the named sizes above, beside today's names, so nothing moves on day one.
  2. Pages move one at a time, each with its own before and after mockup for sign-off.
  3. An automatic check lists every size, corner, shadow and colour not on this page, so the number falls page by page and cannot creep back.
  4. Every new page is built from this list from the start.

Open question, not decided here

The navigation is three rows across the top: the client bar, the places, then the tabs and sections. HR ERP uses one side menu instead. Moving to a side menu is a structural change with its own mockup, and this standard applies either way.
