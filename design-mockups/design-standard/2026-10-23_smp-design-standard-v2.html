Version 2 · 23 Oct 2026 · replaces the version of 24 Sep

The platform's own best patterns, taken from the Internal Tracker, My work and the console, set down once so every page is built from them. Marked new where it changes September, kept where it does not.

Source Sans 3 everywhere, the console included. It is the client platform's typeface and the Tracker's. The console's typewriter-style labels (FOREFRONT, SUPER USER, WORK) move to Source Sans capitals. Codes such as MB01 use Source Sans with even-width numbers. Six sizes and no others.

Unchanged from September

kept

Space

Four steps: 4, 8, 16, 24. Rows laid out with gaps.

Corners and shadows

6 on controls, 10 on cards, round on pills. A shadow only on what sits over the page.

Colour

Named colours only, in light and dark. The brand bar follows the client's branding.

Buttons

All 32 tall; one solid orange button per screen at most.

Fields and dialogs

Every box 36 tall. Save solid, Cancel quiet.

Icons and empty places

One drawn icon set, no typed symbols. An empty place says why and how to fill it.
