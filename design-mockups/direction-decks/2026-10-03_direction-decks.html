Each direction presents its own part

For a client planned at company level with business units off. Every direction already has an owner and a custodian (built last round). This makes the review match that: each direction gets its own short presentation for its owner to give, and the Strategy office keeps a presentation of the whole company. The slides below are the platform's real slides, made from the worked example.

1 · The Presentation menu, as the Strategy office

Today the menu has one Present, which opens one deck of about 80 slides covering all 25 directions. Proposed: Present the whole company at the top, then a list of directions, each one opening its own deck. Download, Manage slides, Download the plan and Master presentation stay as they are.

3 · One direction's deck: 6 slides

The cover names the direction, its code and the cycle, and says who presents it: the owner, plus the custodian if there is one. After that come the direction's own slides, the same ones it has in the company deck today: the title page with its two numbers, Key measures, Tactics (continued if the list is long), then Thank you.

GR01 Digital & Data-Driven Operations: cover, title page, key measures, tactics, tactics continued, thank you.

4 · The whole company: 7 slides

The office's deck keeps only what is about the whole company: the cover, the list of directions, each direction's score, where the company stands, and Thank you. Each direction's detail lives in that direction's own deck, so it is not presented twice.

Cover, the Strategic Pillars divider, the names, the Overall performance divider, every direction's score, where the company stands, thank you.

5 · The Master presentation runs direction by direction

The flow the office already sets up lists the company's directions as separate stops, so a review meeting runs one owner after another, with the office closing on the whole company. The order, the timers and drag-to-reorder all work as they do today.

To answer before I build

A · What is in the whole-company deck? I recommend the 7-slide summary above. The alternative is to keep today's full 80-slide deck with every direction's detail in it.
B · Who can present which direction? Today anyone who can open a page can press Present; there is no restriction. I recommend keeping that: everyone sees every direction in the list, with their own first and marked "Yours". The alternative is that an owner sees only their own direction, as drawn in section 2.
C · Can an owner or custodian add their own picture slides to their direction's deck? These are the Manage slides pictures, which today only the office and a unit's head or custodian can add. I recommend yes, for their own direction only.
D · In the Master presentation, does the whole company come last or first? I recommend last, as drawn in section 5.
E · Projects. When the company plans in projects instead of directions, I recommend the same treatment: one deck per project, presented by the project's owner.
