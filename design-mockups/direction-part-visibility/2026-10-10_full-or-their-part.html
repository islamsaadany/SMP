Direction owners: full plan, or only their part

Pictures taken from the running platform as a test direction owner (Direction one of two), with business units switched off as at El Abd. “Today” is the platform as it is; the right-hand shots were made by hiding things on the live page, so they show the intended result rather than finished styling. Hiding is on the screen only: the whole plan still reaches their browser (agreed).

Roles & access — the Part owner row

A new two-way choice under the Group / Company cell of the Part owner row: Full plan (today, the default) or Their part. It applies to direction owners and custodians only, for now. Changing it changes no other cell.

Reporting — what a direction owner fills in

With Their part, the company's key objectives table goes (they could only read it), the rail lists only their own direction, and the bar says what is theirs (D001 · 0 of 1 entered) instead of View only. In the build the count beside it becomes theirs too (0 of 1), not the company's 6 of 7.

Performance — what they read

With Their part, Performance opens on their own direction only. The company's overall scores, the themes and the capabilities sections are not shown to them.

Foundation — unchanged

Kept, as you asked: the company's Foundation (who we are, mission, aspiration and key objectives) stays visible to everyone either way.

To sign off
  1. The Full plan | Their part choice under the Part owner row's Group / Company cell, defaulting to Full plan.
  2. With Their part: the company's key objectives and overall scores, the themes, the capabilities and the other directions are hidden; the Foundation stays.
  3. Presentations already show only their own directions (built earlier) and stay as they are.
