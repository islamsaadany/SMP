Dismissing what somebody said about where they work

Made out of the running product, with the proposed control injected into the live page. The claim stays on record and stops counting as something waiting on you — your decision, already taken.

Nothing here is built yet
WHY

The product promises a dismiss it has never had

I drove this rather than read it. Accepting works — somebody in Mobile who said Logistics, pressed Use it, moved to Logistics, count cleared, queue emptied. What does not exist is any way to say no, the register was already right.

Setup › Overview“1 person said where they work — accept or dismiss”
The register rowMobile ◎ — hover: “They said they work in Logistics. Open their row to accept it.”
Their row’s dialog“They said they work in Logistics — the register says Mobile.” → Use it
The whole pagesearched for any dismiss control: none. The server has no action for it either.

So a claim you disagree with keeps its mark, its queue entry, the register’s badge, the rail pill and the Overview row for ever. Only the Overview ever promised the way out.

01

Dismiss sits beside Use it

In the row’s own dialog, under Unit or function — where the claim is already stated and where the field it argues with lives.

What it costs One column on the declarations table to record that you answered, and one server action. Nothing about anybody’s access changes — a declaration never granted anything. If they say it again later it comes back as a fresh claim, which is right: that is a new statement.

02

...and what the row shows afterwards

This is the one open point, and drawing it is what found the problem. The ring is 9.6px wide at 11px type. I first proposed keeping it and shifting its colour from gold to grey — and at that size the two are indistinguishable. A colour change on a 9.6px glyph is not a state anybody can read.

A is my recommendation. The ring exists to say something is waiting on you. Once you have answered, nothing is — so keeping a mark spends that signal on a non-signal, and the register is the one page where a mark should mean work.

What A costs, and it is real From the table alone you cannot tell “nobody said anything” from “somebody said something and you dismissed it”. The claim is still on record and still readable — you open their row to see it, which is where you would go to change your mind anyway. B keeps that visible at the price of a mark on a row with nothing outstanding.
