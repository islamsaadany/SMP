1 · Setup › Terminology
What changes

The same treatment for every term

  • A plain one-line description under each term, written for the person setting up, not for us. Today's notes ("Previously called North Star…") are replaced.
  • The default under each box, so the setup person always sees what the platform would say.
  • Reset appears only when a word differs from the default. Emptying a box does the same thing.
  • The "Internal name, never changes" column goes. The term's own name heads the row instead.
  • A new row, Company (shaded here only to mark it as new): the layer that groups units and functions under the group. Its default is "Companies", and your tenant would type "Divisions".
One exception, and why

Company needs a word for one as well as for several

Every other term is used at two levels, the group and a unit, so its row has two boxes for those. Company is used at only one level, but the screen needs it in two forms: a list says "Divisions" and a column says "Division". So on the Company row the two boxes mean several and one, and each says so under the box. The cost is that this row's boxes don't match the column headings above them.

2 · Where your word appears
  • The Setup rail entry and that page's title (Divisions).
  • The column on Setup › Business units and Setup › Functions (Division), and the field in Edit details.
  • The navigation dropdown that lists the group and each division.
  • The set-up flow's words step asks for it with the other terms, so a new client names it on day one.
