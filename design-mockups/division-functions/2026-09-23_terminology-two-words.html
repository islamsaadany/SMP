Terminology: thirteen rows, a word for one and a word for many

Setup › Terminology, drawn in the running platform. The descriptions and default words are the ones you set in chat. Two rows show a client's own words as examples (Vision, Sector), which is why they carry Reset.

What changes

  • Thirteen rows, in your order. Each opens with your description, so nobody has to know the platform's internal names.
  • Two boxes on each row. One word for a single thing (a column heading, "Add a Project"), one for many (a page heading, the navigation, "3 Projects"). Both start filled with the default.
  • The default sits beside the boxes, one above the other, so you can always see what the platform would say. Reset appears only when either box differs from the default; emptying a box does the same for that box.
  • One word per thing, on every page. The separate group and business-unit words go. Whatever you type shows at the group, in every business unit and in every function.
  • Three new rows: Division, Supporting Function and Project. Division is the word for today's Companies layer.

What this changes on today's screens

  • The aspiration uses one word. Today the group says Vision and a business unit says Winning Aspiration. The default becomes Winning Aspiration everywhere.
  • Capabilities get their own row instead of hiding in the Pillars row as "Group capabilities".
  • The Companies layer is called Division by default. The navigation, Setup and the reports all follow the word typed here.
  • Where a client already typed two different words for one row, the word shown on business-unit pages is kept, and its single form is filled from the platform's default until someone changes it.
