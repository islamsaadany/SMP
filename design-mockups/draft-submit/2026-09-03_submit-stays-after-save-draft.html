Submit stays after Save draft

“in the reporting on saving the draft keep the submit to smo button there as it’s posible to save the draft and if it’s complete we can submit directly rather than reopen to submit.”

Every bar below was shot out of the running platform — Mobile’s report, 1500px, 41 of 41 entered — so what is being compared is the real control at its real size, not a drawing of it.

What happens today

Save draft closes the report and takes Submit away with it, so the only way to send a finished report is Reopen first, then Submit.

What is proposed

Submit stays on the bar in the draft state. Two ways to seat it, and the only difference between them is how loudly Reopen speaks beside it.

A — Reopen keeps its outlined button

as drawn today

Nothing about Reopen changes; Submit is added in front of it. Two bordered controls sit side by side, which is what makes it the wider of the two.

C — Reopen speaks like Save draft did

recommended

Reopen loses its box and becomes the same quiet orange type Save draft wears while the report is open. The bar then reads in the two volumes it already has — the act that ends the report shouts, the lesser act speaks — and it is 29px narrower.

A draft that is not finished

Submit is drawn either way, dimmed, with the reason on hover — the same gate it already has while the report is open. Saving a draft early can never turn into submitting an unfinished report by accident.

What it costs

The bar rides the tab row, so a wider bar meets the edge of the window sooner. Measured on the running platform, at the width where the page first has to be scrolled sideways to reach Close.

On a 1440 or 1280 laptop nothing moves. On a 1000px window the draft bar becomes the widest state of the page, where today it is the narrowest — so somebody working on a small laptop would meet a sideways scroll after saving a draft that they do not meet while reporting. That is the whole price, and it is why C is the recommendation.

What does not change

  • The report stays dimmed. Save draft still closes it, exactly as you asked for it: the figures and the note are locked until Reopen. This only puts Submit back on the bar — it does not reopen anything.
  • Submit still refuses an incomplete report. Same gate, same reason on hover, whether the draft is saved or not.
  • Submitting clears the draft. A report cannot be submitted and parked at once, so Reopen goes on meaning one thing.
  • Nothing new is stored, and the SMO’s side is untouched. The only change is which controls the bar draws while a draft is saved.

One thing to pick

A or C — whether Reopen keeps its outlined button, or becomes quiet type like Save draft.

Say which and it is built; nothing has been changed in the platform yet.
