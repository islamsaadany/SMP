What changes, and what doesn't

  • WhereOnly El Abd's company Reporting page. It is the only box that shows directions and capabilities apart and holds both. Wander's company holds capabilities only, and every unit already reports one list in one rail.
  • WhoAnyone who sees both halves: the SMO and the Super user. A direction owner still sees only their directions and a capability owner only their capabilities, with no labels, exactly as today.
  • SelectionOne selected row on the page instead of two. It opens on your own row if you own one, otherwise on D001, as the rail does today.
  • StrategyUnchanged. Directions and Capabilities stay two tabs there, as you set.
  • PerformanceUnchanged. Its two groups of gauges are read, not filled, so they stay apart.
  • PhoneThe rail turns into the strip it already becomes on a phone. The two labels drop out there, and the D and C codes tell the kinds apart.

Two things to decide

  1. The heading over the section. Drawn here as Directions and Capabilities — Key Measures and Tactics. The alternative is no heading at all, because the two labels in the rail already say it.
  2. The order inside the rail. Drawn here as directions first, then capabilities, each under its label. The alternative is one list in the order the plan stores them, with the kind shown on each row and no labels.
