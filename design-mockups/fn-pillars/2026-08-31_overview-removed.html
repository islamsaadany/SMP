What it costs, and what it reverses

It reverses your ruling of 24 August — “the function that plans in pillars has overview as well.” Recorded as a reversal, not overwritten. That ruling is still right about the word: a capability function keeps its Overview, because it has capabilities to describe. What changed is that this format turned out to have nothing to describe.

Business units are untouched. So is every capability function — both its sections are still there, and its own Overview is still counted.

One thing is now unreachable, and it already was. A pillars function’s own key objectives have no screen to type them on anywhere in the platform. They are no longer counted as missing, because counting something with nowhere to fill it is what caused this.
