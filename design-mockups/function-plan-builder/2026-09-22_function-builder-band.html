What I am asking you to sign off

  • Four boxes for every function, differing only in the third — one shape rather than three.
  • No Foundation box and no SWOT box, because a supporting function does not write its own aspiration or its own SWOT; it takes both from the unit it plans under.
  • “Definitions” becomes “Definition” — it counts the function's one definition now, not several capabilities'.
  • Objectives stays optional and says so on the hover: a function with none is judged by its work.
