What changes

  • Four stacked links → one ⋮ menu. This is the whole 155 px. Delete is renamed Delete permanently and sits below a divider, away from Retire.
  • Status shows Active / Retired. It is a status column that has never held one.
  • # and Function freeze at 38 px and ~216 px.
  • The key finance line goes to an off-by-default column, as the register's sign-in name did (§69.11).
  • A Columns chooser in the header, beside the search.
  • The Official BU list gets the same — it measures 131 px rows today, from the same cause.

What does not change

  • Delete still refuses while anything points at the function, and still names what — the capability, the pillar scored from here, the people attached, the Official BU mapping, anything ever reported (§62). Your answer: keep it.
  • Retire stays the default. Deleting is for a function nothing holds.
  • Plans in keeps its chip and its under line — it is the one cell that genuinely says two things (§59).
  • No column is removed, only hidden by default and recoverable from Columns.

Today IT, Care and Strategy Management Office can be deleted outright. The other five each name what is holding them — Finance, for one: a capability and a person.
