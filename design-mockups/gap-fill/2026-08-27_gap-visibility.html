The one thing to look at before saying yes — collaborators and reporting rights. Being named on a tactic is what lets a Contributor report that line (§50.2) — it is not just a label. My recommendation, drawn above: a collaborator filled by the custodian is visible at once but confers reporting rights only when the office confirms — the same shape as the score waiting on the target. Cheap to build (a pending list reads as empty to the rights question), and it keeps the fill grant from quietly handing out reporting access. If you would rather the rights be live immediately, say so and it is one line the other way.

What this mockup proposes

  • The gap band (fill mode): §129's chip map re-used — one chip per place, counts read live from the data, press to go, ✓ when a place is clear.
  • Next gap: one button that walks every Missing field in order, wrapping across sections and pillars, with the remaining count on it. When it hits zero the band reads "Nothing missing — N awaiting confirmation".
  • The counts that find you: a number on the Strategy tab and on each rail row, read mode included — drawn only for the fill grant and the office.
  • Collaborators fillable (reverses §132.2's exclusion at your direction): empty → fillable, pending until confirmed; an existing list never opens. Rights question stated in the amber note above.
  • This year shown by default on a unit's objectives (reverses §66's default, keeps the toggle and everyone's saved choice) — its Missing becomes visible, and it is already fillable.
  • Nothing changes for anyone without the fill grant — no badge, no band, no walker for a plain reader.
