Which slide are we on?

On the clear views of the key measures and tactics slides, mark the slide so the room knows which one it is and how the two differ. Nothing is dimmed in either option. Real screenshots of the live tour with each option drawn on top.

Option A: ring the slide's title

The title line is ringed in gold and the slide's name (KEY MEASURES, TACTICS) turns gold. Nothing is added to the slide, but the name stays the size it is on the slide.

Option B: a label naming the slide and what it is about

A label in the top-right corner, in the platform's navy with a gold edge: KEY MEASURES, "The results we committed to", and TACTICS, "The work that delivers them". It names the difference, results and work, in words the back of the room can read. It sits in the empty corner beside the title, so it covers nothing.

To decide

  • A, B, or both. I recommend B: it is the one that says what the difference is, not only which slide it is.
  • The label's words if B: "The results we committed to" and "The work that delivers them", or yours.
  • Where it shows: on the clear views only (the opening and closing whole-slide moments), or kept on for every stop on that slide.
