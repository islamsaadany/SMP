Helicopter view: choose which processes to show

Three ways to do it. Each one works below: tick or press to see the rails change.

A A "Processes" dropdown with a tick list

One button beside Milestones | Cards. It opens a searchable list with a box per process, subs under their main. Takes no room until you open it. Ticking a main ticks its subs.

▤ Milestones▢ Cards

B One chip per main process, always visible

A row of chips above the rails, one per main process with its subs counted. Press to show or hide that main and all its subs. Fastest for a few groups; gets long with many, and you can't pick a single sub on its own.

▤ Milestones▢ Cards

C A tick list down the left of the rails

The full list stays open beside the picture, like a legend you can tick. Easiest to see what's in and out at a glance; costs the rails about a quarter of the width.
