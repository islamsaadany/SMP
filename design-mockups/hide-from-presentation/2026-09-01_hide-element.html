Hiding an element from the presentation

A per-row switch in the plan pen, the office's only. A hidden row is kept — every figure, every word — left out of the presentation, and not counted in any score. It applies to an objective, a measure, a tactic, a deliverable, an outcome or a milestone — never to a whole pillar, capability or project.

4 · What was decided, and what each decision carries

Hidden is not counted (Islam, 2026-09-01)
The row leaves every average — the pillar's, the unit's, the group's — on the pages and in the deck alike, so there is one score everywhere. It follows that reporting stops asking for its figure, its blanks stop counting as Missing, and Submit no longer waits on it. Hiding every row of a pillar leaves the pillar reading a dash, never 0.
Rows only — never a pillar, a capability or a project
The eye is drawn on the six row kinds and nowhere else, so a whole slide can never disappear by accident.
The mark travels in the workbook
Each row sheet gains a Hidden column (Yes, or empty), so a download and re-upload keeps the marks — without it, replacing a plan would silently show everything again.
