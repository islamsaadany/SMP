A hidden row stays on the slide

The eye works as it does today: the row is not counted and reporting stops asking for it. What changes is the presentation. Instead of leaving the row out, the slide keeps it in its place, greyed, with the same Hidden — not counted chip the plan page already wears, so the presenter can show that it was set aside on purpose. Always on, for every deck, and for the PowerPoint download too.

3 · What this changes, and what it does not

Changes
The review deck, Present mode, the deck PDF and the PowerPoint download keep a hidden row where it sits, greyed, with the chip after its name. In the PowerPoint the whole row is written in grey with the words Hidden — not counted after the name.
Does not change
Scoring, reporting, Submit, the gap count, the plan page, the Performance tables and the workbook's Hidden column are untouched. The eye still hides and shows with one press.
Cost, stated
A hidden row now takes a line on the slide again, so a long table may split one slide earlier than it does today. And anything hidden is back in front of the room: a row hidden because it should not be seen, rather than because it was dropped, needs the × instead.
