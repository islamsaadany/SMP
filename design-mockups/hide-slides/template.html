Hiding slides from a review

Manage slides · drawn in the built platform, v3.22

“Allow the SMO to hide presentation slides of any unit or function.” — the feature §246 named and did not build: §233 hides rows from the presentation and nothing hides a generated slide.

Scoring
Presentation only. The projector skips it; nothing is re-counted.
Lifespan
It lasts. Hidden until somebody shows it again.
Who
The SMO. Custodians and owners see the state and cannot change it.

The rail

Every row gains an eye. A hidden slide keeps its place in the list, greys out, and says so — a slide you cannot find is a slide you cannot bring back (§61). Both shots are the same build, standing on the same slide.

The slide itself

The pane already explains a generated slide and offers nothing to do with it. Hidden, it says what hiding costs — which is the sentence that keeps this apart from §233.

What it stores

A slide is named by its anchor — the named point §236.3 already writes on every generated slide, and what the picture picker is built from. Never its position: a pillar added upstream renumbers the deck, and a stored number would then hide a different slide (§48).

The model
QuestionAnswerBecause
where units[k].extra / functions[k].extra Both already carry an extra, so no migration. Not REVIEW, which is cleared every cycle.
what a list of anchor keys Absent when nothing is hidden, deleted when the last one is shown again (§50.6).
who the office A unit setting the authoriser does not classify falls through to the SMO by design (§42) — the server refuses everybody else without a new rule.
when after the pictures are placed A picture anchored to a hidden slide still appears. Continuations carry their parent's anchor, so a long table goes whole.

What it does not touch

This is the answer to the scoring question, written out. Hiding a slide is a decision about a projector and nothing else.

  • Every score stays where it is — objectives, pillars, execution, the group roll-up.
  • Every figure is still asked for — the reporting page, the counts, and what Submit refuses over.
  • Every page reads as it does today — Plan, Performance and the workbook are untouched.
  • §233 is unchanged — hiding a row still takes it out of the count; that switch stays the only one that moves a number.

What it costs

  • A hidden slide outlives the cycle. It is hidden in September because somebody hid it in March. The count above the list is the guard: the rail says how many, on every visit, with one press to show them all.
  • A custodian cannot prune their own deck. They open Manage slides for their pictures, see what the office has hidden, and ask.
  • Nothing is carved out. The cover and Thank you can be hidden too — a deck emptied to nothing is refused, and that is the only floor.

Sign off on A, and I build it

Manage slides is where the whole deck is already shown as real slides at one tenth, so nothing new is drawn. If you would rather the control sat somewhere else, or B reads better to you, say so before I touch the sources.
