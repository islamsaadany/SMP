3 · Setup › History: a removed row gets a Restore that works

The removal is one line like any other: who, when, the row, the field Row, and where it sat, MB01 · measure 2 of 5 → removed. Its Restore opens the page's own confirmation, naming the old seat. Below it, the reporting change made on that row is held until the row is back, with the reason on hover.

What this decides

Old position
The row comes back where it was; the rows after it move down one. If a row has since been added in that place, it comes back just before it. Its reported figures and notes are not deleted on removal, so they are there again when it returns.
On screen, on the Plan page
Reviewing an old plan happens where the plan is. Setup › History stays the list of changes with its Restore buttons. The office alone sees the As-it-stood link; a unit head keeps See history.
From now on
A removed row's contents travel with the removal, which is what makes its Restore possible. Rows removed before this is built have nothing saved and keep today's greyed button. The day view reaches as far back as the change log does.
Applies to the six row kinds
An objective, a measure, a tactic, a deliverable, an outcome, a milestone. A whole pillar or project keeps its own way back, the archive.
