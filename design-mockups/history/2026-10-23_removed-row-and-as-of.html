Put back Legacy churn index on Mobile, as measure 2 of MB01?
It was removed by Mohamed Essam today at 09:40, with its target 70, its compile rule Average, its hidden mark and the figures reported this cycle (64 in Q3). It returns to its old place with all of them. Putting it back is an ordinary change: it is saved, authorised and logged like any other, so it can itself be put back.
  • What the line shows. The row's name, the field Row, and where it sat: the pillar, its kind and its position. The row's whole contents travel with the removal from now on, which is what makes Restore possible. Rows removed before this is built have no contents saved and keep today's greyed button.
  • Old position. It comes back as measure 2, the rows after it move down one. If a row has since been added in that place, it comes back just before it.
  • Its own history comes with it. Reported figures and notes keyed on the row are not deleted when a row is removed, so they are there again when it returns. Until then, a changed value on the removed row cannot be restored on its own: the button says to put the row back first.
  • Applies to the six row kinds. An objective, a measure, a tactic, a deliverable, an outcome, a milestone. A whole pillar or project keeps its own way back, the archive.

  • Where it lives. On the Plan page of a unit, a function or the company, beside the Last changed by … · See history line that is already there: one control, As it stood on…. The office sees it; nobody else does. The pen is put away while an old plan is shown.
  • What it shows. The same Plan page, with every value as it was at the end of the chosen day: rows, targets, owners, quarters, and the figures reported by then. Nothing on it can be edited, the banner says which day it is and how many changes have been made since, and Back to today returns to the live plan.
  • Setup › History stays the list of changes. It keeps the filters and the Restore buttons, including the new one for a removed row (part 1). Reviewing an old plan happens where the plan is; putting one value back happens where the change is.
  • Rows that are gone wear a chip. A row removed between that day and today reads Removed since, greyed, so the reader is told why they cannot find it on today's plan. That is the one mark added; nothing else on the old plan is annotated.
  • How it is built. From the change log, walking back from today to the chosen day. It reaches as far back as the log does: a day before the log began shows the plan as the log first knew it, and the banner says so.
  • Office only. The control is drawn for the office alone, like the pen's eye. A unit head keeps the See history door and the change list for their own unit, as today.
