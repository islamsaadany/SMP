And then — how big

The mark grows in twos

It is the square minus the mark that has to be even, not the mark itself. The box stays 34px, so 22 sits 6 a side and 24 sits 5 — while 23 leaves eleven pixels of slack and brings the half-pixel lean straight back. An odd size here is not a slightly different look; it is the fault you reported, again.

Both readings are real and they cannot both be nought. A house is bottom-heavy, so wherever its box is centred its weight sits low, and wherever its weight is centred its box sits high. There is no position that satisfies both.

Shipped as A at 22px, at your word: the drawing’s box centred, no shift and no nudge in the drawing at all, in a box that has not moved. Measured on the build: 0.000px across, 0.125px down, the same at 1500, 1280 and 1000.
