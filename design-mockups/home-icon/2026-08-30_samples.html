What each costs. B and E are the quietest — nothing competes with the destination names — but with no box the mark reads as decoration rather than something to press, and there is no lit state to show when you are on the welcome screen. C and D keep it pressable and square it off; D is the lightest that still looks like a control. F is the only one that can also say you are here, in the gold the bar already uses for the current destination — the cost is that gold on this row currently means "the page you are on", so a permanently gold house would borrow that meaning.

My recommendation: D. It is square as you asked, still plainly a control, and it takes no colour the row is already using for something else.
