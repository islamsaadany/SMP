How it works

  • Home is a new tab at the top of the column, and the console opens on it. My work stays as it is, below Home.
  • The tasks panel opens on Overdue when anything is overdue, otherwise on This week. With nothing to show, it says so ("No overdue tasks"). Write-ups asked of you appear here, marked with a book.
  • Your clients: one row each, with a button into each module.
  • Case studies: your write-ups with a ring for the sections done and Continue, and below them your insights still to place. Beside them, the case studies finished in the last 30 days.
