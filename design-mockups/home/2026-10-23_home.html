What changes

  • My work is renamed Home and stays first in the column. The console still opens on it.
  • Your tasks shows late and this week. "All 9 tasks" opens the full list, which is today's My work.
  • Your clients are the ones you have a seat on, with each module one press away, carrying the same marks as on Clients.
  • New in case studies shows the ones finished in the last 30 days, and your insights still to place.

What it costs

  • Home reads more when it opens: your clients and the case studies as well as your Tracker, so it may take a moment longer to fill.
  • An admin with no seat sees no clients there, only "All clients ›". Admins could instead see every active client; say if you want that.
  • Empty blocks are left out: no case studies in 30 days means no strip.
