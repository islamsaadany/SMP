Card titles, and one look for My tasks and Clients

Two answers. First, four fonts for the card titles beside today's serif, on the same Home cards. Second, My tasks and Clients drawn with Home's look: a navy band at the top in the greeting's style, and everything under it in cards with their header inside. "Before" is what is live.

01

The card titles

the same two cards in each font

Why the serif looks off: it is the only serif on the platform, and on a Mac and on Windows it is a different typeface (Iowan Old Style or Palatino), so it never quite matches the system font around it. A uses the platform's own font, as every other title does, a touch heavier so the card still has a heading; I would also take the greeting's "Good morning" into it, so Home has one font. B makes the title a quiet label, like the column's headings. C is A in the navy, a little larger, for more presence. D keeps a serif but one that looks the same on every computer (Georgia). All four use fonts already on every computer, so nothing new is downloaded.

02

My tasks in Home's look

drawn with title font A

Before · live

After

The band becomes Home's greeting band: the week as its gold line, "My tasks" as the title, the counts on the right, and Show (whose tasks, for admins) beside the title. Each group becomes a card with its header inside: an icon (red for Late), the name and its count. Sort by stays on its own line under the band. The rows are today's rows, unchanged, with the tick and the status.

03

My tasks sorted by client

Sorted by client, each client is a card: its name, open and late counts, and on the right a link straight into that client's Internal Tracker. Sort by sits on the band's line so it stays in one place whichever way the list is grouped.

04

Clients in Home's look

Before · live

After

The band says what is here (4 active · 1 ended · 1 demo) and carries + Add a client as its one button, in amber. One card holds the clients, its header carrying everything that filters them: Active · Ended · Demo, Mine, the search, and cards or list. The client cards inside keep their modules and marks, lose the boxed SUPER USER and UNITS tags for one quiet line at the foot ("Super user · 10 units"), and Settings moves to that line as a word.
