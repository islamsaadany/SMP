Telling people a report is up

One email per report, carrying a link straight to it, and a mark in the top bar so the platform agrees with the email when they open it.

Both halves are made of things the platform already has. The email below is the real builder's output, not a drawing of it.

1 · The email

Sent once, when a report is published. The button is the link.

Nothing in this email is new. It is the platform's own message shell — the client's bar, the gold rule, the greeting by first name, the button, the footer. The button already existed for other messages; this is the first one to point at a report.

The file is not attached. A report is often large and is narrowed to particular places, so it stays behind the sign-in and the link is what opens it.

With nothing new there is no dot and no chip — which is how it reads on most days, and the reason the dot means something when it is there.

What this costs, said before you take it

  • The dot needs more than one module. The modules button is only drawn for a client that has more than one — a menu with one entry is a door behind a door. Raya Trade has Strategy and Insights, so it is drawn. A client with Strategy alone gets the email and no dot.
  • Every page load asks the library a small question to know whether to draw the dot. It is one short read of the reports this person may see.
  • The dot follows the count, not the reading. It says this month, not you have not opened these — the platform does not record who has read what. Remembering that per person is a bigger thing and is not in this.
  • A send that fails is not retried. It is recorded, as every other send is, so you can see who it did not reach.

Three small things to confirm

1 · The subject line. I would use the report's own name, so it reads in an inbox: New report: Q3 Market Position Review.

2 · Should you get a copy? The office publishes it, so you would know already — my inclination is no copy unless you ask, with the record on the Insights room showing who it went to.

3 · Does a re-publish ever re-send? As drawn, no — once per report, ever. If you correct a report and want people told again, that would be a deliberate Tell them again press rather than something that happens by itself.
