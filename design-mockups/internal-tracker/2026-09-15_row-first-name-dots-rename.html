  1. The owner cell shows the owner's first name, not initials.

What this costs, said before you choose.

Two people with one first name. If two of the office on one client are both called Ahmed, the name alone tells them apart from nobody, so the cell lengthens to two words for exactly that pair — the register's own rule for a clashing short name — and the team list always shows the full name beside the first.

Who gets the press. The name is a button only for the action's owner and the client's Super user, which is who may hand an action on today; everybody else reads the name. With collaborators gone, changing an action's date, status, notes or name is also the owner's and the Super user's — nobody else was ever on it.

Double-click on a tablet. A double-tap on the name does the same in Safari and Chrome, but a finger has no hover to say the name can be renamed; the ⋯ row does not carry a second way in, so the rule stays one thing. Say if you want Rename beside Delete there too.

What is stored. Nothing new. The collaborators field stays in the table holding nothing until a migration drops it in the build; the owner picker writes the same owner the opened row's dropdown wrote.
