Internal Tracker — four frames for sign-off

The office's weekly list for one client, inside that client's platform. The chrome, the views row and the list are the module page's own; only what a list of actions needs is new. There is no form and no separate page: the next empty line takes an action and Enter, and every fact on a row is changed on the row. Read top to bottom: the week, adding a line, one row opened, and a client with nothing on it yet.
