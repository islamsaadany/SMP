What each costs, said before you choose.

Part 1. A adds no word to the screen and reuses a mark that already means notes, so there is nothing new to learn; the cost is that the mark is silent until you hover it. B says note outright but sits in the arrow’s column, so the add line stops lining up with the rows above it. C is the smallest build and the only one where a line that needs a note takes two steps.

Part 2, and this is the one I need your word on. An open action due in a past week is late — that is what late means — so it reads Late, Late 1 w, Late 2 w, the words you asked for. Previous week can only ever appear in place of those, and it would lose the alarm. So unless you want it to replace Late 1 w, the set is: This week · Next week · W40, W41…

The bold goes with the words. This week’s number was bold to mark it among numbers; once the row says This week in words, the bold says it twice, so it comes off the row. It stays in the picker.

The picker. You asked for numbers there, and that stands as option 1. Option 2 is only for keeping one vocabulary: press Next week, the row says Next week. Against it: the first column stops being a column of numbers, and it has to grow from 54 to 86 pixels to hold the two words — which is what made option 1 neat.

What is not affected. What is stored does not move either way — a week is still its Thursday in the same column, so nothing is migrated and Exact dates reads exactly as it does now.
