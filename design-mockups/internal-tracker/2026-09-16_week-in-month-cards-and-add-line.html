On the add line, A gives the typing box the whole row until you start, and the box narrows once as the first letter lands; B never moves anything but leaves the right of the line empty, which is close to what you are looking at today. On the names, B fits the longest first name and no more and keeps a row comfortable to hit on a tablet; C is barely narrower — the width was never the rows' doing — and buys its extra 28px by taking a row to 25px, under every other small control on the page.

One thing to know about the weeks. A week is filed under its Thursday, so a week starting in one month and ending in the next is named after the month it ends in. The days are printed beside it in the picker and in the headings, so nobody has to work that out.
