What makes it busy

  • Four big number cards take a whole band before any action.
  • Views, search, the count and the settings dots sit on three different lines.
  • Every label is in the same small uppercase type, so nothing stands out.
  • Groups are a grey word between rows, so where one topic ends is hard to see.
  • The add line sits at the very bottom, after every row.

What changes

  • One header line. The title and week on the left; the four numbers become one slim bar on the right instead of four big cards.
  • One toolbar. The views as one switch, the search, and the grouping said in words (Group by Topic). The dots keep only Weeks or Exact dates.
  • The add line moves to the top, under the toolbar, so you add without scrolling.
  • Each topic is its own white box with its name, its counts, and a small bar for how much is done.
  • Column names once above the boxes, so every row lines up the same.
  • Late is a thin red edge on the row, plus the red date.
  • Status is a small dot and a word, not a wide bordered pill.
  • Done actions fold under "1 done" at the bottom of their topic.
  • "+ Add to Budget" at the foot of a topic adds an action already in that topic.
