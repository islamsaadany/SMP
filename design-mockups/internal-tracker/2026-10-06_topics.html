What you see

  • Each topic is a heading with its counts: open and, in red, late. Done actions stay under their topic, as they do today.
  • The topic with the nearest due action comes first. No topic is always last.
  • No tag on the rows here, because the heading already says it.
  • The three dots on a heading open the topic's own menu (view 4).

What changes

  • On the add line a Topic tag appears with the week and the owner, once you start typing. You type a few letters and the list narrows; the last topic you used is offered first.
  • If nothing matches, + New topic "…" creates it there and then.
  • On a row, the topic is a quiet tag after the action's name. Pressing it opens the same list to move the action to another topic.
  • No tag means no topic. Nothing is required.

What changes

  • A closed topic still shows, marked Closed, while it has open actions, so nothing gets lost. Once they are all done it disappears from the grouping.
  • Delete only works on a topic nobody uses; otherwise you close it.
