Internal Tracker — editing the topics' names

A Topics panel behind the ⋯ at the top right: one list of every topic on this client, open and closed, including the ones with no actions on them right now. A name is renamed by typing over it. Closing, reopening and deleting use the same three words the topic's own heading menu already uses, so nothing is learnt twice. Renaming a topic reaches every action under it at once — it is one row in the database, not a word typed on each action.

Four things each line says. The name · how many actions hold it and how many of those are done · Closed if it is closed · and the ⋯ for the rest. “no actions yet” is the line that cannot be reached today at all: a topic with nothing on it is never drawn on the page, so it can neither be renamed nor cleared away. The list is ordered the way the picker and the grouped list already order it — open topics first, then by name — so one list is never ordered two ways.

The moment it lands. Enter, and the four tags reading Budget down the list become Budget 2027 on the spot — the list is drawn again by the server, as every other change on this page already is, with no reload and nothing you were typing lost. Escape puts the old name back.

4 · A name another topic already has — refused, never merged quietly

Case does not make a second topic. strategy communication and Strategy communication are one name, so the rename is refused in the server's own words and the box stays open with what you typed still in it. Merging two topics is a decision, not a side effect of a rename — it would move actions nobody chose to move. If you do want them joined, that is a thing to ask for on its own.

Nothing new is invented here. Rename, Close the topic (Reopen on a closed one), Delete behind its question, and the question's own sentence — all four are word for word what the ⋯ on a topic's heading says today. A closed topic still shows while it holds open work, and deleting one keeps its actions: they are the work, the topic was only a heading over it.
