Internal Tracker — the weekly check

Islam: “when we come on our weekly check we mainly have topics with actions for every week, and we make this week's actions done or still in progress or keep it didn't start as is, and we create a new action for next week.”

A topic is a thread that runs for months; a week is the unit of work on it. So the check is read down the thread, topic by topic: what happened last week, what is being marked now, and the line that is written for next week. A fifth view — the four that exist are filters, this one is the meeting.

The weeks are cards (option D, your pick): each week inside a topic is its own box, so nothing can be mistaken for belonging to the wrong week, and this week's is the only one lit. Nothing new is stored — the topic, the due week and the three statuses are already on an action.

Three cards a topic, and the week you are in is the lit one. Last week is a plain white box, this week is ringed in gold with a tinted header and a soft shadow, next week is a dashed outline with no fill — not written yet, said by the box itself rather than by a word. A topic with nothing this week has two cards, not three (Governance & delegation, Office move): the box for a week with nothing in it is never drawn empty.

No second line is invented for the same work. The carry moves the action's week forward; its title, owner, notes and history ride with it, and the row redraws inside the next card down. The alarm is kept, not reset: the tracker remembers the day the action was first due, so the mark goes on climbing — Late 1 w becomes Late 2 w — and the line says what it is carrying under it. A thing pushed four weeks running cannot hide behind a fresh date. That rule is already in the tracker; the carry presses it rather than writing a new one. The carry is offered only on an open line under this week — a done line has nothing to carry, and a line already under next week is where it wants to be.

Cards cost height, and this is the one place to get it back. Last week is read once, at the start of the topic, and then it is in the way for the rest of the meeting. Folded, it keeps its card and its header and says “1 done ›” — press it and it opens. This is a question, not a decision: last week could open folded and you press what you want to see, or open as it is in view 1 and fold what you are finished with. The page already folds done rows under “N done ›”, so neither is a new idea.
