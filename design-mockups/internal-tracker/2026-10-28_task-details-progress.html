Islam: "the notes needs to be a description, and we need to have a progress input box per task so we input what happened in this task on weekly basis", with the task's own history of creation and movement "opened in a different way". Three frames below: the opened task, the closed row, and a row on the Weekly check. Built nowhere yet.

What changed, and what did not.
  • Notes is now Description. Same box, same stored text, a new word and a line saying what it is for. The note typed on the add line lands here, as it does today.
  • Progress is a one-line box with Enter, like the add line. An entry is stamped with who wrote it and the week it was written in (This week, Last week, W42); the exact day and time are on hover, as drawn on the first entry. Newest first.
  • Entries are written, not edited. What was said at a review stands. The writer alone sees a quiet Delete on their own entry, and it asks first (frame 1b). Nobody else can touch it.
  • Writing progress changes nothing else. The status stays what somebody set.
  • History is behind one small link under the progress, where it unfolds in place (frame 1c). It is the automatic record — created, moved, handed on, status changed — and is read far less often than what people wrote.

Once it is the detailed record it should also say when a task was carried to another week, handed to somebody else, renamed or moved to another topic — the movements Islam named. Each line: the day and time, who, what, and what it was before.

A task with no progress yet has no second line (the third row), so the list does not grow where nothing was written. The columns on the right do not move.

On the check, every open row under This week carries its Progress box, right under it, so the entry is typed while the task is being talked about, and Enter adds it without leaving the meeting. The latest entry reads under the name as on the list. Last week's and next week's cards show the latest line but no box: progress is written on the week being reviewed.
  • Not drawn, said instead: a finished task keeps its progress and can still take an entry from its opened panel, so the closing note is written where the task is.
  • Deleting the task deletes its progress with it. Deleting a topic leaves every task and its progress untouched, as today.
