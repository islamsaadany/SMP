What changes

  • Column and phone bar turn white. A 1px grey line on the column's right edge (and under the phone bar) separates it from the page, which is only a shade off white.
  • Words become navy for the tab you are on and your name, and grey for the rest. The headings Work · Team · Knowledge stay small caps in a lighter grey.
  • The tab you are on keeps its amber edge bar and gets a pale navy wash. Its icon uses the darker amber, because the bright amber is too faint on white.

Contrast on white

  • Tab you are on, navy on pale wash: 12.1:1.
  • Other tabs, grey #465268: 7.9:1. Headings #5E6E85: 4.8:1.
  • On-tab icon in dark amber #9C5D08: 5.3:1. The bright amber #F5A623 would be 2.0:1.
  • Dark theme: "white" becomes the dark card colour, so the column matches the cards and the page reads as one surface.

For you to decide

  • Brand weight: with the navy gone, the only Forefront colour left in the frame is the amber logo and the navy buttons. Is that enough?
  • Edge: a hairline (shown) or a soft shadow to lift the column off the page.
  • Phone bar: white to match the column (shown), or keep it navy so the phone still opens on the brand colour.
