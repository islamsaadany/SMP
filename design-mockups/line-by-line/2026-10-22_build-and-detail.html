Line by line, and where the two readings come from

Real screenshots of the build on the demo company shaped like El Abd. Figures are the demo's.

1. The switch

Line by line in the slide view's bar, beside Columns. For the strategy office; one switch for every deck of the company, saved with the company so it works on any laptop. Shown here on.

2. A table, one row per press

The slide arrives with its headings; each press brings the next row; the press after the last row turns the slide. ← takes a row back. Tactics with several outcome lines come in as one; a table continued on the next slide starts again from its first row. Projects tables and cards build the same way; objectives and SWOT slides do not.

3. Where it stands: one reading at a time, with where it comes from

Under each reading, the lines it is worked out from, each with its own figure in its colour. Key measures: each key measure (and each category column, when there is one), whose average is the reading. Execution: each tactic it counts, what it delivered against what was planned, and what it leaves out. Five lines, then "and N more". Directions only; the PowerPoint download keeps the two readings without the lines.
