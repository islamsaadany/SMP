The picker becomes a table

“Make the ‘everyone who reports’ searchable, and make it a simple table with another column of a BU or a FUNC. And make the popup a bit bigger to see more of the units and functions.”

Three asks, drawn two ways in the running dialog on your own tenant's shape. Nothing is built — one decision at the end.

The height is capped at min(52vh, 440px) rather than fixed, so a tall screen shows more and an 800px laptop still has the two buttons on screen — a control below the fold is a control that does nothing.

02Two shapes

Both have the search and the Kind column. They differ only in what the right-hand side is.

Recommended: A. The left is a list of eighteen you look things up in — that is what a table is for. The right is usually two to six rows you have just chosen, and giving it its own header bar and repeated columns spends weight on the half that needs the least. The cost of A is stated: the two halves no longer look alike.

03Three smaller calls

Each changes what gets built. Say nothing and I take the recommendation.

1

A or B

  • Recommend

    A. A table where you search, a list where you order.

2

What the search matches

  • Recommend

    The name and the code. Typing mob finds Mobile and typing MB finds it too — those are the letters now on the pills at the bottom of the deck, so the two halves of the feature answer to the same thing.

  • Or

    The name only.

  • Either way

    Typing never redraws the dialog — it hides rows in place, so nothing moves under your hand and the flow on the right is untouched.

3

The words in the Kind column

  • Yours

    BU and FUNC, as drawn — short enough to keep the column at 56px, with the full words on the hover.

  • Worth knowing

    Everywhere else the product says business unit and supporting function in full. This would be the one screen with the short forms; that is a fair trade in a 56px column, and it is your call rather than mine.

04What it costs to build

Small, and one trap already found in the drawing.

The picker's own markup changes and nothing else does: the list of subjects, what a tick writes, when it saves, the deck it opens and every server rule stay exactly as they are.

The trap: the platform floors every data table at 620px wide so a plan table never squashes. Inside a 520px column that would run straight out of the dialog — so this table is exempted explicitly. Found by drawing it rather than by reading the stylesheet, which is what the drawing is for.

The dialog's extra width is marked on the overlay, so every other dialog in the product keeps the width it has.
