Keeping the day where it is, and putting the calendar one press away. Two readings of what you asked for.

Today the day is a button; pressing it swaps in a date field and the calendar is then behind a small icon inside that field — two presses. Both readings below keep the day showing and open the calendar on the first press. They differ in one thing: whether the day itself is still something you press.
