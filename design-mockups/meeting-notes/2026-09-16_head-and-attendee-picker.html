Meeting Notes — the head and the attendee list

Three refinements, drawn in the page's own stylesheet. Today on the left, the proposal on the right.

1. The title it can already be typed

It is a real box with the placeholder “What was the meeting about?”, drawn with no border and no background at 21px bold — so it reads as a heading the platform wrote. Every other thing on the page carries a small uppercase key above it; the title and the date are the only two without one.

2. The date one press, not two

Pressing the date turns it into a date field, and the browser's calendar is then behind a small icon inside it — so it takes two presses to find. It can open on the first press. Nothing about how it looks changes.

3. The attendee list tick several, then done

Every press in this module is answered by the server drawing the page again, so the list is rebuilt after each tick and disappears with whatever you had typed in its search. It can be carried across the redraw, the way the cursor already is. A tick is saved the moment you make it — the chip appears above as you go — so there is nothing to confirm; what is missing is a way to say you are finished, since a tablet has no Escape key.
