Meeting Notes — the list, a note, the minutes, the email

One meeting, one note: a title, a date, the attendees, and the text you type while it runs. Refine asks the assistant we already have to turn the raw notes into minutes in six parts; you read and edit every part; Send emails the minutes themselves to the attendees, who need no sign-in. Adding to the notes later and sending again is allowed, and the email then says it is an update.

What a still cannot show. Typing saves as you go, like the tracker: no Save button. Refine takes a few seconds; the button reads Refining… and the boxes fill when the assistant answers. If the assistant is not set up on the deployment, the button says so in words and the minutes can still be written by hand. A second Refine after new notes is told to add to the minutes, never rewrite what you edited. Send again after a send is allowed; the subject then opens with Updated minutes, and the history keeps every send with who, when and to whom.

Two decisions drawn in. A casual attendee (dashed chip) is added by name and email for this one meeting and does not go onto the client's register. A register person with no email stays an attendee and is marked; the minutes go to everybody who has an address, with a copy to whoever pressed Send.

Not drawn, said instead. The question marks in the minutes are the assistant marking a point it could not settle rather than guessing; they are yours to answer or delete before sending, and the email carries whatever the boxes hold.
