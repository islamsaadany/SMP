7

Scale to 0.97 on press

tips 4 and 14
Before no press state

Press and hold any button. Nothing acknowledges the press until the click lands.

After held: .97 at once · release: eases back 120ms

Press and hold. The button sinks the moment it is held, with no tween on the way down, and eases back when released.

Every "After" animation is switched off when the device asks for reduced motion, which is the rule the platform already follows for all its animations.
