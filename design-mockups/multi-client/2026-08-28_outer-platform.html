The Forefront platform

One door for Forefront's consultants, a card for every client, and inside a card the platform exactly as it is today — named after that client and holding only that client's data. Six screens, drawn from the decisions taken on 28 August. Nothing here is built.

  • Demo is a client, not a button. It sits here, holds a renamed copy of the worked example, and — unlike every demo the product has had — it saves, so it can be added to between pitches. The Demo data button inside the platform goes.
  • The card says what you would ask. Industry, whether a cycle is open or there is no plan yet, whether the client is yours, how many units. Nothing that needs a second look to understand.

Everyone on this list holds SMO team inside Raya Trade. The super user also holds the access matrix, retiring people, and issuing passwords — and may be a person on the client's own register instead, when you decide.

  • The client's own roles are untouched — unit owner, strategy custodian, function head, contributor are theirs, granted inside their client, exactly as now.
  • It lands in two steps. First the seats are read-only here, set from the configuration. Then, once that is proven on a live client, the office seats come out of Roles & access entirely.

Visible to Raya on day one. Their Super user seat is a row on their own register today. After step one it reads as set from outside and can no longer be changed there. Nobody loses anything — but it should be you who tells them, not them finding it.
