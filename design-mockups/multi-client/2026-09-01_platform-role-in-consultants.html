Where the platform role sits

One row, three ways. Islam: “I’m the super user of the platform, I need to have this role in the consultants.” The role is already on that row today — under a different word, and next to a column that uses your word for something else.

The collision to avoid

Super user already means the top seat on a client (§89), and the column beside this one says “Raya Trade · Super user”. So the platform’s role has to be qualified by where it is held, or one screen carries two meanings for one word — §87’s twins.

Two things I’d fix whichever you pick

Your name. The account the platform creates for itself was called “Forefront”; it reads the name off the address instead, so islam.saadany@ becomes Islam Saadany and no person is written into the code. And your own row cannot change your own standing — that stays true in all three, which is why the chip on your row is drawn quiet: somebody else with the platform role has to do it, so nobody can lock the platform’s last admin out of it.
