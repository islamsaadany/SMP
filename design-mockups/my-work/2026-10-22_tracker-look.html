Ticking a row, or choosing Done, finishes the action in that client’s Tracker, and the counts in the panel drop at once. The row stays where it is, green, until you next open My work; then it leaves the list, as a ticked row does in the Tracker. Unticking puts it back as it was.

On a colleague’s list (the Show picker), you can change only the rows on clients where you are Super user, the Tracker’s own rule. Their other rows show the tick and the status without letting you press them.
