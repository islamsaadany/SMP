What the new words do differently

No “server”, no “data” as a technical word. The heading reassures instead of alarming — and with the fix, “just a moment” is usually literally true: the page appears by itself seconds later.

The three facts a user needs are all still said, in their language: this is the example and not your information, nothing you saved is touched, and you don’t need to do anything.
