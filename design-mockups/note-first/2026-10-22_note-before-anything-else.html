A red figure gets its note before anything else

Today

Mobile market share is entered at 18% against 32% due. The row turns red, but every other box stays open. Only Submit waits for the note.

Proposed
  • The bar at the top names the row and Go to it takes you there. It replaces the "to finish" bar while it shows.
  • Every other box is greyed out and won't take typing.
  • The red row's note box is ringed and ready to type in. Its figure stays open in case it was a typo; fix the figure out of the red and the lock lifts too.
  • Write the note and the page opens again.

Everywhere it applies

  • Every Reporting page: units, functions, capabilities and the company, and My reporting.
  • On My reporting the tactic owner writes the note on their own red rows.
  • If a page opens with red figures that already have no note, the bar names them one at a time, and they come first.
  • Save draft and Close still work while it is locked.
