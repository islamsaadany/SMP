The gold buttons in dark

Three buttons in Meeting Notes are hard to read when the platform is in dark mode — including the one that actually sends the minutes.

The two panels below are the real page, in its own colours. Nothing here is a redrawing: the buttons, the search box and the grey count are copied out of the source.

Why it happened

The gold has to get brighter for dark mode, or it would disappear against the dark page. When it was brightened, the white word on it was never changed to follow — so the brighter the button got, the fainter its own label became.

It is the same gold everywhere else on the page — a border, a tick, a line of text — and those are all fine. Only the three filled buttons have it.

What changes, if you say yes

One line. The word on those three buttons stops being white in dark mode and takes the colour of the page behind them. Light mode is untouched. No other screen in the platform has this.

−  .btn.gold { color: #FFF }                      white, in both themes
+  .btn.gold { color: #FFF }                      light: unchanged
+  dark: .btn.gold { color: var(--ground) }       the page's own dark
