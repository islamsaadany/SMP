Being told a message arrived

A box from the computer when a reply lands while the platform is open. Two wordings to choose between, and where the switch would live.

Where the switch lives

Chat settings, beside the away email the real panel

Told while you are here, and told while you are away — the two sit together. One company-wide switch; each person is then asked once by their own browser and can refuse.

How it behaves

  • Only while the platform is open in a tab. This is not a phone notification — it does not reach somebody who has closed the platform. That is what the away email is for, and the two are complementary rather than alternatives.
  • Each person is asked once, by their browser, not by us. The platform asks the moment they open their messages, not on arrival — a permission box thrown at somebody who has not asked for anything is the one people refuse out of reflex.
  • A refusal is theirs to undo, not yours. Once somebody has said no, only they can change it, in their browser’s own settings. We cannot ask twice, and the platform should say so rather than appearing broken.
  • Clicking the box brings them to the conversation.
  • Nothing new leaves the platform. No third party, no subscription, no message content sent anywhere it does not already go.
iPhone and iPad Safari only allows this for a site that has been added to the home screen. The platform already supports that, but somebody using it in an ordinary Safari tab on an iPhone will get nothing — and nothing on the screen would explain why unless we say so.
One thing to decide beyond the wording Whether a person can turn it off for themselves inside the platform, or whether the only off-switch is their browser’s. Their browser can always refuse; the question is whether we offer a quieter way that does not require them to go digging in settings.
