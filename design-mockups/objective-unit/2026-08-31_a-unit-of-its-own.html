A unit for a key objective

You asked for one because some objectives are counts, some are money and some are square metres. Here is what the plan holds today, measured — not guessed — across all 90 key‑objective targets that ship with the platform.

  • 1There is no unit field. The unit is typed into the target itself, as text: "6.2B EGP", "60000 SQM", "100#". The platform stores one string and has no idea which part is the number.
  • 2Which is why the actual has no unit. Your screenshot shows 100# against a bare 70 — not a bug, just nobody retyping the unit into the second box.
  • 3Your upload template already has a Unit column, and the platform glues it onto the number on the way in and pulls it apart again on the way out. So the concept exists and is flattened at the door.

My recommendation: A. The unit is a fact about the numbers, not about the name, and putting it in its own column is what makes a column of targets readable down the page for the first time.

Three things I need from you before building either:

1 · The 90 targets that already exist. The platform can already split "6.2B EGP" into 6.2 and B EGP correctly — it does it on every download. Do I run that once over the stored plan so existing rows get their unit filled in, or leave them exactly as typed and let the office set units going forward? Splitting is reversible and I would test it against every one of the 90 first.

2 · Free text, or a list? A list keeps it tidy and somebody has to maintain it; free text handles SQM, trips, orders, days without anybody being asked. I would suggest free text with the tenant's own existing units offered as suggestions — no maintenance, no dead end.

3 · Does this reach the pillar measures too? They have exactly the same shape and the same problem. Doing both together is barely more work than doing one; doing one leaves the platform saying it two ways.
