Starting a conversation

Platform Inbox · awaiting sign-off

Today the office can only ever answer. If nobody has written in, there is no way to reach them from the Inbox at all. This adds the other direction — and it needs one decision from you: where the control sits. Everything else follows from a rule the chat already has.

The decision

A · Beside the search

Recommended

In the column it acts on. The list of conversations is right below it, and a new one appears there the moment it is sent.

What it costs: the search box gives up about 60 pixels. On a narrow window that is the difference between seeing a full name in the search field and not.

B · On the page header

Where every other Setup page puts its buttons, beside the page name. It is the house standard, and it stays put when the list scrolls.

What it costs: it sits above the split, so it reads as belonging to the whole page rather than to the list of people — and it is the furthest point on the screen from the list it adds to.

What it opens into

Either button opens the same thing, on the right where a conversation normally reads:

  1. To searches the register — the same picker the plan uses for owners, so it behaves the way the rest of the platform behaves.
  2. Send writes the first message and opens their conversation, which now appears in the list under Answered — it is not waiting on you.
  3. They get it three ways: in the corner next time they are on the platform, as a box on their screen if notifications are on, and as an email if they are away.

Settled by what already exists

These are not open questions

  • One conversation per person. Picking somebody who has already written in simply opens the conversation you already have. This can never make a second one — the platform does not allow it.
  • It is the office's alone. The Inbox is already closed to everybody else, so nothing new is being opened up.
  • Nothing new is built underneath. A message the office starts is the same act as a reply, so it inherits the rules a reply already has: the email chase, the notification, and the conversation leaving the waiting list.
Worth saying out loud: somebody who is not on the platform that day will receive an email. That is what a reply already does, and it is the right behaviour — but it means starting a conversation is not a quiet act.

A or B? Once you pick, I build it — and it doubles as the clean way to test notifications, without leaving a stray conversation behind.
