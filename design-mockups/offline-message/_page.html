Say “you are offline” when you are offline

Every banner below is the platform’s own, shot out of the built file with the words written into the live element. Nothing here is drawn.

What you see today

The browser’s words, not the platform’s

The exact banner you photographed, reproduced at a 1000 px window.

  • It does not say the one thing you need. You are offline, and the sentence talks about the server — which sends you to look at something that is working perfectly.
  • “Failed to fetch” is the browser talking. It is not a status number anybody can act on, and it names nothing.
  • The advice is backwards while you are offline. It tells you to reload, and reloading is exactly what would throw the work away.
  • The two sentences run into each other. …(Failed to fetch).Your change… — no space, on every failure the platform has ever reported, not only this one.
Option A · recommended

Plain words, and the advice reversed

The voice you settled for the slow-start message: no “server”, no “database”, short lines.

Why this one. The heading answers the question before you read a word of the second line, and the second line is the whole of what to do — which is nothing. It is also the only version that is true for somebody who does not know what a server is.

Option B

The banner’s existing voice, made honest

Same fact, keeping the words the other two failures use.

What it costs. Two lines that say the same thing twice, and it still ends on the word database — which is what you called too technical last time.

The other two ways a save fails

A save fails three ways and only one of them is being offline. They are shown together because a person meets one at a time and has to be able to tell them apart.

Online, and the server did not answer. The browser’s “Failed to fetch” goes; it moves to the console, where the one person who can act on it looks.

The server answered with an error. Unchanged except for the missing space. The number stays deliberately — it is what sends somebody to the right place.

The dark palette

Both banners in the dark palette, because the colours are themed and a sentence measured in one is not measured in the other.

Today, dark.

Option A, dark. Same tokens as the light one, so nothing new was picked and nothing needs measuring.

What this does not change

  • Nothing about saving. Only the sentence on the banner. No change to the data, the server, or what is stored.
  • The banner still clears itself. The platform retries every five seconds while the tab is open, and a save that lands hides it — so when you come back online it goes away on its own, within about five seconds. Nothing to press.
  • The work is genuinely waiting, not lost. Measured, not assumed: the retry runs for as long as the tab stays open. Closing the tab while offline still loses it, which is why the banner says not to reload.
  • The chat corner is left alone. It has the same blind spot in its own message. You named the save banner, so it is flagged here rather than widened quietly.

What I need from you

A or B, and whether the run-together sentences should be split on all three failures or only on the offline one.

Then it is one small function in the save code, an offline case added to the check that already drives the other failure states, and proof that it fails on today’s build first.
