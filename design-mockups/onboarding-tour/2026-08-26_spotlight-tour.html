Onboarding tour — dim, spotlight, story

2026-08-26 · not the product

What is proposed — rev 3 after review

First sign-in opens a guided tour on demo data: the page dims, what matters stays lit, a short card says what it is. Look steps advance with Next; do steps have no Next — the person presses the lit control itself. Every card carries ‹ Back, the step count, Skip tour and ×.

Changed from rev 2: on navigation steps the spotlight is the one button that says where you are (Mobile, then the Strategy tab), not the whole row — and the dim can now hold two spotlights at once, so each strategy section lights its own section button AND its content together while everything else dims. Presentation is now a do-step: pressing it really opens its menu, and the next step explains Present and Manage slides inside it. 10 steps.

Costs, stated

  • The tour opens demo mode itself — the Demo data button is the SMO's, so the tour cannot send people to it. Demo writes nothing, which is what makes letting a first-time user press real controls safe.
  • Do-steps rot silently when a control is renamed — so a check walks every story as every role and asserts each target is present and hittable, or the build fails.
  • "Seen it" is per browser (localStorage) — a new device replays the tour once. Replay lives in the Knowledge base.
  • In this replica the controls sit where the built product puts them; in the real build the tour anchors to the real controls, so their place is right by construction.
Check: press through all 10 steps · use Back, including from a do-step · press the lit controls on steps 7–9 · try Skip and × · resize mid-step.
