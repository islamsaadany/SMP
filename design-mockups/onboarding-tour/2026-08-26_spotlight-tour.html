Onboarding tour — dim, spotlight, story

2026-08-26 · not the product

What is proposed

First sign-in opens a guided tour on demo data: the page dims, one control stays lit, a short card says what it is. Two kinds of step — look steps advance with Next; do steps have no Next: the person presses the lit control itself, and that press is what advances the tour. Every card carries the step count, Skip tour and ×. The demo banner stays on screen the whole time, so nobody mistakes whose figures they are looking at.

Below is a working sample of the strategy custodian's story, 7 steps, on a replica of the platform. Steps 5 and 6 are do‑steps. The BU/function owner's story shares the engine with different steps.

Costs, stated

  • The tour opens demo mode itself — the Demo data button is the SMO's, so the tour cannot send people to it. Demo writes nothing, which is what makes letting a first-time user press real controls safe.
  • Do-steps rot silently when a control is renamed — so a check walks every story as every role and asserts each target is present and hittable, or the build fails.
  • "Seen it" is per browser (localStorage) — a new device replays the tour once. Replay lives in the Knowledge base.
  • The overlay lives outside the repainted region and re-anchors after every repaint, scroll and resize.
Check: press through all 7 steps · press the lit controls on steps 5–6 · try Skip and × · resize mid-step.
