Who answers for each direction

When the company plans its own directions, each direction gets an owner and a custodian. These pictures are the real company page, with the change drawn in.

How it works (agreed)

  • Each direction has an owner and a custodian, picked from the register.
  • Both read the whole company plan, and fill only their own direction.
  • Each owner submits their own direction's report.
  • Where nobody is named, the Strategy office fills it.
  • It works the same whether the company plans alone or beside business units.
