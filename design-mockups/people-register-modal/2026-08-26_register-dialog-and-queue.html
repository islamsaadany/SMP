6 — the top panel

Three rows become one

What went, and one thing I did not remove. “76 rows” and the five filter chips are gone as you asked. The two counts said the same thing twice, so one quiet 76 active sits beside the title. I kept the search box — with the filters gone it is the only way to find a row by hand, and it is the one control on that row that costs nothing.

The cost of dropping the filters, stated once. No password and No email were how you found those people in one press. The attention queue covers them only if we put them in it — so I would, and then nothing is lost. If you would rather the queue stayed strictly about declarations, say so and those two become findable only by eye.

3 — the queue

The button knows which lines. You never have to.

Press Needs attention and the first person opens with what is pending stated at the top. Fix it, Save & next, and the following one takes its place in the same dialog — the register never moves underneath you.

What feeds it. The three the rail already counts, plus the two the filters used to find: a declaration to accept, somebody never issued a password, somebody with no identifier at all (§87), a duplicate to merge, and no email. One of the rail’s three cannot join — “units with no custodian” is about units, not people, so it stays its own line and names the units.
