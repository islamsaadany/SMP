People register › Roles. Why the pick does nothing, and the one line that fixes it. Both pictures are the real platform, same build.

What is happening

The second question is asked underneath the Email box

The picker is two halves — the role in the Roles column, Choose where… in the Unit column, which is how you asked for it (§69). The Unit cell is one line and 158px wide, so the second control is laid out beside the first rather than under it, runs 133px past the edge of its own cell, and lands beneath the Email field. Email takes every click. The role is never granted, and nothing says so.

It only bites the roles that have a real choice — business unit owner, strategy custodian, company CEO, function head. Super user, SMO team and Group CEO are granted the moment the role is picked (§92), so they never draw a second half and have always worked. That is exactly the difference you have been seeing.

The change

One wrapper and one rule

The picker’s second half is wrapped in an element of its own, and that element is given a line: display:block; white-space:normal, inside an open row only. Nothing else in the register is touched — not the columns, not the read rows, not §69’s split of the picker across two columns.

And it gets a check that presses the control rather than looking for it in the document (§70, §93.4). Every existing check would have gone green on this the whole time: the select is present, enabled and correctly positioned. It is simply underneath something.

Two more things found on the way

  • A retired person can still be given a role. The unit is written to point at them, the row goes on saying No role because a retired person holds nothing, and nothing is said. You have already said: refuse it, and say why.
  • Cancel half-undoes a grant. Grant the role, press Cancel on the row: the role stays on the unit, the person’s own unit reverts. They read as owner of Nigeria while sitting at the group.
