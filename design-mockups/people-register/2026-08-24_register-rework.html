01
Roles & access is read-only for them Editing the matrix is editing who may edit the matrix. Without this line, the role is Super user with extra steps.
02
No deleting, no clearing Retiring a person, unit, function or company — yes. Deleting one, clearing a plan, clearing the tenant, restoring the demo — no. Those are irreversible at tenant scale.
03
Passwords: the client’s, never their own kind They may set and reset a password for anyone on the client’s side. They may not touch a Super user’s, and not another SMO team member’s — including their own colleagues. Enforced on the server, not by hiding the button.

Left to the Super user alone, as a consequence: the access matrix, every delete and clear, and passwords for the SMO’s own people. Everything else the SMO team can do — which is the point of the role.
