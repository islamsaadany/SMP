That's a very wide design that even require a scroll — make it compact with no need to scroll.

Two things are spending that height: the Unit row leaves the cell beside it empty, and Sign-in name carries a two-line paragraph.

The three changes behind those numbers

  • Three columns instead of two. Six rows become four. Nothing is removed and nothing moves section — the same fields in the same two groups, three across instead of two.
  • The Unit row no longer orphans a cell. With three columns, Job title, Official BU and Unit are one row, so nothing is left standing beside a gap.
  • The sign-in explanation moves to the hover. The paragraph is the tallest single thing in the dialog and it says the same sentence every time you open anybody. The name still shows, greyed and in a dashed box so it reads as not-editable; the reason is on hovering it.

What this costs

A 264px field is narrower than a 434px one, so a long email — islam.saadany@forefront.consulting — fills its box instead of sitting comfortably inside it. It is still fully editable and scrolls within the field, which is ordinary for a text input, but it is a real loss of comfort on the two longest fields. If you would rather keep the email roomy, say so and Email spans two columns — that costs about 56px of the 197px saved, and still never scrolls.

What happens next

Say yes, or say what to change, and I will build both against the sources, run the suite, and put the result on the branch. Two questions are genuinely open: the no-custodian chip in part one, and whether Email should span two columns in part two. Everything else follows your words.
