Plan titles you can read while you edit them

Islam: wrap the content of the plans edit boxes across pillars and functions, specially for the titles and descriptions, as they become hard to read when the lines get long.

What is actually wrong

It is not that the boxes wrap badly. They cannot wrap at all. Every title and description on a plan — the pillar name, the objective, the measure, the tactic, the milestone, the sub-line — is a <input>, and an input is a single line by definition. Only the project Brief is a real text box, and even that is fixed at two rows.

So a long title is not shortened or wrapped; it simply runs past the end of the box and you scroll sideways inside it to read your own words.

The change

  • Titles and descriptions become boxes that grow to fit — however many lines the text needs, while the pen is on. Nothing scrolls inside them.
  • Short fields are left alone. Targets, dates, codes and directions stay exactly as they are — they are one value, and giving them room to wrap would only make the rows taller for nothing.
  • Reading mode does not change at all. This is only what you see with the pen open.
  • Units and functions together, because both draw through the same builder — there is no version of this that reaches one and not the other.

What it costs

  • Rows get taller while you are editing. A plan with many long titles is a longer page with the pen on. That is the trade: you can read them.
  • The tables stay the same width. Nothing moves sideways, so no column shifts and nothing new scrolls.
  • The pen closes and the page goes back to its usual height.
