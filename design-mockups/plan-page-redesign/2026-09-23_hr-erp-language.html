What changes

  • Destinations move to a navy side menu, as in HR ERP. The top loses two of its four rows.
  • Page and pillar titles use a serif (Fraunces) at 30px and 22px, so the eye knows where to start.
  • Five text sizes in total (12, 14, 16, 22, 30). Today the platform uses about 25.
  • Four spacing steps and three roundings, used everywhere, on a warm off-white page.
  • Pillars are cards with code, kind and counts. The open pillar has a gold edge.
  • Quarters are four small boxes. Navy means planned; the gold ring is the current quarter.

What stays

  • Every word, figure and control on today's page: the tabs, Foundation · SWOT · Drivers · Plan, Edit, the two tables and their columns.
  • The navy table headers and the navy/gold palette.
  • The viewer switch, now at the top right.

Decisions for you

  • Side menu or top bar? The side menu is HR ERP's biggest change and the most work: it touches every page.
  • The serif for headings: keep Fraunces, as in HR ERP, or use a different one?
  • Scope: this Plan page first as a test, or the rules for the whole platform at once?
