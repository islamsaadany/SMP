What changed from round 1

  • Title font: Fraunces is replaced by Source Serif 4. Its & is plain; two other options are above.
  • Navigation stays horizontal: the top line, then the navy destination row with Group, Units | Capabilities | Functions, the units and the gear, as today.
  • Colours are SMP's own house navy #16325C and gold #C9A24D, on today's grey page, not HR ERP's warm paper.
  • The pillar rail keeps its navy header, with four cards of equal height under it. The chosen one is tinted gold with a gold edge.
  • Quarters use today's exact colours: dashed for not planned, gold tint for a planned quarter that has passed, cream with navy for one still ahead.
  • Table lines stay visible: row and column lines, an outer border and the zebra stripe.

What is kept from round 1

  • Five text sizes, four spacing steps, three roundings.
  • A serif page title, "Mobile", with the unit type and cycle above it.
  • The pillar shown as one card holding both tables.
