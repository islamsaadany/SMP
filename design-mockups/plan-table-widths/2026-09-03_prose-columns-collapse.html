The plan table's squeeze

With the pen open, the columns that hold words give up their width to the columns that hold controls. Below about 1,300 px a tactic's title wraps one character per line. Every figure and every picture below comes from the real page with clean values — no line breaks anywhere.

01What is actually happening

The control columns hold about 600 px of near-fixed width — Target 224, Owner 155, Collabs. 92, Quarters 132 — in a table that has only 762–1,085 px to spend. The prose columns absorb the entire shortfall, and the growing box (which is sized to what is in it) then reports a height for a column two characters wide.

02Three ways the width can come back

All three put a floor under the two prose columns (200 px for the tactic, 140 for the outcome) so they stop absorbing the shortfall. They differ in where the width comes from, and that is the decision.

The four boxes in the Target cell drop from 96 px to 76. Nothing moves, nothing folds — the cell simply asks for less.

03What I would do, and why

C, with B underneath it. C is the only one that keeps a title on one line at 1,200 and the only one where the row stops growing at all — 208 px against today's 1,957. B is the honest fallback for the middle widths, where the tail still fits and only the target block needs to give way. A is the weakest: it buys 27 px and still runs past the pane at 1,200.

Both keep every control reachable, which is the rule that governs this table — the four target boxes are the only way to set a target, so they may narrow or stack but never disappear while the pen is open.

Two things to decide before I build:

  1. Where the tail goes at the narrow end. In C the collaborators picker and the four quarter marks sit under the name, as a strip. They lose their column headings there, so they would need naming in place — a short label before each, or a line of their own.
  2. Where the switch happens. At 1,300 the table is still comfortable in B and generous in C. I would fold the tail only below 1,280 and narrow the target block between 1,280 and 1,400, so a full-size window never changes shape.
