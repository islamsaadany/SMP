What is on your screen

Measured by building your exact shape and reading every row back out of the database, rather than by looking at the picture.

  • WP1, WP2 and AC1 are activities, not work packages. All three were typed into the phase's box with + ACTIVITY lit — which is what that box does when nothing is pressed.
  • That is why there is nowhere to write an activity under them. An activity holds nothing, so no box is drawn beneath one. The box you do have, under 1.3, is the phase's.
  • An activity hung straight off a phase is numbered 1.1 — the same shape a work package gets — because the numbering closes up a level that was skipped. On screen it sits three pixels further in, in a lighter weight. Nothing on the row says which it is.
  • And the screen still offers you a work package on that phase. Press it and the plan holds two rows both numbered 1.1, and the phase reads 0% done while counting 1 of 3 activities finished. Measured, not reasoned.

What it costs

A phase can no longer hold a work package and a loose activity. That is the product's own existing rule, finished — but it is a rule, so it is worth saying out loud before it is built.

A plan already holding a mixed phase keeps it. Nothing is rewritten, nothing is migrated, nothing moves in the database on any of the three.

The kind word appears on every line of a long plan. It is the quietest thing on the row, and it is the only thing that answers the question after the fact.

What I would do

C. A and B each answer half the question — A before you type, B afterwards — and neither stops a plan ending up with two rows numbered 1.1, which is the fault underneath the one you reported. C is the two of them plus a guard the product has already written once, pointing the other way.

Flagged, and not proposed here: there is no way to change a row's kind. Your WP1 and WP2 can only be removed and typed again as work packages — a conversion would move the row between two tables and renumber what follows it, so it is a decision of its own rather than something to ride in beside this.
