What the three roles reach

  • Lead — builds and restructures the plan, and signs off completions. Whoever does the work marks an activity Done; only a Lead marks it Completed and sets the real end date.
  • Contributor — sees the plan, reports the activities assigned to them, and comments on the ones they are tagged on. Whether they report or only comment is read off the activity, not from this table.
  • Viewer — reads, changes nothing. Where everybody starts.

Two behaviours worth pressing

  • Ticks land as you make them. There is no Done button — the platform's ticking lists commit each tick and stay open until you click away, so somebody is on the project the moment you tick them. Untick to take them off again.
  • Taking somebody off who holds activities is refused, by name. Press the × beside Karim. Unassigned activities in a live plan are exactly what nobody notices until a checkpoint, so the platform says what is in the way instead of letting it happen.
