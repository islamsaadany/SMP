Portfolio — who is on a project

The six levels ScopePlan already has, kept. What changes is only where each one is answered: two of them are about a whole client, four are about one project.

This replaces yesterday's drawing, which was wrong. That one put Strategy's company roles — BU owner, Strategy custodian, Function head — down the side of a Portfolio tab. A function head is not a project lead, and being a BU owner says nothing about whether you are on a piece of delivery work. Membership belongs to the project, not to the org chart, which is what ScopePlan already does and what this keeps.

The six levels, and where each is answered

Nothing is invented and nothing is dropped. Two are about the whole client; four are about one project. Two are renamed, and only because scope no longer exists and client lead already means the client's own SMO in SMP.

What this costs. Somebody on four projects is named four times — which is the honest price of membership belonging to the work. What it buys: a person's reach on one project says nothing about another, and taking them off a project is one row rather than a change to what their company role means.
