04Pausing it

You asked for pause in the bottom bar beside reset. Both are built — but the bar is not on the screen in fullscreen, which is the same obstacle that put the clock on the slide. So the bar is where you pause while rehearsing, and there are two ways that reach you while actually presenting.

The bar, windowed

Mobile · 1 of 9 · Q3 2026 Review 13 / 28

Laptop, fullscreenThe P key

The deck already answers F for fullscreen and W for fit, so a letter is the existing idiom rather than a new one. P is free — measured.

Tablet, fullscreenTap the clock

A tablet has no P key, and you present from one. The deck already knows not to advance a slide when a button is pressed, so the clock can be that button.

Deliberately bar-onlyReset is not either

Pause is reversible; reset throws the elapsed time away. A stray tap that pauses costs a press. A stray tap that resets cannot be undone.

And the clock has to say it is paused
