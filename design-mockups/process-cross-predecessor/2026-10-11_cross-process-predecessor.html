“Ready”: steps have no done status in this module, so I read it as “documented”: a step whose only link is a cross-process predecessor no longer shows no predecessor. If you meant something live (blocked until done), that needs a status on steps and is a bigger change. Say so and I will draw it.

What does not change
Deleting the source step

The link is removed, never the step that depended on it. Same rule as a branch today.

Loops

A chain that comes back to itself across processes is refused when you save, with the path named.

Numbering and layout

Step numbers and lanes in HR-02 are unaffected. The other step sits in the gutter and is not numbered here.
