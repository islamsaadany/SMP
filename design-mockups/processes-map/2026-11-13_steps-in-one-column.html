Steps that happen together, drawn in one column

These are real pictures of the Map from the built app, not a drawing. The option is off by default, so every existing map stays exactly as it is.

What it is: a tick on the step's form, "Draw it beside the step before it". The step moves into the same column as the step before it, in its own lane, so two things that happen at the same time line up. Nothing else about the step changes.

What I checked so it does not touch the look

  • Option unused: on six processes, including one with 24 steps, loops and skip arrows, every arrow route and every box position is identical to before.
  • Option used: the steps share a column, each stays in its own lane, and the arrows between them are short and straight.
  • Two steps in one lane cannot be put in one column; the tick is greyed out.
  • Readers see the same drawing as you do, so what you align is what they read.

Not changed: the exported PDF of the Map has its own layout and is not affected by this option.
