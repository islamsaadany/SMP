Ways in

  • Processes in the top bar. An office seat lands in the builder, on All processes, with To handle as the second tab; a client’s own person lands on My processes. The seat decides, so there is no switch to forget. SMP works this way today.
  • From any process page. Office seats see “Open in builder” at the top right; staff never do.
  • From a suggestion or a returned approval. The button opens the builder on the right process, with a draft started and the step selected.

Way back out

  • Preview as manager shows the draft exactly as the owner will read it, under a gold strip with “Back to the builder”
  • Open the staff view on the builder home opens Processes as client staff see it today

Not chosen

  • A Read / Build switch in the top bar for office seats: one more thing to forget, and the seat already says who builds
  • A second “Process builder” entry in the top bar: two doors to one module, and client staff would ask why they cannot open it
