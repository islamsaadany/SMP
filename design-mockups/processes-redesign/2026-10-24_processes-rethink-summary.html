On every page

What holds everywhere

  • SMP’s look, not FFProcess’s: settled when Islam asked for navy and gold on 23 Oct; it reverses the 1 Oct “keep its design”.
  • The navy band is the Internal Tracker’s masthead: a gold path, a white title, one line of description, count tiles on the right.
  • Actions sit at the top right of the band: Download SOP, Print, Suggest a change, New process, Send for approval. The main one is gold.
  • Tabs are navy buttons under the band, with counts; a gold count marks what is waiting.
  • The tab bar stays in place as you scroll.
  • No title above a section. The highlighted tab says where you are, and the section’s one-line description takes the band’s subtitle.
  • Tables alternate white then light grey under a slightly darker header, with no row hover; long ones can be searched and sorted by any column.
Reading side

What a manager sees

Opening the module: My processes

  • Yours: the processes they own or approve, with their part, where each is, and what needs attention, such as a risk with no control, who has read it, or the review date.
  • You have a step in: processes someone else owns where their role does part of the work.
  • Every row carries its own button: Open and approve, Review by 12 Nov, Read the change, Reply to the office.
  • To do is the second tab, with a gold count: what to approve, review and read, and the office’s answers to their suggestions. A “Waiting on you” line in the band opens it. Acting in a row or on To do clears it in both.
  • Map: every process by area, theirs in gold, the ones they have a step in dashed.

A process

  • Opens on Flow: lanes per role, arrows, decisions; click a step for its detail. Steps and Summary are tabs. On a phone it opens on Steps.
  • Summary answers “is it in good shape?”: approval, risks with no control, who has not read it, a missing SOP version, and what is waiting on them.
  • Suggest a change works on every tab, pinned to a step. It goes to the Strategy Office, and the answer comes back on the page and in To do.

Builder

What the Strategy Office sees

Ways in, and back out

  • Processes in the top bar lands an office seat on the builder home.
  • Open in builder sits at the top right of any process page, for office seats only.
  • Start a draft from this on a suggestion opens the builder on that process, with the draft started and the step selected.
  • Preview as manager shows the draft exactly as the owner will read it, under a gold strip with Back to the builder.

The builder home

  • Opens on All processes: every process with its owner, where it is and its gaps, searchable, and sortable by any heading. “Where it is” sorts what needs the office first.
  • To handle is second: owners’ suggestions, drafts the approver sent back, processes with gaps.
  • Landscape (today’s Value chain and Helicopter, merged), Priorities, Governance, Org & roles, Export, Settings. Today’s Dashboard goes.
  • On a section, its numbers sit in boxes under the tabs, and its own views stay under the tab bar as you scroll (on a phone only the tabs stay).

Building one process

  • A menu on the left: Map · RACI · Authority · Risks & controls · Quality · Lifecycle, each with a status dot.
  • The map is edited in place: add a step or a decision, connect steps; the selected step opens as a form with who does it, what they do, who is involved, approval, time limit, risk and control. Gaps show as red marks.
  • When the draft came from a suggestion, the owner’s words sit at the top of the step.

Still open

For the team to settle before the build plan

Four questions from the 23 Oct prototype. Each is drawn on the builder mockup with its answers to try; the recommended answer is shown first.

Open · 1The three empty tabs on a process

People, Compliance and Connections were signed off on 14 Oct but hold nothing and offer no way to fill them. Recommended: off for now, each back when it has content.

Open · 2Readiness marks on every process, or on deep dives only

Map, RACI, Authority, Controls, SOP, Approved. Recommended: on every process; a high-level one shows mostly empty marks, which is true.

Open · 3Moving to another process inside the builder

Back to the list, a picker on the process name in the band, or a list beside the section menu. Recommended: the picker, because you stay on the same section with no page in between.

Open · 4Check with AI as a panel or as its own section

Recommended: a panel beside whatever section is open, from a button in the band.

LaterOwners editing their own drafts, and the table header across SMP

With a separate builder, owners need a limited way into it or a lighter editor; nothing here blocks either. The darker table header stays in Processes until Islam decides about the rest of SMP.

Not drawn yetThree screens

The reading side’s All processes list; what frontline staff with no ownership see; the approver’s full approval screen.
