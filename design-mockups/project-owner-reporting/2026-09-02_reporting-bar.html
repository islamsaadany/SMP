Today

What is not changing

A project owner still does not submit, write the cycle note, or add picture slides — each speaks for the whole function, and one project’s owner cannot speak for the others. The rows he reaches, and the rows he does not, are exactly as they are today.

Why the control is not in the bar

The mockup could not show the case that forced it

A supporting function draws every capability at once, each with its own project rail. One control in the bar would have to guess which project it meant — and somebody owning projects in two capabilities would find one of them unreachable. In the project’s own band there is nothing to guess.

It also follows a rule you set generally: the control that answers a thing sits under the box it is about. Say the word if you would rather have it back in the bar and I will draw the single-project case that way.

Also worth knowing: the finished mark is a signal, not a lock — a marked project still takes figures until the report itself is closed. And the rail does not show the mark yet: the custodian sees it on the band as they scroll the panes. Putting it on the rail too is a second surface for one fact, so it wants its own drawing rather than a quiet widening.
