What was measured

  • View onlywhat the bar above all of it says
The bar says

View only. No Submit. No Save draft. Nothing that acknowledges anything was entered.

The page does

Takes his figures on his own project, saves them as he types, and shows the tally moving.

Today

NowThe pill is drawn because he cannot submit — and submitting speaks for the whole function, so that part is right. What is wrong is that the page uses those two words to describe everything he can do.

Proposed — nothing entered yet

ProposedThe pill is replaced by his own project’s state, named by the code the rail already shows, and by the one act that is his: telling the office the project is finished.

Proposed — he has marked it done

ProposedDone and Reopen are the words the report already uses for a unit, in the same two shapes — the state, then the way back. The custodian sees which projects are ready and submits the function.

What is not changing

A project owner still does not submit, write the cycle note, or add picture slides — each speaks for the whole function, and one project’s owner cannot speak for the others. The rows he reaches, and the rows he does not, are exactly as they are today.

The decision

Three things to confirm before I build

  1. The wording. ADM02 · 0 of 3 entered and ADM02 · Done, with the sentence about who submits on a hover rather than printed.
  2. Mark done is stored per project, per cycle. It is a new fact the platform has not held before, and it clears when a new cycle opens — the same way a submission does.
  3. Both sides of the switch. A pillar owner on a business unit meets the identical bar today, so the same change lands there or the two drift apart.
The cost, stated: the bar gains a control that the custodian and the office also see, so the row is one item wider at every window; and a project marked done can still be edited until the function is submitted — the mark is a signal, not a lock.
