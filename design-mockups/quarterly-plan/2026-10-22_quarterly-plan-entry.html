Targets by quarter

A target can already be shaped month by month. This adds a second way to type the same plan: four quarters instead of twelve months. The drawer, the arithmetic and the reading stay the ones the monthly plan already uses.

Settled before drawing

A review inside a quarter owes that quarter in proportion: each quarter is spread evenly over its three months. At every quarter end the figure is the quarters that have passed.
Switching a row that already has a plan carries the figures across, in both directions.
The workbook columns are called Q1 target to Q4 target, and Outcome Q1 target to Outcome Q4 target on the Tactics sheet.

The switch

The drawer that opens from the mark beside the target, as it is today, with one addition: By month | By quarter at the right of its title line. It is the two-way switch the platform already uses elsewhere, the chosen half in navy.

Switching carries the plan across

Pressing By quarter on a row planned by month fills the four quarters from the months, and the target does not move. The line under the boxes says where the figures came from.

Planned by quarter from the start

The same rules the monthly plan follows today. The target becomes what the quarters set come to from the first one typed, the box locks, and an empty quarter counts as nothing planned.

August is two months into Q3, so it owes Q1 and Q2 and two-thirds of Q3: 45 + 51 + 56 = 152M EGP. A Count row still rounds down to whole ones, so 3 shops planned for Q3 owe 2 by August. A Latest or Average row reads the quarter’s figure for each of its three months, so Latest in August reads Q3’s figure.

The workbook

Four columns appended after December on each sheet that already carries the twelve months, so no existing column moves. A row has months or quarters, never both.

  • A row planned by quarter downloads with its four quarters filled and its months empty, and a row planned by month the other way round.
  • A row with both filled is refused at upload, by name, the way an owner who is not on the list is today: “Accessory revenue has both months and quarters filled. Keep one.”
  • A workbook downloaded before this change has neither column, and uploads exactly as it does today.
  • On Tactics, the existing Q1 to Q4 columns still say which quarters the tactic runs in. Requirements stays the last column.

What stays as it is

  • Rows with no plan, and rows planned by month, read exactly as they do today.
  • Reporting asks for one year-to-date figure per cycle, as now.
  • The slides print what is due, as now. Like by month, nothing on a slide names the shape.
  • Who can set or clear it is unchanged: the office, in the pen, as with months.
  • No database change. The quarters are kept where the monthly plan is kept.
  • The help answer How do I set a target month by month? gains a line on quarters.

Found while drawing this

The mark beside the target is meant to light amber as soon as a month is typed, so a plan somebody has started is easy to find again. Today it only lights after you leave the pillar and come back. The shots above show it lit, as a saved row shows it. The build fixes it for months and quarters together, since it is the same control.
