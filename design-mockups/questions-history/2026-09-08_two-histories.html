Why this half has no verdict

The assistant reports what it did — it answered from an entry, or it declined — so a row can honestly say needs an answer. A person's reply is always an answer of some kind, and the platform has no way to tell a good one from a holding line. So the office half is a record to read, not a queue to clear: no amber rows, no unanswered filter, and nothing marked. Add an answer is still there, because a question you have replied to six times by hand is exactly the one worth putting in the knowledge base — but it is an offer, never a judgement about the reply.

The cost of collecting it raw

Rows two and three above are the same question, counted separately, because people type it differently. Questions are grouped only when they are worded identically — anything looser would have the platform deciding in front of you that two questions are the same, which was refused when the assistant's half was drawn. So the counts on this half read low, and the reading of it is yours.

What this does not change

  • One store, one grouping — the same question can be counted on both sides and compared. The two histories are two readings of it, not two tables that will drift.
  • Nothing new is exposed. Both halves are already readable by the office in the Platform Inbox, and this page has the same gate.
  • The window stays 90 days, and nothing is backfilled.
