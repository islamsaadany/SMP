What changes, and what each costs

  • 1
    The email box is always there, and it is the only name the door takes

    Today the box hides until the server says a database is behind the door, and the door also accepts a person's internal key. Under spec 043 identity is an email (§313.2): one field, always shown, no key, no "AdminSMO" mode.

    Cost A person on the register with no email cannot sign in until one is added — already decided in §313.2, so nothing new is paid here.

  • 2
    The wall's fourth claim changes

    Today it reads "Installs, and opens offline — one file the browser keeps." The new app is not one file, and the wall may only claim what the product does (§34). The wall is an introduction, not a pitch, so the line says what the platform does for the people using it: the review deck is built from what was reported.

    Cost The install-and-offline promise leaves the wall until the new app can make it again. Recorded, not lost.

Carried across exactly as it is

  • The wall, the card, the eye, the ring on focus, the 30-day sentence, the entrance, and both themes (§34).
  • The one sentence for a wrong password, a retired account, and a rate-limited attempt — never which (§43).
  • The password card on first sign-in, with the optional "pick your unit or function" declaration (§56).
  • A client's door at /<client>/sign-in wearing that client's mark; the root door wearing none (§313.36).
  • The welcome screen: greeting, roles, the tenant's signature, what is waiting, your pages, the cycle, the intro round, and the way out spanning both columns (§148, §159, §200, §202).
  • Where you land: a client's person on their one client; the office on the platform's own pages (§313.2, contract §2).

Not in this group, drawn later behind its own mockup

  • The office's landing — Forefront's own pages with the client cards (§313.3).
  • Every page behind "Continue to Mobile": Strategy, Performance, Reporting, Setup.

What I need from you

A yes on the two numbered changes, or a different wording for 2. The person on the landing is an example, not a row from the register. Nothing here is built until you say so.
