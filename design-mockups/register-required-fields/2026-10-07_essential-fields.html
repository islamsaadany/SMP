Add someone: the two boxes you must fill

On the register's Add someone form, Full name and Email get a red star. If you press Add them with either one empty, the form stays open, rings the box in red and says what is missing. Nothing else on the form moves.

The words, for your approval
  • Bottom line of the form: * needed to add them. Everything else can wait. (today it says Only a name is needed.)
  • Full name left empty: A full name is needed to add them.
  • Email left empty: An email is needed — it is what they sign in with.
