The open box, and the frozen column

Both faults photographed out of the real build with your own state in it — a supporting function called Strategy Management Office — because the worked example has no label long enough to show either one.

1 · The open box runs out of its own column

“how can you resolve this issue”

Measured261px of box inside a 176px column — it hangs 93.6px into EMAIL, and a click inside the email cell lands on the box.
CauseOne word. An open cell was told max-width:none on the reasoning that a block fills its container. True of a typed box, false of a drop box — a button sizes to its text.
Fixmax-width:none → max-width:100%. The box ends at the column edge, the long name clips with an “…” the way every other cell already clips, the row height does not move, and the email gets its clicks back.
CostWhile it is open, a name too long to fit reads Strategy Manageme…. The full name is still what is stored and what the list shows.

2 · The three dots are not in the Roles column

“you added the 3 dots to the roles whihc is wrong the 3 dots is out of this column”

They are their own last column, frozen to the right edge since §69.19 so you can always act on a row however far you have scrolled. What you are seeing is that frozen column floating over whatever is underneath — Roles, in your window.

MeasuredAt 1500px the table does not scroll and the dots sit properly after STATUS. At 1200px and below it scrolls, and the dots sit at x1129 inside a Roles column running 977 → 1186. Nothing is lost: scrolled fully right, STATUS is visible again.
FindingThe seam that column is declared to have has never painted. box-shadow on a table cell does nothing in a border-collapse:collapse table — proved by putting a solid red one on it and photographing no change. Both frozen columns have been seamless since they were frozen.

Recommendation: A. The freeze is doing a real job, and the fault is that nothing ever said the column was frozen. C — making the table fit your window so it never scrolls — means giving up or narrowing a column, which is a bigger decision about what the register shows.

Every figure here was measured on the built file, not estimated. Nothing has been changed in the sources — this is for sign-off first.
