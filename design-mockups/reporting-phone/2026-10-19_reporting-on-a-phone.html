Reporting fits a phone

Today the Reporting page is wider than a phone, so the whole page slides sideways and the Submit button sits off the edge. Below is the same page at a phone's width, today and with the fix applied in the browser.

What changes, and what doesn't

  • DesktopNothing. Compared pixel for pixel at 1440 and 1280 wide on Reporting, Performance and Strategy, before and after.
  • Top barOn a phone only, on every page: two lines, the platform's and the client's names on the first, "Viewing as" and the two buttons on the second. The role note beside "Viewing as" is hidden on a phone, since there is no room for it.
  • BandOn a phone only, the band over each row scrolls with the page instead of staying pinned. Wrapped, it is taller, and pinned it would hold a quarter of the screen. This is the same band on Strategy and Performance, so it behaves the same there.
  • Pinned areaAbout as much of a phone's screen stays pinned while you scroll as today, 275px against 272px today, out of 760px.
  • TablesUnchanged. They already scroll sideways inside their own box.
