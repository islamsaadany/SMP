RHI set-up: two Setup changes

Both pictures are the running platform with the proposal drawn into it. Today is on the left, proposed on the right.

1 · Setup › Capabilities: its own owner and custodian

  • Owner and Custodian replace the Head column, which was read from the holding function. They're picked from the register, like a business unit's.
  • Held by becomes optional. RHI's "Company Wide" holds directions 1–4 and 15 as pillars, with no function.
  • Access follows the capability's own owner and custodian, not the function's. This reverses the 13 September decision.
  • The "One function each" note goes, because it's no longer true.

2 · Getting started › Structure: a "Plan details" card

  • One card after the level cards, with four switches in the same style as the Components chips.
  • Direction overview adds Objective, Why now, and Risks & mitigations as the direction's Overview section.
  • Years 1 · 2 · 3 adds which years each direction runs, plus the yearly revision.
  • Several outcomes per tactic and Requirements add them on the tactic.
  • Every window (plan, reporting, performance, deck, workbook) asks the same switch. Off hides and keeps what was written.
