RHI set-up · step 3: the Direction overview

1 · Plan page: folded (how it opens)

One line under the direction's name. It shows the start of the Objective so you can tell what's inside without opening it. Press the line to open it.

2 · Plan page: opened

The three texts side by side. Press the line again to fold it. With Edit on, the same three become text boxes.

3 · Presentation slide, redesigned

The Objective leads in large type. Why now and the risks sit under it, with one line per risk. On the right is a panel listing the key measures with their targets and the key tactics with their owners, all taken from the tables.

4 · Switch off (Raya): nothing changes

No line, no slide. A direction with all three empty gets no line and no slide either. Empty boxes are not counted as missing.
