RHI set-up · step 3: the Direction overview

1 · Plan page, reading

An Overview block under the direction's name, above Key measures: Objective · Why now · Risks & mitigations, as plain text. Key measures and tactics are the tables below, so they are not typed again.

2 · Plan page, with Edit open

The same three boxes become text boxes under the owner and kind rows. Only the office writes them, like the rest of the plan.

3 · Presentation: a new slide after each direction's title slide

The three texts, then the key measures and key tactics listed by name from the tables.

4 · With the switch off (Raya): nothing changes

The block, the slide and the workbook columns only appear when Direction overview is on in Structure › Plan details. An empty box shows a dash, and a direction with all three empty gets no slide.

To decide

  • Is the block's position right (under the name, above Key measures)?
  • Is the slide's position right (straight after the direction's title slide)?
  • Should empty boxes count as missing items? Recommended: no. They are optional text, like a description.
