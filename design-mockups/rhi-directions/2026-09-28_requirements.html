RHI set-up · step 5: what each tactic needs (Requirements)

What changes

  • Each tactic gets a Requirements list: what it needs to happen, e.g. people, budget or a sign-off. It's plain text, one item per line, like RHI's own plan sheet.
  • It is never scored and never counted as missing. An empty list just shows nothing.
  • It shows on the Plan page only. Reporting and Performance don't change.
  • It's off by default. It turns on with a new Requirements button on the Plan details card (Getting started › Structure), next to the two already there. Raya Trade stays as it is.
