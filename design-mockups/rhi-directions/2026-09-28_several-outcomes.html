RHI set-up · step 4: several outcomes per tactic

What changes

  • A tactic can carry more than one outcome. Each outcome has its own target, unit, direction and adding-up rule, exactly like today's single outcome.
  • When a tactic has two or more, each is numbered O1, O2… (like RHI's review slides) and sits on its own line under the tactic. A tactic with one outcome looks exactly as it does today.
  • Each outcome is scored on its own, and the tactic's progress is their average.
  • It is off by default. It turns on with a Several outcomes per tactic button on the Plan details card (Getting started › Structure). Raya Trade stays as it is.
