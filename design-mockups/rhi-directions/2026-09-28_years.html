RHI set-up · step 6: Years 1 · 2 · 3

What changes

  • The planning period can run three years. The platform names each one: Year 1 · 2026, Year 2 · 2027, Year 3 · 2028, and knows which year we are in from the month the cycle covers to.
  • Each direction marks the years it runs in, with three small boxes (the same boxes the tactics use for quarters).
  • A direction that doesn't run this year stays on the Plan page, greyed, so the three-year picture is still visible. It isn't asked for in reporting and isn't scored until its year comes.
  • Once a year the office starts a yearly revision: last year's plan is archived as it stood, and the plan is refined for the new year. Tactics work yearly.
  • Off by default: a fourth Years 1 · 2 · 3 button on the Plan details card. Raya Trade stays as it is.
  • The example years on Mobile's four pillars are made up.
