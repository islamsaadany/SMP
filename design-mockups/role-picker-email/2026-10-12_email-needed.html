The role picker asks for an email

When you give somebody a role and they are not on the register yet, you type their name and press “+ Add”. Today that adds them with no email. This would hold the press until there is one, the same way the Add someone form does.

What changes

  • The Email box in the role picker gets the red star.
  • Pressing “+ Add” with no email adds nobody and says why, in the same words as the Add someone form.
  • Typing in the Email box clears the red ring.
  • It is one picker, so this reaches all five places it appears: a business unit's head and custodian, a supporting function's head and custodian, a function's Led by, the capabilities table, and the group's capability seats.

What it costs

  • You can no longer give a role to somebody new by name alone. If you do not have their email yet, you add them later or ask for it first.
  • This reverses an earlier rule that said neither box in the picker was required.
  • Picking somebody already on the register is unchanged, even if their row has no email.
  • The people upload is left as it is.
