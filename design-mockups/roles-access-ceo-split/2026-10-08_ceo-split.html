Two CEO rows, and Part owner may read

What changes, in your words.

  • The CEO row becomes two rows: Group CEO (the top) and Division head (the second layer). Each is named in the client's own words: the word for the top, plus "CEO", and the word for the second layer, plus "head".
  • A client with no second layer has no Division head row.
  • Setup stays shut for both. Nobody but the Super user and the SMO team sees it.
  • Part owner's Plan and Reporting become may read, so the amber "differs" goes away and the warning line above the table goes with it.
