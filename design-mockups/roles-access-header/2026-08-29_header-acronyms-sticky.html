Four changes you asked for, drawn inside the real platform rather than sketched. One of them conflicts with another, and that is the decision at the bottom.

3 · The header stays while you scroll

Scrolled a third of the way down the roles: the two header rows hold together at the top, so you can always see which column a cell belongs to.

What this costs, plainly. A header can only stay put if the thing under it does the scrolling. So the matrix gets its own scrollbar and sits inside the window, the way the People register already does — instead of the whole page scrolling past it.

4 · The one thing that conflicts

You asked for BU, Func. and Support, and for a maximum of two lines. Written out in full, those two asks fight: Other Support Func. is still too long for a 93px column and wraps to three.
