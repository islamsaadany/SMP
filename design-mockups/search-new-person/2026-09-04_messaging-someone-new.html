Messaging someone who has never written in

Islam: “in the serach I need to be able to send to a new person as well how do oy uthink we do this correctly”

Drawn in the real corner, not sketched. The platform was opened, the corner opened, the search typed — only the list is replaced with the proposal. Every other pixel is the product's own, so what you sign off is what gets built. The names and places are from your own screens, not the worked example.

1What happens today

The search reads conversations only. Type somebody who has never written in and the answer is nothing found — a dead end, when the office is the one side that is allowed to start a conversation.

2Why two groups and not one list

  • They are different things to press. One carries history, the other starts something. Mixed together, the office messages somebody twice by accident.
  • Nobody appears in both. Somebody with a conversation is only ever in the first group.
  • The office only. Everybody else's corner is their own single conversation with the office — there is nothing to start.

3What this costs to build

Very little, because it is not a new way to start a conversation. The Platform Inbox already has one: it checks the person is on the register, refuses somebody who has left, and carries on into their existing conversation if they have one.

This reuses that whole thing. The message box, the email that goes out, the waiting list and every rule about who may do it come for free. Nothing new is stored, and nothing new is decided about who is allowed what.

4What I would like you to look at

  • The second heading's words. Currently “3 more on the register, with no conversation yet”.
  • Whether the second group should be capped. Search for a and half the company matches. I would show the first ten and say how many more there are.
  • Whether a retired person should appear at all. I would leave them out entirely — they cannot sign in to read it.

Three answers and I build it

  1. Two groups, or something else?
  2. Cap the second group at ten, or show everyone?
  3. The heading's wording — keep, or your words.
