Mockup for sign-off · third round · the office's corner

Messaging someone who has never written in

Islam: “i still can see a header” — and “I cant see the button open the platform inbox which should be always there”

Both fixed, and the second one was a real fault rather than a preference. The way out of the corner sat inside the scrolling list, so a list of any length carried it off the bottom. It is pinned now.

1Every heading is gone

The last grey line — “5 found in all conversations and on the register” — read as a header because it was one. It said two things: where the search looked, and how many it found.

The count was never needed: the list is the count. And where it looked belongs in the search box, which is where somebody reads it at the moment they decide to type — for no row at all.

Why it could not simply be deleted. The Waiting tab is the one lit while results reach conversations that have been answered. Without a word anywhere, the office is quietly misled about what it is looking at. The box now carries that, and nothing sits above the rows.

2Today, and the proposal

3The way out is pinned

This was broken before today's proposal and would have stayed broken. Open the Platform Inbox lived at the end of the list, so on any search with a few results it scrolled away — the one permanent way out of the corner, gone whenever the list was long.

It sits above the message box now and does not move, in all four pictures. The cost, said plainly: one row of list height, always spent. Worth it for a way out that is always there.

4Settled

Two groups — carried by the row shape and the order, with no headings at all.
Cap at ten, the overflow counted at the foot.
No headers. Both gone; the scope moved into the search box.
The way out is pinned above the message box.
The office only. Everybody else's corner is their own single conversation; there is nothing for them to start.
Somebody who has left is not offered — they cannot sign in to read it.
Nothing new is stored. Pressing a name opens the same empty conversation the Platform Inbox already opens, with the same rules, the same message box and the same email.
