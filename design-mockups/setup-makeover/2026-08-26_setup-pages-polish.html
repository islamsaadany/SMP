One name, in a header that stays put. The repeated section heading is gone, and the column dividers separate the headings.

The name comes from the rail, not from the page

The rail's label becomes the page's title, read from the one list that already holds it — the same rule the Overview's rows follow, so a rename can never again leave a page calling itself something else. That fixes the two duplicates and the five disagreements in one move.

A section heading that repeats the title is dropped

Where a page's first section is called what the page is called, it renders without its heading — §28's rule that a header which says nothing new still spends its line.

Shortening the label, rather than letting it clip

An ellipsis would keep the name and hide half of it behind a hover — fine in a table cell, wrong in a navigation list, where the label is the destination. “Import & archives” keeps both halves of what the page does; “Plan” is what the whole of Setup is about, so it is the word that can go. Your call — the alternative that also fits is “Plans & archives”.

4The tablesnote 5

A divider between header columns

The headings are uppercase and letter-spaced with 8px of padding and nothing between them, so at your width “Shown in the nav” and “Code” merge into one word. A hairline between header cells is what makes a table read as columns — it costs no width and no height.

The table head sticks under the page header

On the register at 33 rows, and on Business units at 10 units × 11 columns, the headings are gone by the time you are reading the rows — which is the moment you most need to know which column you are in.

One page rhythm, so the Setup pages read as a set

Every page: header · (its own controls) · sections. The same gap above a section, the same gap between a section's heading and its table, on all eighteen.

5What I need from you

Does the title carry anything besides the name?

Some pages have controls that live at the top today (the register's Columns, Passwords and file menus). I would put them in the sticky header beside the title so they stay reachable too — or they can stay where they are and only the name sticks.

Should the table head stick as well as the title?

I recommend yes — it is the single biggest gain on the register and on Business units. It is also the one item here that touches every table in Setup rather than the chrome around them.

“Import & archives”, “Plans & archives”, or keep the long name and clip it?

I recommend “Import & archives”.

Setup only, or the whole product?

A unit's pages have the same title pattern and the same non-sticky table heads. I recommend Setup only for now — it is what you are looking at, and it keeps this reviewable. The rest can follow once you have seen it working.

Row height: 36px, or tighter?

36px keeps a comfortable target for a mouse. I recommend 36px; 32px is possible and starts to feel cramped beside the 15px glyph.
