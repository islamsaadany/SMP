BUILT — and this half was narrowed on the way. Option B shipped. The claim below, that the two rail numbers wear one look, was overstated: measured, the entry count is quiet grey mono and the alert is a gold pill, and they are already told apart. What is actually wrong is narrower and is what shipped — the entry count was drawn on an open group, where the rows it counts are all on screen. The dashed marks are the counts that go. A number beside an open group heading tells you how many rows are already on screen; the gold one tells you something is waiting. With only one kind of number left in the rail, the gold one can be trusted. Where the group is FOLDED the gold count still shows, which is what it was built for.

My recommendation: A, with the rail change. It is the smaller move, it survives a narrow window without changing shape, and it fixes the thing that is actually wrong — the page answered the cycle's question first and yours second. B is better on a wide screen and I would not build it until the queue routinely has more than three rows in it.
