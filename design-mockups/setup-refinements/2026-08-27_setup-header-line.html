Send an email

Renamed, the two chips dropped, Drafts and Sent lifted onto the pinned line — and the Email settings folded in as the page's second half.

Answering your question — dropdown or sub-panel?

A sub-panel, and specifically a second tab on this page. A settings dropdown is right for the Inbox because that is seven switches. Email settings is not switches: it is a status table, four fields, a live rendered preview of the message, and a send-a-test block. A rendered email inside a dropdown is not a dropdown.

This is the shape the platform already uses twice — Figure sets and Import & archives are each one rail entry with two sections inside. It also keeps the access right: each section keeps its own gate, so somebody who holds Email but not Send a message still reaches the settings half. Setup › Email leaves the rail.

Item 1

Where somebody sits

Two ways to put the company in front of you. They differ in one thing only: whether it is a field of its own or a group inside the field that is already there.

One thing to settle, and it is not cosmetic

A person sits in exactly one place today — that is not a habit, it is built in: attachPersonAt() clears unit, function and company before setting one, and personAt() gives one answer. Sign-in, the Official BU list, roles and the Overview all rest on it.

So Option A is two controls answering one question, which is exactly the pair that was removed from this dialog in §110 for colliding. It works if picking a company visibly clears the unit — which is what the grey line under the fields says — but it can be read as “Mobile, in Distribution”, and it isn't.

My recommendation is Option A with one change: Company is filled in for you and read-only whenever a unit is chosen (Mobile's company is Distribution — the platform already knows), and becomes editable only when there is no unit. You get the field beside the unit that you asked for, the register gains a real Company column, and there is still one stored answer. Say the word and I'll draw that third version.

Item 5

The left rail — measured

I could not reproduce this and I would rather say so than guess. On the current build I scrolled the Setup page to 300, 800 and 1500px at nine window sizes from 1920×1080 down to 1000×650. The navy SETUP header holds at 96px and “Find a setting…” at 136px in every single one — they never move, on the page or inside the rail.

Two things I can see that would feel like this, and I need you to tell me which:

  • The rail list has its own scrollbar. Below about 900px of window height the eighteen entries do not fit, so the list scrolls inside its own box while the page scrolls separately — two scrollbars, and the rows move under a header that doesn't.
  • The group headings scroll away. Running the cycle, People & access and the rest are inside that scrolling list, so they leave the screen while you scroll through the entries beneath them.

If it is neither, a screenshot mid-scroll would settle it in a second.
