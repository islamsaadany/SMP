Setup pages keep a change only when you press Save

Every picture is the real platform, photographed, with the new parts drawn into the live page. The behaviour is HR-ERP's.

1 · You change something, and the area says it isn't saved yet

You pick a new accent. The page shows the colour straight away so you can judge it, but nothing is kept yet. Under the table are Save, Discard changes, and an amber line naming what is waiting: 1 change not saved yet: the accent. Discard puts the old colour back.

2 · You press Save

The change is stored for everyone. Save goes grey because nothing is left to save, and a green Saved shows for a few seconds. It never says Saved while anything is still waiting.

3 · The same on every Setup page where you change settings

Terminology, with the last word changed to Legacy. The box you changed is outlined, and the same Save row sits under the table. Each area on a page gets its own Save, next to what it saves.

4 · You try to leave without saving

You press another page on the left (Scoring bands, outlined). The page doesn't change. A strip at the bottom asks: Save and continue, Discard changes or Keep editing. The same question comes up for the browser's Back button and for Sign out. Closing the tab gets the browser's own "Leave site?" question.

What you are agreeing to

Which pages
Every Setup page where you change settings, such as the colours, Terminology, Scoring bands and Roles & access. The plan and report pages keep saving on their own for now.
Tables with a pen on each row
Business units, Functions, the People register and the rest already open a row and give it Save and Cancel. They stay that way, and they get the leave question too.
Pages that act when you press a button
Send an email, Import, and opening or closing the cycle already ask before they act. They stay as they are.
If a save fails
The reason shows in red beside Save, and your changes stay on the screen so nothing is lost.
