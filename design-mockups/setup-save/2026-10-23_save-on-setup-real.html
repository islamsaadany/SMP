Setup pages keep a change only when you press Save

Every picture is the real platform, photographed, with the new parts drawn into the live page. The behaviour is HR-ERP's.

1 · You pick a colour, and the page stays as it is until you press Apply

You pick a new accent. Only the colour box and its code change. The rest of the page keeps today's colours. Under the table are Apply, Discard, and an amber line: 1 change not applied yet: the accent. Discard puts the box back.

2 · You press Apply

The page takes the new colour, for everyone. A green Applied shows with Undo beside it for about ten seconds. Press Undo to go back to the colours you had just before. After that, Reset to the shipped palette is still there to go back to the platform's own navy and gold.

3 · Every other Setup page works the same way, with Save

Scoring bands, with the top floor changed from 90 to 85. The box you changed is outlined, and the same row sits at the foot of the area: Save, Discard, and 1 change not saved yet: the floor for On track. On a page that opens with an Edit button, like this one, Save and Discard take the place of Done.

4 · You try to leave without saving

You press another page on the left (Business units, outlined). The page doesn't change. A strip at the bottom asks: Save and continue, Discard changes or Keep editing. The same question comes up for the browser's Back button and for Sign out. Closing the tab gets the browser's own "Leave site?" question.

What you are agreeing to

The colours
Apply, not Save. Nothing on the page changes before Apply. Undo shows for about ten seconds after Apply. Reset to the shipped palette stays.
Which pages get Save
Every other Setup page where you change settings, such as Scoring bands, Roles & access and Revenue drivers. The plan and report pages keep saving on their own for now.
Tables with a pen on each row
Business units, Functions, the People register and the rest already open a row and give it Save and Cancel. They stay that way, and they get the leave question too.
Pages that act when you press a button
Send an email, Import, and opening or closing the cycle already ask before they act. They stay as they are.
If a save fails
The reason shows in red beside Save, and your changes stay on the screen so nothing is lost.
