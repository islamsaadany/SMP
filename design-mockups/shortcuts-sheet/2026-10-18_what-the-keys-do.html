What the keys do

The audit says the platform has one keyboard idiom and it is the projector. Measured against the sources today, there are four — and the real finding is the one the audit does not make: nothing on any screen says so. There is no list of keys anywhere in the product. The only way to discover Alt+Enter is to hover the one button that carries it in a tooltip.

So this adds no shortcut. It writes down the ones that already work, and gives every one added later somewhere to appear.

A ? in the same pill the other top-bar controls wear. It needs a visible route as well as the key: a sheet you can only reach by knowing the key to reach it is the problem it was built to solve, one level up.

What this is, and what it is not

  • No new shortcut. Every key on the sheet already works today. Nothing is bound, rebound or taken away.
  • The keys were read from the sources, not remembered. Each line was taken out of the file that binds it.
  • One thing to decide beyond A or B: ? must not fire while somebody is typing — most of this product is fields. It opens only when the cursor is not in a box, which is the same guard the reporting keys already use.
  • It makes the next round discoverable. If accelerators are added later for Submit, Save draft or moving between destinations, they appear here rather than in another tooltip nobody hovers.
