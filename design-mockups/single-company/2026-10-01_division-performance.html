Where divisions go when the strategy is planned on the company

El Abd, with the strategy planned on the company. The division dropdown leaves the navigation; each division's performance, scored only from the supporting functions linked to it, moves under Strategy. Two placements to choose from. Function names and figures are illustrative.

One Performance tab, with a small row: Company · each division

The difference
  1. A keeps one Performance tab; the company and the divisions are side by side in one small row. One more click to reach the company's own reading is not needed: Company is the first entry and opens by default.
  2. B keeps Performance exactly as it is and puts the divisions behind their own tab. Cleaner separation; one more tab on the row.
