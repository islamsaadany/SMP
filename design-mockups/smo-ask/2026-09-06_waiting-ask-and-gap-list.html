Every question the assistant is asked lands on one list on the Knowledge base page, marked answered or not, and an answer written there becomes a knowledge base entry. Drawn in the platform’s own colours and controls; nothing is built.

The question, as it will read in the knowledge base
Can a unit head reopen their own report?
The answer
Yes. Whoever may submit for a subject may reopen it — the unit’s head or its strategy custodian, and the office. Reopening…
Answered to The office ▾ — because the office asked it. Change it to Everybody if the same answer suits both.

The question comes across editable. What somebody typed in a hurry is rarely how it should read in the knowledge base, and editing it here does not change what is on the list, so the record of what was actually asked stays honest.
The audience defaults to the side that asked, and can be changed in one press. That is the SMO-answer-versus-user-answer distinction carried from day one, with nothing to decide now.
Only questions the assistant read and could not answer are marked as unanswered. The times it was never reached at all — no key, service down — are not on this list; they are a plumbing problem with their own diagnostic.
Similar questions are not grouped. The platform putting “how do I close the cycle” together with “when does the cycle close” would be it guessing in front of you, and wrong often enough that the counts stop being worth reading.

3 The switch splits

The existing switch keeps its name and its meaning — the users’. The office’s is new, and starts off.

The assistant
Answer people before the office does i
Unchanged. A person writing to the office gets an answer from the knowledge base first; anything it cannot answer waits for you as it does today.
Ask, for the office i
Off. Turn it on and the office’s own corner gains an Ask box. It answers from the same knowledge base and starts no conversation.

Two switches rather than one, because reusing the existing switch would silently have turned the Ask box on for every tenant that had already switched the assistant on for their people. A new switch starts off for everybody and nobody is surprised.

4 What this drawing does not decide

Named here rather than discovered later.

What happens to a row once its answer is written. Drawn as staying on the list with the answer beside it. Nothing removes a row.
How far back the list reaches. Drawn as 90 days, which is a starting number rather than a decision.
Where the list sits on the page. Drawn under the page header on the Questions tab, above the questions themselves.
The answer goes live the moment it is written — no draft, no second person approving it. Say if you would rather it waited.
