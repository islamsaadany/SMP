Joined or split

The one new setting: whether a box shows its pillars as one list or as two sections. For El Abd and RHI nothing on screen moves — the pages below are the proof, drawn from their real data. The only new thing is that the choice now exists.

3The setting itself

One row in Set-up, on the box. It appears only where a box plans in pillars — there is nothing to join or split otherwise.

Plans in exists today
Pillars show as the new one

The second option is named from the box's own words, so a client that calls them something else reads its own names here. Switching moves no data and renames nothing — the same rows, drawn in one list or in two.
