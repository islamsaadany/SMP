Strategy, for reading: a window with the strategy in it and nothing else

The platform as it stands is a workbench: navigation, pens, Reporting, the cycle. A unit owner needs it. A CEO, a peer function head or anyone opening it for the first time wants to read the strategy. This is a window that opens over the platform and holds only that: a picker for the company or a unit, five tabs, and Exit. Drawn on the worked example in the platform's own look; the control at the right shows it in the other theme.

  • Who. A Read button on the bar for everyone; for a seat that owns nothing (a CEO, a reader) the platform opens in the window, with the workbench a press away only for those who have it.
  • Where it starts. On the company, because that is "the company strategy in general". The units are in the picker, and a person who owns a unit starts on theirs.
  • What is in. Foundation, Analysis, Directions, Plan, Performance — the five things a strategy is. Out: Reporting, the cycle, Reviews, the Copilot, every pen and button.
  • Which figures. What is in the platform now, as Presentation mode does: no exported copy to go stale. The top says the review point ("as of H1 2026").
  • A line per tab for first-timers, drawn from the page info the platform already carries (pageinfo.js), so nobody needs a tour to know what Directions means.
  • Read only. No reacting in this round. A reader who has something to say has the chat, which is a tab of the workbench and not of this window.
01

The door: Read, on Strategy's bar

beside the theme ring and Sign out; one word, for everyone

Read opens the window on what you are looking at — this unit if you are in one, the company otherwise — and Exit returns you to exactly where you were, as Presentation mode does. A seat that owns nothing never sees this bar first: for them the window is the platform, and the bar's place is taken by a quiet Open the workbench only where their seat allows it.

02

The window on the company: Foundation

the aspiration first and large, then the mission, what the company is, and its values

The navy strip is the window's only chrome: the mark, whose strategy this is, the picker, the review point, Exit. Under it the five tabs on a gold rule, the same gold line the unit's own section row wears. Every tab opens with one line in the gold eyebrow's voice saying what the page answers.

03

The window on a unit: Directions

Mobile's pillars, each with its objectives and where they stand

A pillar is a card: its code and name, its end-state, then its objectives with target, actual and a bar in the band's colour. No weights, no pens, no ordering. The tactics that deliver a pillar are on the next tab, Plan, so a reader is not shown everything at once.

04

Analysis, Plan and Performance, on the same unit

the other three tabs, each as plain

Analysis is the SWOT as four plain lists. Plan is the tactics under their pillars: owner, the quarters they run, status. Performance is the unit's three figures, never blended (the page info's own rule), then the headline objectives. Each is the workbench's page with everything that edits, weighs or orders it taken off — nothing new is computed, so the window never disagrees with the page behind it.

05

Two ways to reach it, in order

first a window over the platform; later, its own address

A — a window over the platform (first). Opens from Read, takes the whole window, Exit returns you. Built from the pages' own renderers in a read-only dress, the way the deck is. Quickest to have, and where the reading itself gets settled.

B — its own address (after). /raya/strategy/read, the way Processes has its reading side: a link a CEO opens directly, the landing for a seat that owns nothing, the console's door into Strategy. The same window, with an address instead of an Exit.
