The reading window's picker, by reach

Today the picker lists the company and every business unit, for everyone. That is less than the workbench's navigator shows and more than some seats should see. This round the picker is the navigator's own list — the company, the divisions, the units, the supporting functions, the capabilities — cut down for each viewer by the one rule Roles & access already holds, and each subject is read in its own shape.

  • In the picker when the matrix gives this viewer view or better on the subject's pages; otherwise not there at all. The navigator's rule, not a second one.
  • Grouped as the navigator groups: a CEO with ten units and eight functions needs the headings.
  • One subject reached, no picker to speak of: the window opens on it, and the control reads the name and nothing else.
  • Each subject in its own shape. A unit: Foundation, Analysis, Directions, Overall performance. A function: Foundation, S&W, then what its plan is — Projects, Directions, or Objectives — then Overall performance. A division: its own Foundation and the performance of what it holds.

the same list, cut down by what each reaches

Finance's reader — the function alone

The list is built once from the navigator's own entries and filtered by grant at page for the viewer: a subject stays when any of its pages answers view or better. For the third viewer the picker is the name alone, drawn quiet, and the window opens on Finance rather than on the company.

02

A supporting function: Finance, which plans in projects

Foundation · S&W · Projects · Overall performance

A function's second tab is S&W — strengths and weaknesses alone, as the workbench keeps them for functions (§399). Its third tab says what its plan is: Projects here, folded to one line each as directions are, opened to the project's brief, owner, milestones and standing; Directions for a function that plans in pillars; Objectives for one that plans in objectives and actions. Overall performance reads the function's own figures, as its Performance page does.

03

A division: Distribution

Foundation · Overall performance

A division has a Foundation of its own where the office has given it one (§404), and a performance that is its units' headlines at their weights; it plans nothing of its own, so there is no Directions tab. Its units are in the picker under it, for whoever reaches them.
