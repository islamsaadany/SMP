The reading window's navigator row

Round 5 put the subjects in a drop-down; with ten units and eight functions that is a list to scroll through. The workbench already solved this: a row with the company menu pinned at the left, the Units | Functions switch, and the names on one line that scrolls. The window carries that same row under its strip, cut down for the viewer by the Roles & access reach rule. Nothing else about the window changes.

  • The same row, the same rules. The company menu holds the company and its divisions; pressing one reads it and narrows the units to its own, as the workbench does. The switch has two sides, or three where a capability exists (§334). The names scroll; the controls stay pinned (§136).
  • Cut down by reach. A name is in the row when the matrix gives this viewer view or better on any of that subject's pages. A side of the switch with nothing in it is not drawn; one side alone is a word, not a switch (§464).
  • The lit name is the subject; the tabs below are that subject's own: a unit's four, a function's four with its plan's tab, a division's two.
  • One subject reached: the row is its word alone and the window opens on it.
