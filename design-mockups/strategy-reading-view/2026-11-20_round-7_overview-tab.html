The reading window's Overview tab — El Abd's board

The window as it is — the strip, the subject row, the tabs, Exit, the SMO's Print — with one tab before Foundation: Overview. A reader lands on it. It is a dashboard of the figures the other tabs hold, and every card is a door to the tab that holds the full picture.

  • Nothing new is computed. Every figure is the one its tab draws: the three readings are Overall performance's, a direction's standing is the Directions tab's, a function's headline is its own window's.
  • Every card is a door. The title opens the tab; a line opens the tab on that direction, that capability, that function.
  • The tabs stay. Foundation, Analysis, Directions, Capabilities, Overall performance are a press away, as today; the window opens on Overview instead of Foundation for a reader.
  • El Abd's shape. No business units, so no units card. On Raya the same tab would show the units' line, and no capabilities card where the box is not split — the cards follow the Structure, as the tabs do.

What the three questions settle

Who lands here

A reader’s seat (§687) opens the window on Overview instead of Foundation. The SMO and anybody opening the window from the workbench see the tab too, and open on Overview as well — one first screen, not two.

Where the key measures table is

Behind the first card: “Measures” opens Overall performance, where the headline measures table already is. The Overview stays one screen tall for a board.

The same tab on another client

On Raya the Directions door becomes Business units (each unit’s headline, at its weight), the Capabilities door is absent where the box is not split, and Functions is as here. Nothing is configured: the cards follow the client’s Structure, exactly as the tabs do.
