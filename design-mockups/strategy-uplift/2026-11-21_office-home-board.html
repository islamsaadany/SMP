On the two overview pages

They are two pages because they answer two questions. The reading Overview answers how are we performing, for the people who read the strategy. The office’s Home answers is the strategy written, is it reported, what is still missing, for the people who run it.

The duplication to avoid is narrower: the same score worked out twice. So the board carries one figure per row, it is the same figure the reading Overview shows, and pressing it goes there. The board never draws its own performance cards.

And only one of them is called Overview. This one is Home.

What changed from round 1

Not a new tab. The group keeps Strategy, Performance, Focus, Temple. The board is what the office meets after sign-in and behind the mark at the top.

The greeting band stays and takes the cycle’s own lines, so the separate cycle block goes.

Units sit under their company, and Functions are the other side of the same switch.

Waiting on you and Your pages are gone, as you chose. Each row is its own door, and what waited on the office is under Still missing.

How it behaves
  • A row opens that place at its first gap: Mobile opens on Drivers, a unit with only its report owing opens on Reporting, a unit with nothing owing opens on its Plan.
  • The figure at the end of a row opens that place’s Performance page.
  • Every status is read from what the page behind it already knows. Nothing is counted a second time for the board.
  • Everybody who is not in the office keeps the Home they have today.
