Client set-up › Structure — the company's plan cards

Drawn from the real step on the worked example (its top level is called “Group”, its pillar word “Pillars”).

  • One card, two halves side by side: the company's own plan on the left, the capabilities it carries on the right.
  • The left half is called “Third layer” until a name is typed in its Called boxes; then it shows that name.
  • Capabilities in the company plan have no first or second section: just their name, their plan section and its two ways. Their “Except for” box goes too, because it only ever covered those two sections.
  • Both off: the same one card, each half its switch and one short line saying it is off and why.

1 · Planned on the company, both on

2 · Planned on the units, both off

Off lines, one each: “Off — the company plans nothing of its own.” and “Off — they are their own layer (below).” Nothing else on the step moves.
