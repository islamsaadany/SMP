Business units, with two exceptions

The ticks above stay the default for every business unit. Under them, Except for lists only the units that differ. Each chip shows what that unit carries; a gold ring marks the part that differs from the layer. Pressing a chip switches it; Remove puts the unit back on the layer's ticks.

Adding one

Pick a unit in Make an exception for…. It joins the list with the layer's ticks, then you switch the part that should differ. A unit whose chips all match the layer again leaves the list by itself.
