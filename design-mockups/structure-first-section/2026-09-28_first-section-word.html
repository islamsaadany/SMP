What each box would rename

  • Top level: the group's Foundation tab.
  • Second layer: each company's Foundation tab.
  • Business units: the first section on every unit (Foundation · SWOT · Plan), plus the chip in the missing bar.
  • Supporting functions: the first section on every function (Overview · S&W · Plan).
  • Starting words are today's words: Foundation, Foundation, Foundation, Overview. Nothing moves on an existing client until someone types a new word.
