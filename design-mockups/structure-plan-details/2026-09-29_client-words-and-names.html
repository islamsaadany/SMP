The same four switches sit in the Plan section of the top level and the second layer too. The separate Plan details card at the bottom goes away.

What it costs and what it touches
  • Nothing a client has switched on today moves. A layer that has not been set on its own takes the client-wide choice made before, so the client who turned these on keeps them on everywhere until you change a layer.
  • Years: the switch decides whether a layer's directions are marked Year 1 · 2 · 3. The plan's current year and the yearly revision stay one for the whole client, because the revision moves everybody's year at once.
  • A capability follows the layer of the function that holds it.
  • Off hides and never deletes; turning it back on brings what was written back.
  • Every page that shows these (the plan page, reporting, the deck, the plan download, the workbook) has to ask about the layer it is on, so this is a real piece of building rather than a relabel. No database change.
