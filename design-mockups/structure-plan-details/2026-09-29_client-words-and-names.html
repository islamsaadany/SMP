What it costs and what it touches
  • The switches stay client-wide; the names are per layer, like every other part. The chip words follow the business units layer, because that is where most plans live.
  • The same three new rows appear on the top level, second layer and functions cards too, each with its own names.
  • With a switch off, its row is not drawn here and nothing typed is lost.
  • The names then reach the plan page, reporting, the deck and the workbook headings through the same word reader as every other part.
  • No database change: the names sit in the same place as the other part names.
