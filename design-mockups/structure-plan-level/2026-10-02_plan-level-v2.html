What moving between the two does

Units level → Company level folds the units' plans into the company's Directions (as built), and folds separate capabilities into the company's Capabilities section. Nothing is deleted: plans are hidden and kept, and come back if the client switches back. Raya Trade keeps exactly what it shows today.
