Structure, section by section

Setup › Client set-up › Structure. Every layer gets the three sections its pages already have. For each one you choose whether it is there, what it is called, which parts it holds and what each part is called. Each layer is set on its own.

Dashed goldmarks what is new. Everything else on these cards exists today in a flatter list. The values shown are examples to show the choices, not a client's settings.

How it behaves

  • A section switched off is hidden from that layer's pages, its slides and its missing-items count. What was entered is kept, and switching it back on brings it back.
  • A layer with its first section off opens on the next section that is on.
  • The plan section cannot be switched off. What it holds follows how that unit or function plans (pillars, projects, or objectives and actions); you name those parts, you do not mix them.
  • Every name has a "one" and a "many" form, because the platform says "1 pillar" and "3 pillars".
  • Names are per layer, so a unit's "Pillars" and a function's "Themes of work" can differ. Menus, slides and workbooks read the name of the layer they are showing.
  • Capabilities stay their own item and are not part of these three sections.
