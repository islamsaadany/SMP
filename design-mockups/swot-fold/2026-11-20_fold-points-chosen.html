SWOT points that fold, as you chose

This is the SWOT with your three choices built in: the page opens as you left it, there is one Expand all / Collapse all for the page, and a line is written in one box, a row per point. Press a bold title to fold it; press Edit to write, add and remove. The lines are examples; what you type stays in this browser only.

What to try: fold a few lines, then press "Leave the page and come back", or reload this page: your folds are still there. Press Edit and every line opens; press Done and your folds return. In Edit, the first row of a box is the title and each row under it is a point; add points to a plain line and it gains an arrow, delete them and the arrow goes.

How it will behave in the platform: folds are remembered in each person's own browser, per unit, and change nothing in the plan. The deck, the printed strategy, the workbooks and the PowerPoint always show every point. A function's S&W and the company's Analysis fold the same way.
