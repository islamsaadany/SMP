SWOT points that fold under their title

A SWOT line already keeps a bold title with its points under it. This adds one thing: press the title and its points fold away or open again, and one button folds or opens every line at once. The first SWOT below works, so try it. The words in it are examples, not Mobile's own.

What changes on the page: a small arrow after each bold title, and while a line is folded, how many points it holds. A line with no points under it (Weaknesses 2, Threats 2) has nothing to fold, so it gets no arrow and looks exactly as it does today.

What stays as it is

Paper and slides always show every point. The review deck, the printed strategy, the reading window's print, the workbooks and the PowerPoint cannot be pressed, so a line folded on your screen is still whole there.

While Edit is on, every line is open. You cannot edit a point you cannot see.
