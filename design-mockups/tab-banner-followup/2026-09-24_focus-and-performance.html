Reporting's colour: three options

These are shots of the real Mobile page. All three options also take the box off every tab and leave Performance plain grey like Insights. The only difference between them is how Reporting looks. Pick A, B or C.

A · A brighter orange word

The word Reporting in the clearer orange, with no background. It is the lightest touch: the row still reads as words, and Reporting is simply the coloured one.

Readability: 4.93:1 light, 5.94:1 dark. The minimum is 4.5:1.

B · An orange word in a light pill

The word sits in a soft orange pill, so it stands out from across the room even when you are on another tab. When Reporting is selected, the pill gets an orange edge.

Readability: about 5.4:1 light, about 6.1:1 dark. The minimum is 4.5:1.

C · The solid orange button (as before)

Reporting goes back to a filled orange button with white text, as it was before this round. It is the loudest of the three and the easiest to spot.

Readability: 5.54:1 light, 7.37:1 dark. The minimum is 4.5:1.
