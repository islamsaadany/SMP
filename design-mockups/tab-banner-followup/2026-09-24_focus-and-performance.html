Tab row: the box and Performance's colour

Shots of the real Mobile page. The two changes are shown together, before and after, in both light and dark.

What changes

1. No box on a tab. The gold frame is removed from the main tabs for mouse and keyboard alike. The underline is the only mark on a selected tab. A keyboard user sees a tab with focus go dark with a light underline, and nothing draws a box.

2. Performance gets a colour of its own. It becomes the platform's navy, while Reporting stays orange and Insights stays grey. When Performance is the selected tab, its underline is navy too.
