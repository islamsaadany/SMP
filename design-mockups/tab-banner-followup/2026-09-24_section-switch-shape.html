The section switch, squared: three options

These are shots of the real Mobile page, with Plan open. Only the shape of the Foundation · SWOT · Drivers · Plan switch changes. Its colours, words and sizes stay as they are. Pick A, B or C.

Today · the rounded switch

What is built now, shown for comparison.

A · Softly squared

The same switch with small rounded corners. The outer box matches the Reporting button's corners (6px), and the selected segment sits inside it with a little space around it. This is the closest to today, with the pill shape gone.

B · Sharp corners

The same switch with nearly square corners, 2px on the box and 1px on the segments. It is the most formal and matches no other control on the page.

C · One joined bar

One box divided into cells by thin lines, with the selected cell filling its whole cell edge to edge and no space around it. It reads like a row of joined buttons. The outer corners match Reporting's, at 6px.
