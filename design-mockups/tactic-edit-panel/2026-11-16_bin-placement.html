Where the bin goes

The same list with the second tactic open; the bin, which removes a tactic, is somewhere different in each. The gold outline marks it. One bin per tactic in the table (A, B) or one bin for the open tactic (C, D).

A · In the tactic column, beside the name box

A small bin at the right end of the name box, on every row. Always to hand; it costs the name box a little width and puts a destructive control next to the thing most typed in.
