The bars stay put. The top bar, the Strategy tabs and the Plan line with Edit / Done editing are pinned; everything else scrolls under them.
2
The directions rail scrolls inside its own column, under its own frozen heading. "Directions 15" stays at the top of the rail while the list scrolls; the rail is never taller than the window, so the page never runs on past the direction to make room for it. Scroll inside it to reach D015 and Add a Direction.
3
The direction's banner has a clear line on its top edge and is frozen under the bars while the direction scrolls down: the code, the name, the years, the owner and the custodian stay in view the whole way.
4
The front matter and the key measures run the full width. The panel never narrows them.
5
The tactics list and the panel share one row. The panel's top edge is on the tactics table's own top line, not on the heading above it.
6
The panel stays put beside the list as the page scrolls, and is never taller than the window: a long tactic scrolls inside the panel, with its head and its Done editing always in view.
7
The page ends where the direction ends. No empty area below it: nothing on the page is taller than the direction's own card.
