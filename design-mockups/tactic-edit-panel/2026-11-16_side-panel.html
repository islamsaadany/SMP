What this changes, and what it does not

  • The table in the pen becomes one line per tactic: name, how many outcomes (and how many lack a target), owner, quarters, key measures, a pencil. Nothing is typed in the table itself any more.
  • The panel holds every field a tactic has, in six short sections. It sticks to the right as the page scrolls; ↑ ↓ move to the previous and next tactic; × or Done closes it. One tactic open at a time.
  • Not changed: read mode, the key measures table, reporting, the deck, Excel. The gap count and the Next-gap walk still land on the field, now inside the panel.
  • To decide: whether the panel also serves the key measures rows (same pencil, same panel), or only tactics for now.
