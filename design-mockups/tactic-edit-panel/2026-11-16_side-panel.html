What to try

  • Press Edit: the pen is on, the panel opens on the first tactic (beside the table on a wide window, over the page on a narrow one). Press any row to move the panel there. Change the name: the row follows as you type. Done editing turns the pen off and closes the panel; × or Esc closes the panel while the pen stays on, and a row press opens it again.
  • The owner and the collaborators are picked from the register by typing a few letters, as today: one name for the owner, as many as you like for the collaborators.
  • Add an outcome and give it a target: the Outcomes cell counts it and stops saying one is without a target.
  • Tick a key measure in the panel: the tag appears on the row. Press a quarter: the row's quarters follow.
  • ↑ ↓ move through the tactics. + Add a tactic opens the panel on the new one. × on a row removes it.
