What to try

  • Press a pencil: the panel opens on that tactic and the row is marked. Change the name: the row follows as you type.
  • Add an outcome and give it a target: the Outcomes cell counts it and stops saying one is without a target.
  • Tick a key measure in the panel: the tag appears on the row. Press a quarter: the row's quarters follow.
  • ↑ ↓ move through the tactics. + Add a tactic opens the panel on the new one. × on a row removes it (the panel closes if it was open on it).
