Outcome on the tactics slide

“presentations doesn’t change when the plan performance is done.” The deck is rebuilt every time it opens, so nothing is stale — the tactics slide is simply reading the old figure box. Three ways to put the outcome on it. Pick one and I’ll build it.

01What the slide shows today

Mobile › MB01, out of the real platform. Four tactics, one for each state the slide has to draw.

  • Row 1 has been reported. Its outcome is Stores opened in tier-2 cities, 4 against 6 — and the slide says — / 50% and —. Performance, on the same tactic, says 4# / 3 # and 133%.
  • Row 3 is owed a figure and reads the same dash, so nothing on the slide says anybody still has to enter it.
  • The heading of this very slide already counts row 1. It reads Delivered 98% — a number that includes the outcome the table under it calls empty.

02What all three options do

Every option reads the same number Performance reads, and none of them changes anything stored:

• The figure is shown in the outcome’s own unit against the target due so far — 4# / 3 # — and scored the way Performance scores it, 133%.

• Where a figure is still owed, the row says Not reported · due at 4 min instead of a dash.

• A tactic with no outcome is untouched. Row 2 still reads 62% / 100% and 62%, exactly as it does today.

• Two headings take the words the Performance page already uses: Deliv. / due → YTD actual and Of plan → Progress. One product should not have two names for one number.

03Three ways to draw it

Each picture is the same slide, wearing the deck’s own design. The slide counts are measured on Mobile’s whole deck with 12 of its 22 tactics carrying an outcome; today that deck is 24 slides.

04What is not in this mockup

The reporting count and Submit. The same missing box means a reported outcome is counted as unreported: Mobile reads 40 of 41 where it should read 41 of 41, and Submit refuses with “1 figure still to enter” on a finished report. Nothing about that is visual, so it needs no drawing — it goes in with whichever option you pick.

The plan download (.pptx). It has no outcome column either. That is a different deck with a different job — it carries the plan, not the figures — so it is its own decision and would want its own mockup.

A deck already open on a projector still does not redraw while somebody saves in another tab. I’d leave it that way: slides changing under a presenter mid-sentence is worse than a deck that is right as of when it opened.

What I need from you

  • Which of A, B or C — I’d take A, because a projector should not say something different from the page behind it, and the outcome is what the figure means.
  • The two renamed headings — YTD actual and Progress, matching Performance. Say if you’d rather the deck kept its own words.
