Before I build

Three things are yours

  1. Case B — one door or two? I’d say show it to everybody. A person who owns three lines in their own unit still benefits from seeing them on one screen, and hiding the page from people inside the unit means the rail changes shape depending on where somebody sits, which is harder to explain than a shortcut.
  2. The page is no longer only about figures. A tactic is a line of work, not a number, so Figures I report stops being true. I’d say What I report — it covers both and stays short. Say the word if you want something else; the stored key does not move either way.
  3. Owner only means the collaborators lose what §227 gave them. A milestone’s collaborators can report that milestone today, because you ruled being named there a reporting right. Taking it to Owner-only removes that. I’d say do it — one rule everywhere is worth more than the exception — but it is a reversal of your own decision, so I want it said rather than absorbed.
What does not change. They enter and never submit. The unit still writes the note. The unit’s Submit still waits for their figure, so the head can see whose line is outstanding. And the office sets nothing on Roles & access — naming somebody in the Owner box is the grant, because only the office edits a plan.
