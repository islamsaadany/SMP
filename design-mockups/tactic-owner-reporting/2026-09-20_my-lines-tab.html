2 · Access — in plain words

“align with me more not to ruin any access as this should be similar to what the figure sets should appear for the figure owner as a reproting”

The platform already does this once, and it works exactly the way you describe. Think of a number that belongs to Finance but sits on Mobile's plan. Nobody gave Finance a key to Mobile. The number simply has Hossam's name on it, and that is the whole permission — he types it, nobody else does, and Mobile reads it.

All we are doing is letting a tactic work the same way. The plan already has an Owner column. Today that name is decoration — it decides nothing. After this, if a tactic has an owner, that person types its figure. If it has no owner, nothing about it changes.

So the permissions table is not involved at all. This permission does not come from a tick on that table. It comes from being named on the line, which is why nothing on it has to move.

Being a collaborator

The one thing to be careful about, and my suggestion

The day this is switched on, every tactic that already has an owner changes hands at once — 26 of them on Raya. Nobody chose that; it just happens.

So I would put it behind a switch that starts off, the way we did for the Finance numbers, the assistant and the focus board. You turn it on once the owner names on the plan have been checked.

This is the only thing in the round that can move access without anybody deciding it, which is exactly what you were asking me to guard.
