“the sense of saving that we do in the reporting already gives the feel of saving that locks the reporting with ability to open again”

That is Save draft → Draft saved · Reopen, and §309 already built it at owner scale: a project owner presses it, their own rows freeze, the report around them does not close, and Reopen unfreezes. I withdraw the objection — my point was that there is nothing to save, and you were describing a lock rather than a save, which is a real thing this product has.

One decision inside it: it locks per unit. Hossam's Mobile line and his B2B Ecomm line freeze separately, because each unit submits separately and a single button would lock him out of a unit still working.

You are describing the implementation, and reading the code says so. A figure with a named master is already entered by that master and by nobody else — no grant, no role, no attachment: the server's rule is literally the cycle is open and this row is yours (sourceReporting). 4 · The name — I was wrong, measured

“My reporting is better .. let's see what can we change on the roles and access”

Nothing has to change, because “My reporting” is on no screen. I told you it was taken by the access key's label; I have now grepped every source and that label is never drawn — since §37 the matrix shows AREAS (Own business unit — Reporting), and the knowledge base entry for that key already titles itself Reporting. The word a person reads today is the unit's tab, and that says Reporting.

The real cost is the pair on one row, which is worth looking at rather than arguing about:

Reporting is the unit's report. My reporting is my lines inside it. The pair says the difference; the risk is that at a glance they read as one thing.
