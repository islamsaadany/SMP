A tab beside Reporting, for the people who own lines

Corrected from the last drawing. You were right twice: this is not a room, and it does not belong in Setup. It is a slice of Reporting — same Strategy module, one tab along — and it is only ever drawn for somebody who owns a tactic and is not the unit’s owner or custodian.

I checked the two mechanics it rests on before drawing it. A tab is filtered by grantAt(d.ac, target) and its own when(), so a tab that appears only for people named on a line is the pattern the product already uses. And which units are in the navigation comes from one function, so ownership can open a unit the same way a grant does — which is what makes the second case below possible at all.

The name

Three candidates, drawn on the row

“My reporting” is already taken — it is the label on the access key behind the Reporting tab, the words the office reads on Roles & access. Two things called My reporting one tab apart is the collision worth avoiding.

My lines The product’s own word — Roles & access already says a bounded role “reaches only its own lines”. Covers a tactic today and a measure later without being renamed.
My tactics The plainest today, and wrong the day somebody owns a key measure — which the same rule would already let them report.
My progress Reads as a score rather than a place to type, and the platform already uses Progress as a column heading for a number.
Before I build

Two are yours, one is mine to get right

  1. The name. I’d say My lines, for the reason under it — the product already uses that word for exactly this person.
  2. Case 1 — does he keep the Reporting tab as well? He can type his figures in both places, which is one figure with two doors. I’d say keep both: the Reporting tab is the unit’s whole report and he may legitimately want to read it, and taking it away would make his navigation differ from his colleagues’ for a reason nothing on the screen explains.
  3. Mine, not yours, but worth naming. Opening the unit for a cross-unit owner touches the one function that answers “which units are in my navigation”, and a second place guards a link into a unit. Both have to give the same answer or the tab is reachable from the navigation and not from a link. I will make it one answer, and the check will assert that somebody who owns nothing there still reaches nothing.
What does not change. They enter and never submit. The unit still writes the note and still sends the report, and its Submit still waits for their figure. Nothing is set on Roles & access — being named in the Owner box is the grant, because only the office edits a plan.
