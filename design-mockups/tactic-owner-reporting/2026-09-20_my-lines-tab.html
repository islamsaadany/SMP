My lines — a tab beside Reporting

Drawn again after your two rulings. Below: what measuring changed in the drawing, three things I want to push back on, both cases in the platform's own pixels, and the name.

The last drawing was wrong about Case 2 and it was wrong in your favour. It showed Hossam's navigation carrying Mobile and Retail Stores. Measured: every bounded role ships a_unit_other: "none", and a unit only enters the row when that is not "none" — so a foreign unit never appears today, and your ruling needs no change to the navigation at all. What it needs is for the tab to sit on the person's own place.

What is actually being asked for

One tab, on the place the person already belongs to. Ramy belongs to Mobile, so it sits beside Mobile's Reporting. Hossam belongs to Finance, so it sits beside Finance's Reporting — and the two units he owns lines in are rows on that page, never destinations in the bar. Somebody who sits at the group gets it on the group.

One rule covers both of your cases, and it is the only rule that can keep “beside Reporting” literally true while no foreign unit ever enters the navigation.

Three things I want to push back on

1 · “on saving it submits his lines in the reporting tab” — there is nothing to save

Every box in this product writes when the cursor leaves it and the page autosaves (§35); §273.4 deleted a Save and Cancel pair for exactly this reason. His figure and the unit's Reporting row are the same stored row — type it on My lines and it is on Reporting before the custodian refreshes. So a Save button would be a second state that can disagree with the first.

If what you mean is a deliberate I am finished, that control already exists: §301's Mark done, which freezes the owner's own rows and shows the custodian a state. My recommendation: no Save; keep Mark done.

2 · Case 1's read-only Reporting reverses §301.5, and its scope needs saying

§301.5 exists precisely to fix the fault you are now asking for: a pillar owner could enter figures on one page and not on the page named after the act. Reversing it is fine — it becomes one door instead of two, which is cleaner — but it must be recorded as a reversal and it must say who it reaches.

The platform has three bounded roles: tactic/pillar owner, project owner, contributor. You named tactic owners. My recommendation: all bounded roles, or the Reporting page is read-only for one kind of owner and writable for another on the same table, which nobody can explain.

3 · A filter by unit is a control with one option for most owners

Ramy owns lines in one place. A dropdown offering one unit is a door behind a door (§32) and a control with nothing behind it (§61).

My recommendation: group by unit always — the band is the tag — and draw the filter only where there is more than one unit. That is what is drawn below: Ramy has a band and no filter, Hossam has two bands and a filter.

The name

“My reporting” is not available — it is already the label on the u_report access key, so the matrix and the tab would say one thing twice (§87). Three candidates, drawn on the row:

My lines — the product's own word. §147's rule is literally own lines only, and the page holds tactics, and one day projects and measures. Recommended.

My tactics — exact today and wrong the day a project owner joins the page, which is challenge 2 above. It would have to be renamed then.

My progress — reads as a score about him rather than a list of work, and Progress is already a column on these very tables (§87 again).

Ruled out. Taken by the access key's own label, which the SMO reads on Roles & access.

What this settles, if you say yes

  1. A tab called My lines, on the person's own place, beside Reporting — drawn only for somebody who owns at least one line anywhere, so nobody else ever sees it.
  2. An owner is the Owner column. Being a collaborator or a stakeholder gives no figure. This reverses §227, which made being named on a milestone a reporting right — recorded as a reversal, and it is your “contributor should not report, the owner only”.
  3. No grant, no attachment, no role. Being named is the whole test, exactly as a named figure master already works (§16.7's canEnterFigure). An open, unlocked cycle is the only other condition.
  4. He enters and never submits. The unit's head or custodian submits; the note stays theirs.
  5. The unit's Reporting tab is read-only for him — one door, not two. Reverses §301.5, and I recommend it covers all three bounded roles rather than tactic owners alone.
  6. No Save. The figure lands on the unit's Reporting row as it is typed; Mark done is the deliberate finish.
  7. Grouped by unit always; filtered only when there is more than one.
  8. The welcome screen gains a row — measured: somebody holding unentered figures is told “Nothing is waiting on you” today.
Not in this round, said rather than assumed: the eleven owner values in the worked example that are a single first name (Karim, Hossam, Abuelenien…) resolve to nobody, so those tactics have no owner the platform can see. They were uploaded before the Owner column became a register picker. Correcting them is a data pass on the demo, not code.
