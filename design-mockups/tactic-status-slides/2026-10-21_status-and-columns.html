The status words

The status says what is actually happening. When a tactic is late, its status turns red and says Delayed under it.

StatusWhen
CompletedIt is done.
In progressSome progress has been made.
Not startedNothing done yet.
Not dueIts quarters have not started.
+ DelayedAdded under In progress or Not started when its whole time is over and it is not finished, or when it is still at 0% after its first quarter. A tactic nobody has reported yet is marked Delayed only once its whole time is over.
