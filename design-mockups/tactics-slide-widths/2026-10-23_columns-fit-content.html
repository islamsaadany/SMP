Each column as wide as what it holds

Real screenshots of the tactics slide with six tactics. Today the Note keeps a quarter of the slide even when notes are short, so Tactic and Outcome wrap to three or four lines and the last two tactics move to a second slide. Proposed, the figures take only the room they need and Tactic and Outcome get the most.

1. With owners and collaborators named

Outcome goes from 153 to 205 pixels and Tactic from 159 to 209; owner names stay on one line. All six tactics fit on one slide instead of four.

2. With Owner and Collabs switched off

The room the two columns gave back goes to Tactic and Outcome, 300 pixels each, so every tactic name reads on one line and most outcomes do too.

3. With nobody named yet

An empty Owner or Collabs column is only as wide as its heading, so Tactic and Outcome get its room.

How each column is sized

ColumnWidth
#, Quarters, Status, YTD actual, ProgressAs wide as their widest entry, on one line.
OwnerThe longest name on one line.
Tactic and OutcomeThe biggest share of what is left, equal: about a third each.
Collabs. and NoteThe rest, Note a little more than Collabs. Never narrower than their longest word.
A column nobody filledOnly as wide as its heading.

A slide and its continuation keep the same widths, so the columns do not jump between them. The PowerPoint download takes the same widths. Nothing on the slide changes except the widths.
