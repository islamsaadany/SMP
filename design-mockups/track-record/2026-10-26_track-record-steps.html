The client is the page. Each client has its assignments (Planning, Retainer). The write-up and every learning belong to one assignment, so a reader always knows which piece of work it came from. Drawn in the console as it looks today.

Your four points

  1. Current look: every drawing below now uses the real console: the white side menu, My tasks, the navy band.
  2. The Clients / Learnings switch is gone. You were right: learnings live inside the client's assignment. The one thing the switch did was answer questions across clients, like "every hiccup on a Retainer". That is now just the search and the Hiccup / Practice / Lesson filters on the same front (step 3).
  3. One button, "+ Add to Track Record", with two choices: Write up an assignment or Add learnings. There is no "add a client" here, because clients and assignments only come from the registry.
  4. Yes, learnings have a prompt today (it is called "Write up a period"). It stays as the main way to add learnings, now tied to an assignment, and typing one by hand is still there (step 7).

Step 7

Adding learnings at any time

Every consultant

For things that happen during the work, without waiting for the write-up. Pick the client, then its assignment. That gives every learning its context. Then either talk it through with the prompt, which can give several learnings in one go, or type one in. What comes back is checked the same way as a write-up before it is saved.

Track Record › Add learnings

ATake this prompt to your assistant

I want to record what my consulting firm learned on a piece of work, for the next consultant.

  Client:   Raya Trade (Distribution)
  Work:     Strategy follow-up (Retainer, since July 2025)
  Period:   the last month

Interview me, one question at a time. Ask what went better than expected, what cost us time, what I know now that I did not at the start, and what I would warn the next consultant about.

For each one, ask what happened, what we did, what came of it, and what the next person should know.

What changes from today

  • The name becomes Track Record. Each entry is a client's assignment, and "insights" are called learnings.
  • Assignments are added on the Clients page by the super user. Nobody types a client, dates or a team anywhere else.
  • No admin has to ask for a write-up. The task appears on the lead's My tasks when the assignment ends, and any consultant can start one.
  • The prompt is the first thing you see, already filled from Clients, with Open in Claude beside it. Check and publish happen on the same page.
  • Every learning belongs to an assignment, whether it comes from the write-up's prompt, the learnings prompt or is typed in.
  • Gone: "Open a piece of work", "Ask for the write-up", the desk and shelves, the Insights list as a separate view, and "Not yet in a case study". Old insights with no assignment are shown on their client's page under "Not tied to an assignment yet" until someone picks one.
