3. The unit list in the plan

Today

%#Y/NEGPM EGPB EGPK USDM USDSQMdh

Proposed

%#Y/NEGPM EGPB EGP$$K$M$B

SQM, d and h leave the list, so a new row counts with # and its name says what it counts. Rows already using SQM, d, h, K USD or M USD keep their unit; a USD row shows as $.
