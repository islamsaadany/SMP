3 · The autosave fix, in plain words

What happens today: when you change anything — a setting, a name, a figure — the platform waits a short moment (under a second) and then saves by itself, so it isn’t saving on every keystroke.

The bug: if you close the tab or switch away inside that moment, the save never happens. The screen showed your change; the database never received it.

The fix: the instant the tab is closed or hidden, anything waiting to save is sent immediately. Nothing changes on screen — it simply makes sure the last thing you touched is never lost.
