2 · The three sentences under the numbers your redirect

You looked past the chips at the real problem: the descriptions do not agree with the numbers above them. The middle card lists ten weights nobody can use; the execution card says 104% above a line about 50%, 48% and “variance +2”, leaving the reader to divide one by the other to see they agree.

Proposed

The rule behind the rewrite: every card already carries “How this is calculated →”, which opens the full derivation. So the line under the number says what the number is, and the arithmetic stays one click away where it belongs.

The execution card is the one that mattered: “ahead of plan” is what 104% actually means, said in words — so nobody has to work out that 50 ÷ 48 is where the number came from. It reads “behind plan” when it is under 100.

Still in the picture, and still your call: the chip refinement from the original item 2 — PRIMARY as a quiet outline, and the ▲3 delta moved beside the 67% it qualifies. You did not say either way, so it is drawn here and built only if you want it.

3 · Captions read like sentences, not shouting

Section captions set a whole explanatory clause in italic capitals with letter-spacing — three emphases at once, on the hardest form to read at length. The noun keeps its capitals; the explainer becomes ordinary quiet text.

4 · Branding stops showing a colour the product never uses

The colour pickers on Setup › Branding open on #4F46E5 — an indigo that exists nowhere in SMP — so an unbranded tenant sees two indigo swatches while the platform is actually painting navy and gold. The one page that defines colour opens with a small lie about it.

5 · The register stops showing the same name twice

Name and Full Name are two real facts and both should exist (§93.8) — but until someone types a short name they are identical, which today is all 33 rows. The frozen first column appears duplicated beside itself.

Nobody who has already chosen their columns is affected — a saved choice is merged with the defaults, so this only changes what a fresh viewer sees.
