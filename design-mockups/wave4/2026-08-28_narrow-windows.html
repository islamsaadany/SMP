Narrow Windows

On a smaller screen the plan tables were being cut off down the right-hand side. Here is what it looked like, and what it looks like now that it is fixed.

What was wrong, and what changes

Every table in the platform has a minimum width it refuses to go below. That rule is right nearly everywhere — it stops tables squashing into unreadable columns. But when the window gets narrower, the plan pane gets narrower with it, and at a certain point the table stops fitting: it holds its minimum and the last column is cut off by the edge of the pane.

The fix is to let these tables shrink with the pane instead of holding a fixed floor. The columns give up a few pixels each and everything stays on screen and readable. No scrollbar, no extra furniture, nothing new to learn.

Where it happens

Only in a fairly narrow band — between roughly 820 and 960 pixels wide. On a laptop or a desktop you would never see it. On a smaller browser window, or a tablet held a certain way, you would.
