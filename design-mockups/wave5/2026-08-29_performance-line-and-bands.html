One thing I found while wiring this up

There is a second, hard-coded copy of the bands in the chart legend — it reads “On track 70+ · At risk 50–69 · Off track under 50” and already disagrees with the real thresholds. I'll point it at the same single definition, so the button, the legend and the scores can never say three different things.

A cost worth naming before you say yes

Once the banner goes, the colours are only explained behind a button. Anyone who has not pressed it sees a red number with nothing saying what red means. That is the trade for the row of space — I think it is the right one at three bands rather than four, but it is your call.
