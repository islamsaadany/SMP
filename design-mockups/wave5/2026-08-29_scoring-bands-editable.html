The editable table

One thing I need you to decide: the colours

I have shown a choice of five — the product's own green, amber, orange, red and grey — rather than a free colour picker.

The reason is not tidiness. Every one of those five exists twice in the palette: once as a fill and once, slightly darker, for text. A number printed in a band's colour uses the text version, and it has been measured as readable on both the light and dark backgrounds. A free colour has neither — pick a pale yellow and the percentages in that band become unreadable, and nothing on the page would warn you.

If you want free colours anyway, I can do it — but then the score has to keep printing in the ordinary ink, and only the dot and the pill take your colour. Tell me which you prefer.

And a defect on that page

This may well be what you photographed

The heading was told to stay 136 pixels from the top of the page, but the table sits in a box that scrolls — so the browser measured those 136 pixels from the top of the table instead and pushed the heading down inside its own body. At every width, with the page not scrolled at all.

The same mistake was found and fixed on the Roles & access table a while back, and the fix stopped there — every other Setup table still had it, and nobody had looked. It is fixed for all of them now.
