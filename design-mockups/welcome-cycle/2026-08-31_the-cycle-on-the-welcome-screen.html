The cycle on the welcome screen

You asked to see how it changes. The screen already names the cycle — the gold chip in the hero reads H1 2026 cycle is open — so what is being added is the numbers. Drawn on the real screen at 1500px, as the office.

One thing to decide before the placement, because it is not about layout. The figure 222 of 245 counts every unit and every supporting function in the business, and the page that shows it — Reporting cycle — is access‑gated. A unit head does not normally hold that key. So putting the business‑wide numbers on everyone's welcome screen shows the whole company's reporting state to people the platform currently does not show it to. That is why there are two options below rather than two placements.

Nobody is shown anything they could not already open — a unit head's block is their own unit's, which is on their own Performance page. And a deadline with days left is worth more to the person who has to meet it than to the office watching. Cost, and it is real: two different blocks means two things to keep true, and for a unit head it partly repeats what the row directly above it already says.

My recommendation: A, and then B as a second step if you want it. A is the small move — the same block the Overview already has, in the same place, gated on a key that already exists, and no new question about what anybody may see. B is a better screen for the eleven unit and function heads who are not the office, and it is a genuinely new thing to design and keep true, so I would rather it be its own decision than ride along with this one.

Either way the gold chip in the hero goes, because the block says what it said.
