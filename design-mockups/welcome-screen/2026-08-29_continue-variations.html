Mockup · Welcome screen — the Continue control · five variations for sign-off · shown as Mennah Farouk, custodian of Mobile · 2026-08-29

Each frame is a 600px window over the real screen, opened at the foot, where four of the five exits live — scroll a frame up for the greeting. C opens at the top, where its control is.

Why it disappears

  • It is the only way out. There is no ×, no Escape key and no click-outside — every other control on this screen goes somewhere and stays. This one link is the whole exit.
  • It is the quietest thing on the screen. 13px, in the navy the page uses for quiet text, against 15px bold row titles, three bordered buttons and one solid orange one.
  • It sits inside the left column, so it reads as the end of the Waiting on you list rather than as the way past the whole screen. That is the part a heavier weight alone would not fix.
  • And below 960px it is not even last. The columns stack there, so the exit lands in the middle of the screen with Your pages and the intro round beneath it — measured at 900px wide: the screen is 949px tall and 411px of it comes after the way out.

For the record — not part of the screen

  1. The accent budget holds in all five (§41): Report keeps the one solid orange fill while a cycle is open, so no variation gives Continue a second one. The empty case is the exception — with nothing waiting there is no CTA on the screen and Continue becomes the loud control, which is what the approved round already said and what the built screen never did. It is drawn under B.
  2. Nothing here changes what Continue does — it still only steps aside, because the platform under the overlay is already on the page §94.6 chose, and it still names that place.
  3. One way out, not two. A corner skip and a footer bar is a duplicate, not a choice (§94.15) — so these are alternatives, and only one ships.
  4. Escape is a separate small ask. Whichever wins, the overlay could also close on the Escape key — one line, no design. Say the word and it rides along; it is not in any of these frames.
