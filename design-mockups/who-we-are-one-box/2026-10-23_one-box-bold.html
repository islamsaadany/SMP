Who we are becomes one box, and any words can be bold

Real screenshots of the platform with the change drawn in. Anything new is outlined in gold. Nothing here is built yet.

1 · While editing

  • One box for the whole of Who we are. The small left boxes and "+ Add a line" go. Press Enter for a new paragraph.
  • Bold: select words and press B above the box (or Ctrl+B, ⌘B on a Mac). Press it again to take the bold off.
  • What's there now comes in as it is. Each line becomes a paragraph, with its opener ("We are", "Offering"…) already bold. Nothing is lost.
  • Pasting brings in the words only, not colours or fonts from where they were copied.

2 · While reading

The paragraphs read as sentences, with the bold parts bold and a thin line between paragraphs.

3 · Elsewhere

  • Slides: the Who we are slide shows the same paragraphs with the same bold.
  • Excel plan download and upload: unchanged. Bold words at the start of a paragraph still go in the first column, as the openers do today.
