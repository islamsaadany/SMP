Who we are: typed in one box, shown as today

A working copy to try. Type in the box; the table below redraws as you go. Nothing here is saved.

1 · While editing

Description

Select the opening words and press B (or Ctrl+B). Bold always runs from the start of the line. Enter starts a new line.

What you have today comes in as it is: each line with its opener already bold. Pasting brings in the words only. A blank line is not a row.

2 · How it shows (unchanged from today)

Description

What to try

  • Put the cursor anywhere on a line and press B: the bold runs from the start of the line to where you are, and the table moves those words to the left.
  • Press B inside bold words: the bold comes off and the words join the right-hand side.
  • Press Enter at the end: a new line, a new row. Type “Backed by” then the rest, select “Backed by”, press B.
  • Paste a whole statement from somewhere else: each pasted line is a row, with no colours or fonts carried in.
