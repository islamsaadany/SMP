# Sample recipes — for settling the voice

Five of the ~40 in `spec.md` §5.2, chosen because they fail differently: one is
a plain how-to, two end in *"you can't, and here is who can"*, one corrects a
misreading, one explains a number. **No section numbers and no internal
vocabulary** — these are read by somebody who has hit a wall, not by us.

---

### Can I still report once the cycle is locked?

No. Once the Strategy Office locks a cycle, the figures are fixed — that is
what makes a locked cycle a record of what was true at the time rather than
something that keeps moving.

If a number was wrong, tell the office in this chat. They can still correct a
figure inside a locked cycle, and the correction is logged.

---

### There is a mistake in my plan — how do I fix it?

You can't change it yourself, and that is deliberate: a plan you are measured
against is not yours to rewrite. It keeps the target honest, and it means
nobody has to wonder whether a number moved because the work changed or
because the plan did.

The Strategy Office corrects plans — including the aspiration, the SWOT and the
key objectives, not only the measures. Tell them here what is wrong and they
will amend it.

---

### Why does my score show a dash instead of 0?

Because nothing has been reported for it yet, and nothing is not zero.

A dash means *not yet measurable* — there is no actual to compare against the
target. Zero would mean somebody reported a real result of nothing, which is a
completely different thing and would drag the average down as if the work had
failed.

Report an actual and the score appears.

---

### How is my unit's headline number worked out?

It is your unit's **key objectives** — each actual against its own target,
combined.

It is **not** a roll-up of the pillars below it. That is why the two can
disagree without either being wrong: a pillar can be struggling on its own
internal measures while the objectives the unit is judged on are fine. They sit
at different altitudes and answer different questions, so the platform keeps
them apart rather than blending them into one number that answers neither.

---

### Why can't I open another business unit?

Access here is by page, not by field: if you can open a page you see all of it,
so a unit you are not attached to is removed rather than trimmed.

What you can open comes from your role and what it is attached to — the head
and the custodian of a unit see that unit; a company CEO sees the units in
their company; the office sees everything. Nobody types that in unit by unit.

If you need to see something you can't, ask here and say which unit and why —
granting it is the Strategy Office's to do.
